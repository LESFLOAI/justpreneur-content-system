**Headline:** The Paris Café Wasn't the Business. The Postcard Was.

### Slide 2 — Setup
**Header:** rhode went continental.
**Body:** Sept 30, 2026: rhode launched at Sephora across continental Europe — online and in 500+ stores, 19 markets. The brand's first rollout across continental Europe, after 3+ years as DTC-only.

### Slide 3 — The Activation
**Header:** Five days. One café.
**Body:** Per trade press (Cosmetics Business, Mercado Negro, PremiumBeautyNews — not company-confirmed), rhode opened "Café Rhode," a reservation-based pop-up bistro on Rue Bonaparte, Paris 6th arrondissement. Sept 30–Oct 4.

### Slide 4 — The Mechanic
**Header:** The real menu item: a postcard.
**Body:** Guests reportedly left with a branded postcard and "metro card" — redeemable at a participating Paris Sephora (reportedly including Champs-Élysées) for a skincare sample with purchase, while supplies lasted.

### Slide 5 — The Insight
**Header:** The café was the hook. The register was the point.
**Body:** Pretty rooms get people to show up. A redemption mechanic gets them to buy. Café Rhode reportedly built both — not just one.

### Slide 6 — Final Takeaway
**Header:** Design the redemption mechanic before the aesthetic.
**Body:** A pop-up isn't a marketing expense if it's built like a funnel. Most brand activations skip this step and settle for photos.

### Slide 7 — Call to Action
**Header:** Audit your next activation.
**Body:** What's the one trackable action that ties your foot traffic to an actual revenue channel? Save this before you build your next pop-up.

## Final Caption

rhode's Paris pop-up got the headlines for the aesthetic. Per trade press, the real work was happening on a postcard.

Café Rhode — reportedly a reservation-only bistro on Rue Bonaparte, open Sept 30–Oct 4 — handed guests a branded postcard and "metro card" redeemable at a participating Paris Sephora for a skincare sample with purchase, while supplies lasted.

That's not décor. That's a funnel.

A pop-up isn't a marketing expense if it's built like one — with a specific, trackable action tying foot traffic to a revenue channel. Most brand experiences stop at the photo op.

Save this. Before your next activation, write the job description for it: what's the one action that has to happen for it to pay for itself?

*(rhode launched at Sephora across continental Europe Sept 30, 2026 — 500+ stores, 19 markets — following earlier US/Canada and UK Sephora rollouts. Café Rhode details per Cosmetics Business, Mercado Negro, and PremiumBeautyNews — not company-confirmed.)*
