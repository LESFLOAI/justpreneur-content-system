## Final caption

Bogotá's Movistar Arena is losing its naming sponsor after eight years. The contract expires October 31, 2026.

Here's the full picture: Telefónica exited the Colombian telecom market entirely — selling its 67.5% stake in Coltel (Movistar's local operator) to Millicom, Tigo's parent company, in a deal that closed February 6, 2026 for about US$214 million. The Colombian government sold its remaining 32.5% stake to Millicom on April 24, 2026. Telefónica had operated in Colombia since 2005. Millicom is folding Movistar into Tigo, with the brand expected to phase out of the Colombian market by the end of 2026.

The naming-rights deal is a separate contract — but once the sponsor's parent company left the country, non-renewal became close to certain.

Here's what didn't change: Movistar never owned the arena. It's operated by Colombiana de Escenarios under a concession with Bogotá's IDRD dating to 2016 — the sponsor was only ever a name on the building. BeatHub Entertainment, now sole shareholder of CDE after buying out BeLive Entertainment Group's stake in September, says operations are unaffected: more than 50 shows are still booked through the rest of 2026, and more than 80 artists are already booked for 2027. A new naming partner is expected in early 2027.

The business never depended on the sponsor. The sponsor was just a revenue line sitting on top of infrastructure the venue actually controlled.
