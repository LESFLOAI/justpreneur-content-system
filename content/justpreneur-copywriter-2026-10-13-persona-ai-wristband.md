## 3. Final Caption

Everyone's repeating that Zach Yadegari raised $10M for Persona in just 4 months after selling Cal AI. That's not right.

The real gap between MyFitnessPal's March 2026 acquisition of Cal AI and Persona's October 8 funding announcement is about seven months.

The correction matters because it points to the real lesson: that round moved fast because of proof, not pace. Cal AI's reported $30M+ to $40M+ in annual revenue, plus a completed acquisition (sale price undisclosed), gave investors something to verify. That's what opened a warm round led by Vine Ventures, with Cory Levy and Collective Global participating (some reports also name WHOOP and Eight Sleep).

Persona's free AI assistant is already live in beta on iMessage. Its $179 screenless wristband — activated by a button press or gesture, not always-on listening — is open for pre-order now, shipping targeted for December 2026. Valuation wasn't disclosed.

Speed still matters. But it's proof that buys you the fast lane, not hustle alone.

Save this for the next time you're tempted to chase speed before you've built something worth verifying.
