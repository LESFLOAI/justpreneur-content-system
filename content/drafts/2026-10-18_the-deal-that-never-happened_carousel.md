## SLIDE-BY-SLIDE COPY

### Slide 1 — Cover
**THE DEAL THAT NEVER HAPPENED STILL MOVED BILLIONS**
Sub: No offer. No deal. Still moved two stocks.

### Slide 2 — The bio
Brian Niccol ran Chipotle as chairman and CEO from March 2018 to August 31, 2024 — about six years.

He became Starbucks CEO on August 13, 2024, starting September 9, 2024.

### Slide 3 — The rumor breaks
October 8, 2026: The Financial Times reported — citing people familiar with the matter — that Starbucks had worked with outside advisers on a possible takeover of Chipotle.

FT's own caveat: unclear if a formal offer was ever made, or if any deal would proceed.

### Slide 4 — The stock reaction
Reported in the days after (figures vary by source and timing):
— Chipotle shares: up roughly 6–7%
— Starbucks shares: down roughly 3%
(Some intraday reports showed larger swings.)

### Slide 5 — The denial
October 12, 2026 — Starbucks spokesperson:
"We don't comment on rumors and speculation, but we want to be clear we are not buying Chipotle."

Starbucks said it remains focused on its "Back to Starbucks" turnaround plan. Chipotle did not comment.

### Slide 6 — The partial reversal
After the denial, the moves partially reversed (reported):
— Chipotle: down roughly 3–4%
— Starbucks: up roughly 1%

No formal offer surfaced. No evidence either board ever considered a deal.

### Slide 7 — The lesson
No deal happened. The market still moved — on a name and a biography.

One analyst reportedly put completion odds near 20%, before the denial. That was the entire basis.

Your track record is a priced asset. You either manage the story, or the market — and the rumor mill — writes it for you.

## FINAL CAPTION

Starbucks never bought Chipotle. There was no formal offer. No deal. But for four trading days, two of the biggest names in American fast-casual moved billions in value on a rumor — because of one man's resume.

Starbucks denied it outright on October 12. The moves partially reversed. The deal — real or not — is gone. The lesson isn't.
