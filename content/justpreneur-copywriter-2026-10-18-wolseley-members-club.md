## 5. Final Caption

On October 7, 2026, Wolseley revealed what the hotel brand was really building toward: a Members Club. Not a loyalty add-on — the architecture of a business that earns before a guest ever checks in.

76 rooms. One hotel, currently targeting an early 2027 opening. The Members Club is targeting Q2 2027. Minor Hotels hasn't disclosed membership pricing — and under CEO Dillip Rajakarier, the sequencing is the strategy: build the membership engine, then build the room around it.

For founders, the takeaway isn't about hospitality. It's about what you build first — the recurring-revenue core, or the one-off product wrapped around it.

Both opening dates are targets, not confirmed. Pricing remains undisclosed.

**Call to action:** Save this if you're building a membership or subscription layer into your own business — and tell us in the comments: would you build the "room" or the "membership" first?
