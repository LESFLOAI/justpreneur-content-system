## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block — Fact Verifier, status: Cleared with qualifications)

Live Nation Entertainment (NYSE: LYV) is providing capital and strategic backing for a new venture called Creator Nation, announced October 1–2, 2026, with Hollywood Reporter breaking the story as an exclusive. Creator Nation is co-founded by Alexis Fisher, founder of Timeline Management (a majority stake in which Live Nation acquired in December 2024), and Scott Fisher, founder of Select Management Group (fully acquired by Propagate in 2025, after Propagate's Artists First division first took a minority stake in 2020). The pair are widely reported to be a married couple with roughly 15 years building creator-economy businesses together.

Creator Nation is positioned to support creators across content, commerce, brand partnerships, and live experiences, and is actively hiring across six categories: creator strategy, commerce, touring, data, operations, and marketing. The company has stated plans to acquire and scale existing creator-management firms and adjacent businesses, with its first acquisitions "expected in the coming months" — no acquisition targets have been named, and no deals have closed as of this writing.

The dollar amount of Live Nation's capital commitment to Creator Nation has not been disclosed. Live Nation's exact equity or ownership stake in Creator Nation has also not been disclosed and is a distinct, separate matter from Live Nation's already-closed, majority-stake acquisition of Timeline Management in 2024 — the two are never conflated or combined into a single figure in this package.
