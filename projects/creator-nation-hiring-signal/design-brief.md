## 1. Overall Creative Concept

The carousel is built around one recurring device: **a six-point signal map** — a flat, hexagonal network graphic that visually says "map," not "mood board." Each point on the hexagon represents one of Creator Nation's six hiring categories (Creator Strategy, Commerce, Touring, Data, Operations, Marketing). The cover shows the full map dim and unresolved, with a fading dollar-sign/funding glyph dissolving above it — a visual of "the capital made headlines, then faded; the map underneath is what's real." Each of the six body slides lights up exactly one more node of the hexagon in energy gold, in the same clockwise order as the approved copy (Strategy → Commerce → Touring → Data → Operations → Marketing), so a reader flipping through the carousel watches the map complete itself one category at a time. The takeaway/CTA slide shows the full hexagon fully lit and connected — the completed signal. This is an explainer-graphic aesthetic (think a Bloomberg/Economist "six functions of X" infographic), not a motivational quote template, and it directly visualizes the headline's core idea: the hiring categories are the real map.

## 2. Composition & Focal Hierarchy (carousel-wide)

1. JustPreneur wordmark — smallest, top-center, identical position/size every slide (brand anchor, never competes with headline).
2. Headline / category name — largest, highest-contrast element per slide.
3. Body copy — secondary, generous line spacing, centered or left-aligned within a fixed-width text column.
4. Six-point gold signal-map motif — connective tissue, positioned consistently (lower-middle to lower-third of canvas) so the reader's eye tracks its progressive completion slide to slide.
5. Background field — deep navy, flat or very subtly gradiented, never busy enough to compete with text.

## 4. Typography Direction

- **Headline face:** Bold condensed/display grotesk (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk Display Bold, Söhne Kräftig, or Inter Display Bold as a substitute) — sentence case, not all-caps, to read as premium business journalism rather than a motivational template.
- **Category name headlines (Slides 2–7):** Same display face as the cover headline, single word or short phrase, large enough to anchor the slide on its own even before the body copy is read.
- **Body copy:** Clean grotesk regular/medium (e.g., Inter, Söhne Buch), generous line height (~135–150%), centered column with fixed, comfortable side margins.
- **Node labels:** None rendered directly on the map graphic itself — the six category names live only as slide headlines, never as tiny labels crowding the hexagon nodes. This keeps the map clean and avoids small, hard-to-read text.
- **Never** render large blocks of approved copy inside the generated image itself — every word of headline/body copy and the JustPreneur wordmark is added at the layout stage over the abstract background (see Section 11).

## 5. Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base background | Deep navy | `#0E1B2A` | Full-bleed background on every slide |
| Dim/unlit map nodes & lines | Steel gray | `#3C4A59` | Unlit hexagon nodes and perimeter lines, dissolving funding glyph on Slide 1 |
| Primary text | Warm white | `#F4F1E9` | Headlines, body copy |
| Accent / lit nodes / emphasis words | Energy gold | `#C79A4B` | Lit hexagon nodes and connecting lines, "Audit your skill set against these six." |

Adjust only if brand guidelines change; this story does not call for a departure from the default palette.

## 6. Image Treatment

Every generated background is a flat, editorial, vector-leaning abstract composition — no photography, no photorealistic rendering, no stock "creator filming content" imagery, no texture-heavy photo-illustration. Treat each as a print-magazine explainer-graphic backdrop, not a decorative poster. The hexagon map is drawn with crisp, thin, consistent-weight lines and clean circular nodes; the only "texture" allowed is an extremely subtle fine-grain noise on the navy base to avoid flat-digital banding at export size. No gradients busy enough to fight with overlaid text.

## 7. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait, 4:5).
- Keep all text and the signal-map motif inside a safe area of **96px minimum on left/right** and **150px minimum on top/bottom** (top clearance avoids the profile-grid crop and app UI overlap; bottom clearance avoids the caption/like-bar overlap in feed view).
- JustPreneur wordmark sits at a fixed vertical position ~110–130px from the top edge, inside the top safe margin — never flush to the very top edge.
- The hexagon map should sit fully within the lower-middle two-thirds of the canvas on Slides 2–8 (and the lower half on Slide 1), never touching or crossing the safe-margin boundary.

## 8. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

1. JustPreneur wordmark: identical size, weight, color, and vertical position on all 8 slides.
2. Deep navy `#0E1B2A` base on all 8 slides — no slide switches to a different base color.
3. The six-point hexagonal signal map appears on every slide in the same size, position, and orientation (nodes at 12/2/4/6/8/10 o'clock) — only the count of lit (gold) vs. dim (steel-gray) nodes changes, and it only ever increases as the carousel progresses (Slide 1 = zero lit, Slide 7 and Slide 8 = all six lit).
4. Node lighting order is fixed and must match the copy order exactly: Creator Strategy (12 o'clock) → Commerce (2 o'clock) → Touring (4 o'clock) → Data (6 o'clock) → Operations (8 o'clock) → Marketing (10 o'clock).
5. No node labels, icons, or small text are ever rendered inside the generated map graphic itself — category names exist only as the large slide headline, added at layout stage.
6. No slide numbers, dates, citations, watermarks, or corner labels on any slide, per standing brand instruction.
7. Headline type size may vary by copy length but must never drop below a size that renders comfortably at typical in-feed thumbnail scale (roughly 60–72pt effective at full 1080 width for single-line headline words).

## 9. Accessibility & Contrast Check

- Warm white `#F4F1E9` on deep navy `#0E1B2A`: contrast ratio ≈ 14.7:1 — passes WCAG AAA for all text sizes.
- Energy gold `#C79A4B` on deep navy `#0E1B2A`: contrast ratio ≈ 6.4:1 — passes AA but not AAA at small sizes. **Rule: gold text is reserved for large display words only (bold, ~28pt-equivalent or larger)** — "Audit your skill set against these six." All other gold usage is restricted to the hexagon map's lit nodes/lines (a graphic element, not a legibility-critical text color).
- Steel gray `#3C4A59` is used only for dim/unlit map elements and the dissolving funding glyph on Slide 1 — never for body text, so no low-contrast text pairing exists anywhere in the set.
- The lit-vs-dim distinction on the map is never the sole carrier of meaning for a reader with color-vision deficiencies: it is reinforced by fill (solid gold node vs. hollow gray outline), not color alone.
