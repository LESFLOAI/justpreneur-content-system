## 1. Overall creative concept

This is a platform-vs-performer business explainer dressed as a premium culture/business-press breakdown — think Bloomberg, Puck, or The Ringer covering Sphere's economics, not a concert-recap or fan-hype post. The hook is counterintuitive ("Metallica didn't launch this — they're the 11th act to plug into it"), so the visuals must consistently de-emphasize any single performer and emphasize the *system*: the giant curved screen, the sound architecture, the seating technology, the repeatability.

No real person is rendered at any point. Lars Ulrich and Robert Trujillo's quotes (Slide 5) are carried entirely by the approved text and by anonymous, non-identifiable silhouette figures — never a likeness. The venue itself is treated the same way: evoked through generic spherical-architecture-at-night and wraparound-screen imagery, never a literal reproduction of the real building's signage, logo, or exact exterior artwork.

A single recurring graphic device ties all seven slides together: a thin gold arc along the bottom edge of every frame — a quiet nod to the Sphere's own curved geometry — carrying a row of small circular light nodes. The arc's state tracks the argument:
- Slide 1 (cover): arc present but dim/unlit — the story hasn't been told yet.
- Slide 2 (the list of acts): eleven nodes, ten dim steel-gray, the eleventh (rightmost) lit gold — a direct, wordless visualization of "11th resident act."
- Slide 3 (the system): all nodes lit evenly in a cool blue-white "screen-glow" tone — the system treats every node the same; no node is special.
- Slide 4 (the residency/demand): nodes pulse brighter, slightly overloaded-looking, the eleventh node brightest — demand surge.
- Slide 5 (credibility): nodes calm again, the eleventh node glows warm gold while the rest stay steady — this specific act's achievement, inside the same system.
- Slide 6 (takeaway): all nodes lit evenly in steady gold — resolved; the format itself is the asset.
- Slide 7 (CTA): a single node pulses gently — an open question for the viewer.

This motif does the storytelling work that icons, charts, or labels would otherwise do, without adding any unapproved text to the frame.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

Every slide follows the same top-to-bottom hierarchy:
1. Headline, label, or lead copy — upper portion of frame.
2. Supporting body copy (where present) — middle band, left-aligned, generous line-height.
3. Photographic/illustrative environment — lower half to two-thirds of frame, always de-emphasizing any single human figure.
4. Thin gold arc + light nodes — fixed bottom-edge band, state advancing per slide as described in Section 1.
5. JustPreneur wordmark — small, fixed bottom-center position, identical placement across all seven slides, sitting just above the arc.

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface (Slides 1, 6): a bold, high-contrast editorial serif with a business/culture-press feel (Tiempos Headline / Canela / Freight Display style) — premium journalism, not a motivational template.
- Short label headlines (Slide 6's "The Takeaway"): a heavier-weight grotesk or the same serif at a condensed/bold cut, set noticeably smaller than full headlines so it reads as a section marker.
- Body copy typeface (Slides 2, 3, 4, 5, 7): a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne / Inter / Neue Haas style) for maximum mobile legibility.
- Pull-quote treatment (Slide 5): editorial serif-italic for the quoted words, small-caps gold for attributed names, visually distinct from the regular sans body copy around it.
- No kicker labels, no slide numbers, no date tags, no citation chips added as separate small text anywhere — every fact, date, and qualifier (including Slide 4's reported-not-confirmed parenthetical) appears only inside the approved copy sentences themselves, rendered at normal body-copy size.
- Headline sizing: no line should exceed ~34–38 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on the 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, line-height ~130–145% (Slide 2's list and Slide 4's longer paragraph get the higher end of that range for scanability), left-aligned except Slide 7's centered CTA treatment.
- Slide 2's lead sentence must be visually larger/bolder than its ten-name list continuation so the list reads as supporting detail, not a second headline.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A range) — dominant background/shadow tone, identical across all seven slides.
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces (seating, stage floor, dim light columns on Slide 2).
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 / #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds.
- Energy gold (#C9A24B–#D4AF37) — the arc line, lit nodes, "Format" accent word (Slide 1), "The Takeaway" label, pull-quote attribution names, wordmark.
- Cool blue-white "screen-glow" (#9FD8E8–#BFE6F2, used sparingly) — reserved exclusively for the giant LED-screen imagery on Slide 3 and the evenly-lit node state on that slide, signaling "the system's own light" as visually distinct from the brand's gold accent. Do not let this cool tone bleed into text color or the wordmark.
- No red, no additional accent colors beyond the cool screen-glow allowance confined to Slide 3's venue-screen imagery — keep the palette disciplined so the carousel reads as one continuous brand set.

## 6. Image treatment

- All backgrounds graded toward navy/gray/gold so the venue's screen-glow and stage lighting feel like controlled highlights within the brand palette rather than a different color story.
- Apply a subtle dark navy gradient overlay (~40–55% opacity) behind every text zone on every slide to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying image, with a heavier overlay (~55–60%) on Slide 4's dense tile field and Slide 3's bright screen imagery, since these are the busiest/brightest backgrounds in the set.
- No text, logos, signage, trademarked exterior artwork, or real identifiable faces generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief. The real venue and real people (Metallica, Lars Ulrich, Robert Trujillo, the other ten listed acts) are referenced only through the approved copy text, never through a rendered likeness or a literal architectural reproduction.
- Slide 5's distant performer silhouettes must be small and non-identifiable by composition (far wide shot, low detail) rather than by blurring alone, so they read as "scale of the achievement" without resembling any real person.
- Slides that depict the venue (1, 3, 6) should share a consistent sense of scale and curvature so the "sphere" idea reads as one continuous architectural motif across the carousel, even though no single real building is being copied.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of at least 96px from every edge. Instagram's feed UI can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px of the preview — keep headline/body anchor points starting no higher than ~150–170px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge (the gold arc itself can sit lower, in the ~1290–1320px band, since it is a thin graphic device rather than text).
- Keep body copy line length under ~860–880px wide (leaving ≥100px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading, with extra care on Slides 3, 4, and 5, where body copy runs longest.
- Slide 4's long paragraph (including the closing parenthetical) must stay within the 150–760px vertical band with no line touching the bottom 250px crop-risk zone; reduce type size slightly before shrinking margins if the paragraph runs long.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080 x 1350) on all seven slides.
- Identical thin gold arc position along the bottom edge on every slide; node states advance in this exact order: unlit (1) → eleven nodes, tenth dim/eleventh lit gold (2) → all nodes lit cool blue-white evenly (3) → nodes pulsing brighter, eleventh brightest (4) → calm, eleventh warm gold, rest steady (5) → all nodes lit evenly steady gold (6) → single node pulsing gently (7).
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center, just above the arc) on every slide, with slightly increased prominence only on the thesis slide (6) and the CTA slide (7).
- Identical color grade (navy/gray/gold) across all seven backgrounds except the deliberate cool blue-white screen-glow confined to Slide 3 only.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention on Slides 2, 3, 4, 5; Slide 7 uses the same sans family but centered; Slides 1 and 6 share the headline/serif treatment.
- No slide may include a rendered likeness of Metallica, Lars Ulrich, Robert Trujillo, any of the ten other listed acts, or any other real named person, and no slide may reproduce the real venue's actual exterior signage or trademarked artwork.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) text over the navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, especially over Slide 3's bright screen imagery and Slide 4's dense glowing tile field.
- Gold (#C9A24B) is reserved for large display text (the "Format" accent word, "The Takeaway" label, pull-quote attribution names, the arc/nodes, the wordmark) on dark backgrounds only — do not set gold body copy at small sizes, where contrast drops.
- The cool blue-white screen-glow tone is for imagery only and must never be used as a text color, to avoid any accidental low-contrast pairing against warm white.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas.
- Slides 3, 4, and 5 carry the longest paragraphs in the set — prioritize a slightly heavier gradient overlay behind text on these three slides.
