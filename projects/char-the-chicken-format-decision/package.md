## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, cleared with qualifications by Fact Verifier)

Matt Broussard (@acooknamedmatt), a Seattle-based food content creator with more than 7 million followers across social media, is partnering with Conscious Hospitality Group to open his first brick-and-mortar restaurant, Char the Chicken, on Capitol Hill in Seattle. Broussard previously worked for about six years as a chef at Tom Douglas restaurants before being laid off in 2020, after which he built a full-time content-creation career. Char the Chicken will serve fire-finished rotisserie chicken — cooked on a rotisserie and finished over a fire grill with chicken fat and a house spice blend — sold whole, half, or quarter, primarily as grab-and-go counter service rather than full-service dining. The restaurant is taking over the former Mt. Joy space at 1530 11th Ave, next door to Matcha Magic, and is targeting a mid-October 2026 opening, per Conscious Hospitality Group co-founder Norman Wu.

**Note on sourcing limits:** The specific financial/ownership structure of the Broussard–Conscious Hospitality Group partnership and a platform-by-platform follower breakdown have not been independently confirmed and are deliberately not stated as fact anywhere in this package.
