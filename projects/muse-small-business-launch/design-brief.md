## 1. Overall Creative Concept

Treat this as a premium business-strategy editorial story — the visual register of Fast Company, The Information, or a Bloomberg Businessweek explainer, not a founder-motivation template and not a literal "AI chat app" mockup.

The through-line motif is a **desire path** — the urban-planning concept the headline is literally built on: a worn, informal trail that pedestrians carve across a lawn before city planners notice it and pave it into the official sidewalk. That is exactly the shape of this story (users improvise a use case → the company notices → the company builds the formal feature), so it is rendered directly rather than invented as a separate metaphor.

Across the 8 slides, a single continuous graphic evolves like a storyboard:
1. An empty architectural blueprint grid with one rough, hand-drawn gold path already cut across it (the cover states the thesis before it's explained)
2. The grid, clean and undisturbed — no path yet (the tool as originally built, no adoption)
3. A sketchy, informal gold path begins appearing across the grid, with small unlabeled markers along it (organic small-business adoption)
4. The same path, now crossing a small checkpoint/gate glyph (the owner-approval gate — explicitly *not* full autonomy)
5. The same path shown pulled back slightly, with thin survey/viewfinder corner marks framing it (Meta observing the pattern)
6. The sketchy path redrawn as a clean, straight, paved gold line, with a row of small neutral node dots along it (the formalized connectors — never named or logo'd on-image)
7. The motif drops to a faint, barely-visible ghost — a quiet, text-dominant reflection slide
8. The paved path returns as a low-opacity closing echo behind an oversized quotation mark, anchoring the final pull quote

No human faces, no literal chat-app UI mockups, no fabricated Meta/Muse product screens, and no rendering of Notion/Shopify/Stripe/Slack/QuickBooks logos or wordmarks anywhere — the approved facts block requires the connector list be treated as illustrative examples, not an exhaustive or brand-verified set, so visualizing it as abstract, unlabeled nodes avoids both trademark risk and overclaiming completeness. The approval-gate glyph on Slide 4 is a deliberate visual guardrail matching the fact-check constraint against implying autonomous execution — it must read as a calm checkpoint, not an alarm.

## 2. Composition and Focal Hierarchy (applies to all slides unless noted)

Standard portrait canvas: **1080 x 1350 px**.

Focal hierarchy, top to bottom:
1. JustPreneur wordmark — small, consistent, top-left corner
2. Headline / slide copy — primary focal block, upper-to-mid frame, large and left-aligned for mobile scanability
3. Blueprint-grid + desire-path motif graphic — mid-to-lower frame, supporting visual, never competing with text for attention
4. Generous negative space in the bottom third to keep the design breathable and premium (avoid cramming)

Slide 1 carries the full headline plus subheadline (largest type moment in the carousel). Slides 2–6 carry body copy only, set smaller and left-aligned, each paired with its evolving path-motif state. Slide 7 is the exception: text-dominant, no competing graphic, a quiet reflective beat. Slide 8 combines a body block with a large gold pull-quote treatment as the closing statement.

## 4. Typography Direction

- **Headline (Slide 1):** Bold geometric/grotesk sans-serif (style of Neue Haas Grotesk Display, Söhne Breit, or Inter Black), tight tracking, large scale, warm white on navy. The single largest type moment in the carousel.
- **Body copy (Slides 1–6, 8 top block):** Same sans-serif family, regular/medium weight, generous line-height for mobile legibility, warm white or soft steel-white.
- **Slide 7 reflective block:** Same sans family stepped up in size relative to other body slides (no competing graphic here, so type can breathe larger) — medium weight, warm white.
- **Pull quote (Slide 8):** Contrasting italic serif (style of Tiempos Italic or Canela Italic) in energy gold — the one deliberate typographic contrast in the set, marking the closing line as the editorial "money quote."
- **JustPreneur wordmark:** Clean sans, medium weight, consistent size and color (warm white; gold on Slide 8) across all 8 slides.

No more than two type families in the entire carousel (primary sans + one serif accent reserved for the Slide 8 pull quote).

---

## 5. Color Palette

- **Deep navy** `#0E1B2A` — dominant background across all slides
- **Steel gray** `#3C4A59` — secondary panels, grid-line chrome, subdued body text tint, Slide 7 alternate field
- **Warm white** `#F4F1EA` — primary text color, headline color
- **Energy gold** `#C9A227` — the desire-path line itself, checkpoint/gate accent (Slide 4), survey-bracket accent (Slide 5), node dots (Slide 6), pull-quote type and quotation mark (Slide 8)

No red/alarm color anywhere (the approval-checkpoint beat on Slide 4 must read as calm and procedural, not as a warning), and no green/celebratory color (there is no "problem resolved" beat in this story — keep the palette consistent and editorial throughout rather than shifting tone).

---

## 6. Image/Graphic Treatment

- All "imagery" in this carousel is a single evolving blueprint-grid-and-path graphic motif — flat, clean vector line work, not photography and not illustrated people. Closer to an architectural/urban-planning diagram or a premium fintech data-graphic than a lifestyle-photography aesthetic.
- Consistent grid proportions, path position, and line weight across all 8 slides so the eye reads it as one continuous evolving graphic, not eight unrelated illustrations.
- No legible text, numbers, or labels inside any graphic element.
- No Meta, Muse, Notion, Shopify, Stripe, Slack, or QuickBooks logos, wordmarks, or app-icon likenesses anywhere in the generated imagery — the approved facts block requires the connector list be presented as illustrative examples only, and no trademarked marks should be fabricated. Connector references appear in copy text only.
- No literal smartphone/chat-app UI mockups — this story is about a pattern in usage and a company's response, not a specific screen, so the visual stays at the diagram/metaphor level rather than simulating Meta's actual product interface.
- No human figures, faces, hands, or silhouettes.

---

## 7. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 px per slide.
- Keep all headline, body copy, and JustPreneur wordmark within a safe zone of **72px minimum inset** on all four sides (left/right/top/bottom) — protects against Instagram's UI cropping (profile row, caption overlap on the grid, swipe-indicator dots).
- Nothing load-bearing (text or wordmark) closer than 72px to any edge; decorative grid/path graphics may bleed closer to the edge but must not carry text.
- Maintain at least 90px of clear vertical space between the bottom of the last text block and the bottom canvas edge on every slide to avoid crowding.

---

## 8. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

- Same canvas size (1080x1350) for all 8 slides.
- Same deep-navy background base color across all 8 slides (Slide 7 may shift to steel-gray field as the one deliberate variation for pacing).
- JustPreneur wordmark identical size, color (warm white; gold only on Slide 8), and position (top-left) on every slide.
- Body copy left-aligned and set at the same type size/weight across Slides 2–6 and the top block of Slide 8 (Slide 1 headline, Slide 7 reflective block, and Slide 8 pull quote are the only intentional scale exceptions, per their roles above).
- The blueprint grid keeps identical proportions and orientation across all 8 slides so the path's state changes (absent → sketchy → gated → observed → paved → ghosted → echoed) read as one evolving object.
- Gold accent used sparingly and only for the specific "path state" cues described per slide — never as generic decoration.

---

## 9. Accessibility and Contrast Check

- Warm white (`#F4F1EA`) body/headline text on deep navy (`#0E1B2A`) background: contrast ratio well above WCAG AA for large text (~13:1), safe for headline and body use.
- Steel-gray (`#3C4A59`) used only for secondary grid chrome and subdued sub-text, never for primary headline or body copy.
- Energy gold (`#C9A227`) on navy background used for the Slide 8 pull quote: contrast ratio sufficient for large/bold serif display type; do not use this gold-on-navy pairing for small body text.
- No text is ever placed directly over busy grid/path detail — each slide's copy sits on a clean navy (or steel-gray, Slide 7) field with the motif confined to its own zone, protecting legibility at small mobile thumbnail size.
- Minimum effective text size targets: headline (Slide 1) large/bold enough to read at thumbnail scale; body copy set no smaller than what renders clearly at a ~350px-wide phone preview.
