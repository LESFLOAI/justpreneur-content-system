## Story / Facts Summary

Disney and Netflix have reportedly struck a new, multi-year, non-exclusive content-licensing agreement, first reported by Variety on October 2, 2026, and independently covered by Reuters the same day. Under the deal, titles remain simultaneously available on Disney+ and Hulu. Starting October 4, 2026, Netflix is set to stream the first two seasons of "Percy Jackson and the Olympians" and all five "Ice Age" films worldwide, each for a reported three-month promotional window — timed, per reporting, ahead of "Percy Jackson and the Olympians" Season 3's Disney+ premiere (Nov. 20, 2026) and the theatrical release of "Ice Age: Boiling Point" (Feb. 5, 2027). Additional titles reportedly follow: ABC's "Will Trent" and "Shifting Gears" (Nov. 3 and Dec. 1, 2026, respectively), legacy library shows ("Felicity," "Revenge," "Fresh Off the Boat," "Army Wives," "This Is Us"), and non-franchise Walt Disney Animation/Pixar films ("Soul," "Elio," "Raya and the Last Dragon") arriving in early 2027. No Marvel, Star Wars, or core Pixar franchise titles appear anywhere in the reported title list — consistent with, but not an explicitly confirmed policy statement for, this specific deal. No financial/dollar terms for this agreement have been publicly disclosed in any source reviewed.

**Not asserted anywhere in this package:** any dollar figure or deal valuation (none exists in any source reviewed); the Marvel/Star Wars/core-Pixar exclusion as a stated or confirmed policy for this deal (it is framed throughout as an inference from the reported title list — "notably absent," "none of the reported titles include"); any CEO or executive quote (the originally-considered Bob Iger "selling arms to the competition" line was dropped entirely — fact-checking found it factually inaccurate and conflated with a different, older, unrelated remark); this deal as an officially Disney/Netflix-confirmed announcement (all claims carry "reportedly" / "per Variety and Reuters" attribution throughout).
