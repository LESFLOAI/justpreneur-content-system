## CANDIDATE 1 (TOP PICK) — Getty Images goes private via prepackaged Chapter 11, founding family + Koch Equity take 100%

1. **Working headline:** "The Stock Photo Giant Just Un-IPO'd Itself — On Purpose"

2. **What happened:** Getty Images announced a restructuring support agreement under which it
will go through a prepackaged Chapter 11 process; the Getty family (its founding family) and
Koch Equity Development (Charles Koch's private investment arm) will commit up to roughly
$533 million in new equity and become the 100% owners of the reorganized, private company.
Existing public shares are cancelled (worth $0), unsecured bondholders agreed to retire $270
million in notes, and secured lenders agreed to extend maturities and lower interest, with the
restructuring expected to close by year-end 2026.

3. **Date of development:** Announced October 12, 2026. The 8-K sets October 21, 2026 (today) as
the start of the formal vote-solicitation period for the plan — meaning the process is active
right now, not stale news.

4. **Why it's gaining attention now:** It's a rare "founder family buys back the company they
took public" story, paired with an unusual, headline-grabbing partner (an industrial
conglomerate's investment arm, not a typical media/PE buyer). It also closes the loop on the
very public, very expensive collapse of the planned Getty–Shutterstock merger (killed by UK
regulators in June 2026), which CEO Craig Peters said cost Getty over $100 million in dead-deal
fees. There's also a live undertone about AI-generated imagery eroding stock-photo economics,
which is itself a hot topic.

5. **Potential JustPreneur lesson:** Don't bet the balance sheet on a single regulator-dependent
merger; when the "easy exit" (merging with your biggest rival) gets blocked, sometimes the
sharpest strategic move is going private and recapitalizing with insiders who already understand
the business, rather than chasing another public buyer. Also: visual/content businesses are
being forced to rethink ownership structure in the AI era.

6. **Best format:** Carousel (needs 3-4 slides to explain: IPO history → failed merger → bankruptcy
mechanics → new ownership structure) or a Reel with voiceover walking through the "from public
company to founder-family-owned" arc.

7. **Expected audience response:** Strong engagement from business/media/creator audience —
recognizable brand (everyone has used a Getty-watermarked image), surprising twist (an oil-and-
industrial conglomerate's equity arm now co-owns a photo library), and a clean "lesson learned"
hook. Comments likely to debate AI's role in killing stock photography.

8. **Sources:**
   - [Getty Family and Koch Equity Development commit up to $533 million — Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/getty-family-and-koch-equity-development-commit-up-to-533-million-to-getty-images-in-restructuring/)
   - [Getty Images to go private after restructuring deal — KSL/AP wire](https://www.ksl.com/article/51635383/getty-images-to-go-private-after-restructuring-deal)
   - [Getty Images restructuring details — ContentGrip](https://www.contentgrip.com/getty-images-restructuring/)
   - [Getty Images Chapter 11 bankruptcy background — Black Enterprise](https://www.blackenterprise.com/getty-images-to-file-for-chapter-11-bankruptcy/)
   - SEC 8-K filing (referenced across coverage) setting Oct 21, 2026 vote-solicitation date

9. **Uncertainty / needs verification:**
   - Exact breakdown of the $533M figure — one source says it nets to ~$433M in new cash equity
     once ~$100M of converted existing debt is excluded; needs confirmation from the actual 8-K/
     court filing.
   - Whether the Chapter 11 petition has actually been filed yet, or is still pending ("expected
     in the coming weeks" as of Oct 12 reports) — Fact Verifier should check for a filing date
     between Oct 12-21.
   - Total debt figure (~$2.1B) is cited by financial press, not the company — flag as unconfirmed.
   - Which specific entities/subsidiaries the Chapter 11 filing covers (reports say "certain U.S.
     entities," not the whole global company) — needs precision for accuracy.
   - Koch Equity's reported additional $143M financing commitment is single-sourced — verify or drop.

10. **Scores (1-10):**
    - Timeliness: 10 (vote solicitation begins literally today)
    - Relevance: 9 (media/ownership/investment, directly in JustPreneur's wheelhouse)
    - Visual potential: 8 (strong photo-library brand recognition; needs clean infographic treatment)
    - Business value: 9 (clear, teachable M&A/ownership lesson with a surprising partner)

## CANDIDATE 2 — Penske Corp. and Mitsui take Penske Automotive Group private in $16.3B deal

1. **Working headline:** "Roger Penske Just Bought Back the 28% He Didn't Own"

2. **What happened:** Penske Corporation and Mitsui & Co. — who already controlled roughly 72%
of Penske Automotive Group — agreed to buy out the remaining ~27.8% public float for $212/share
in cash (~$3.9B), taking the NYSE-listed auto dealership and transportation giant fully private at
a $16.3B enterprise value. Penske Corp. will hold ~64% and Mitsui ~36% post-close; a special
committee of independent directors unanimously approved the deal.

3. **Date of development:** Announced October 15, 2026.

4. **Why it's gaining attention now:** It's one of the largest "take-private by the controlling
family/partner" deals of the year, and it's a clean, well-documented example of an insider buyout
rather than a distressed sale — a contrast to Getty's bankruptcy-driven version of the same basic
move (public company → private, insiders end up owning it).

5. **Potential JustPreneur lesson:** When you already control a company operationally, going
private lets you stop managing to quarterly Wall Street expectations and run the long game —
but it only works cleanly when minority shareholders get a real premium and an independent
committee signs off, which is the detail that keeps this from looking like a squeeze-out.

6. **Best format:** Single post or short carousel — the deal mechanics are straightforward
(premium, ownership split, timeline) and don't need as much visual explanation as Getty.

7. **Expected audience response:** Moderate — automotive/dealership business is less visually
or emotionally resonant for a general IG audience than a brand like Getty Images, but will land
with business-minded followers interested in M&A structure and the "controller buyout" format.

8. **Sources:**
   - [SEC Form 8-K / press release — Penske Automotive Group, Inc.](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001019849/000114036126039664/ef20083688_ex99-1.htm)
   - [Penske Corp. and Mitsui to take Penske Automotive private — Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/penske-automotive-group-to-be-taken-private-by-penske-corporation-and-mitsui-in-16-3-billion-deal/)
   - [WardsAuto: Penske Automotive goes private at $16.3B valuation](https://www.wardsauto.com/news/penske-automotive-goes-private-at-163b-valuation/833132/)
   - [Paul Weiss client news on the deal](https://www.paulweiss.com/insights/client-news/penske-automotive-group-to-be-acquired-by-penske-corporation-and-mitsui-co)

9. **Uncertainty / needs verification:**
   - Premium percentage is reported two ways (8.6% vs. July 21 close; 8.84% vs. Oct 14 close) —
     needs a single clean number for the final post.
   - Deal is not yet closed (expected Q1 2027) and still requires a majority-of-the-minority
     shareholder vote — framing should be "agreed to go private," not "is now private."

10. **Scores (1-10):**
    - Timeliness: 8
    - Relevance: 6 (real estate/investment-adjacent, but auto dealerships are a thinner fit for
      JustPreneur's usual lanes)
    - Visual potential: 5 (dealership/car-brand imagery is less distinctive)
    - Business value: 7
