## 1. Overall Creative Concept

The carousel is built around a single recurring image: **a ledger** — the accounting device that gives the angle its name ("two authors, one insurance policy"). A thin gold hairline divides every composition into two accounts, echoing a business media graphic (think a Bloomberg or Economist explainer chart) rather than a motivational-quote template. The ledger line opens as vertical (cover), becomes a two-column table (Callaway's account, then Good Good's account), softens into a bridge (the fair-balance slide), fractures into an uneven shield (the takeaway), and closes as an underlined contract clause (the CTA). No incident imagery, no photos, no faces — the entire story is told through typographic weight, panel fill, and one recurring gold accent line.

## 2. Composition & Focal Hierarchy (carousel-wide)

1. JustPreneur wordmark — smallest, top-center, identical position/size every slide (brand anchor, never competes with headline).
2. Headline / column header — largest, highest-contrast element per slide.
3. Body copy / bullet list — secondary, generous line spacing, left-aligned within its column.
4. Gold ledger-line motif — connective tissue, never decorative clutter; always structural (a divider, a bridge, an underline).
5. Background field — deep navy, flat or very subtly gradiented, never busy enough to compete with text.

## 3. Slide-by-Slide Placement

**Slide 1 — Cover**
- JustPreneur wordmark: top-center, small, letter-spaced caps, warm white at ~70% opacity.
- Thin gold vertical hairline runs from just below the wordmark to the bottom safe margin, dead center of the canvas.
- Tiny generic wordmark labels flank the hairline in the upper third: "CALLAWAY" (steel gray, left of the line) and "GOOD GOOD" (warm white, right of the line) — small, letter-spaced, clearly secondary to the headline.
- Headline, vertically centered, straddling the hairline, three lines: "Callaway" / "Approved It." / "Good Good Lost Everything." — first two lines in warm white, "Lost Everything." rendered in energy gold for tension.

**Slide 2 — Setup**
- JustPreneur wordmark: same top-center position/size.
- The vertical hairline from Slide 1 rotates into a horizontal ledger line across the middle third of the canvas, with one small marker node on it (a plain circle, no icon detail) — visually says "a single event just happened on this timeline."
- Body copy (all three approved sentences) stacked above and below the line in warm white, generous leading, centered column width with wide side margins.

**Slide 3 — Callaway column**
- JustPreneur wordmark: same position.
- Two-column ledger reappears, but only the **left** column is filled (solid steel-gray panel, full column height); the right column is a thin ghosted outline only — mostly empty, foreshadowing Slide 4.
- Column header "CALLAWAY" bold, letter-spaced caps, top of the left panel, warm white on steel gray.
- Three bullet lines (approved copy, gold en-dash bullets) inside the left panel, warm white text.

**Slide 4 — Good Good column**
- JustPreneur wordmark: same position.
- Mirror of Slide 3's ledger, but now the **right** column is filled — a deeper, heavier charcoal/near-black panel with a fine gold hairline outline (visually "heavier" than Callaway's flat steel gray, to convey asymmetry of consequence without any dramatization). The left (Callaway) column recedes to a thin ghosted outline, quiet and resolved.
- Column header "GOOD GOOD" bold, letter-spaced caps, top of the right panel, warm white on charcoal.
- Four bullet lines (approved copy, gold en-dash bullets) inside the right panel, warm white text — this is the longest text block in the carousel, so the panel gets extra vertical room and the smallest (but still comfortably legible) body size in the set.

**Slide 5 — Fair-balance / nuance**
- JustPreneur wordmark: same position.
- Both ledger columns return, now smaller/condensed and set side by side higher on the canvas (steel gray left, charcoal-with-gold-outline right, matching Slides 3–4's fills but reduced in scale) — a quiet recap, not a new visual idea.
- A single thin gold horizontal bridge line connects the two columns lower on the canvas, under which the fair-balance copy sits — signaling "connection/continuation" rather than a closed ledger.
- Body copy (three approved sentences) below the bridge line, centered, warm white.

**Slide 6 — Final takeaway**
- JustPreneur wordmark: same position.
- Departs from the table motif: a single abstract shield/policy glyph, split down the middle by the recurring gold hairline — the left half thin and small, the right half thick and large — visualizing "two authors, one insurance policy, and it's the smaller brand's" without any literal insurance iconography or text inside the glyph.
- Large pull-quote-style headline block below/around the glyph, warm white with "Not protection." in gold for emphasis, generous line spacing, centered.

**Slide 7 — Call to action**
- JustPreneur wordmark: same position, but slightly enlarged relative to other slides (sign-off emphasis) — still modest, not a lockup.
- Single abstract contract/document glyph (plain rectangle with three generic horizontal rule lines suggesting clauses, no real text) with one clause line underlined in gold, positioned upper-middle.
- CTA copy below it, warm white, with "Save this." rendered in gold as the visual button-like emphasis line.
- Generous bottom safe margin left empty — no added labels, handles, or dates beyond the approved copy.

## 4. Typography Direction

- **Headline face:** Bold condensed/display grotesk (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk Display Bold, Söhne Kräftig, or Inter Display Bold as a substitute) — sentence case, not all-caps, to keep it feeling like premium business journalism rather than a motivational template.
- **Column headers / labels ("CALLAWAY", "GOOD GOOD"):** small caps or true caps, wide letter-spacing (~8–10%), medium weight — clearly typographic wordmarks, never styled to imitate the real brand logos.
- **Body copy / bullets:** clean grotesk regular/medium (e.g., Inter, Söhne Buch), generous line height (~135–150%), left-aligned within its column or centered for setup/CTA/takeaway slides per the layout above.
- **Bullet marker:** gold em/en-dash "—" exactly as written in the approved copy — do not convert to bullet dots or numbers.
- **Never** render large blocks of approved copy inside the generated image itself — every word of headline/body/bullet text is added at the layout stage over the abstract background (see Section 11).

## 5. Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base background | Deep navy | `#0E1B2A` | Full-bleed background on every slide |
| Callaway column | Steel gray | `#3C4A59` | Slide 3 filled panel, Slide 5 recap panel (left) |
| Good Good column | Charcoal / near-black | `#141B22` with gold hairline outline `#C79A4B` | Slide 4 filled panel, Slide 5 recap panel (right) |
| Primary text | Warm white | `#F4F1E9` | Headlines, body copy, bullets |
| Accent / tension words / ledger line | Energy gold | `#C79A4B` | Hairline dividers, bridge line, "Lost Everything.", "Not protection.", "Save this." |

Adjust only if brand guidelines change; this story does not call for a departure from the default palette.

## 6. Image Treatment

Every generated background is a flat, editorial, vector-leaning abstract composition — no photography, no photorealistic rendering, no textured photo-illustration. Treat each as a print-magazine chart/graphic backdrop, not a decorative poster. Panels are solid or near-solid fills with crisp edges; the only "texture" allowed is an extremely subtle fine-grain noise on the navy base to avoid a flat-digital banding look at export size. No gradients busy enough to fight with overlaid text.

## 7. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait, 4:5).
- Keep all text and the ledger-line motif inside a safe area of **96px minimum on left/right** and **150px minimum on top/bottom** (top clearance avoids the profile-grid crop and app UI overlap; bottom clearance avoids the caption/like-bar overlap in feed view).
- JustPreneur wordmark sits at a fixed vertical position ~110–130px from the top edge, inside the top safe margin — never flush to the very top edge.
- No element (hairline, panel edge, glyph) should touch or cross the safe-margin boundary; panels should visually "float" inside the frame, not bleed to the canvas edge, except the full-bleed navy base itself.

## 8. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

1. JustPreneur wordmark: identical size, weight, color, and vertical position on all 7 slides.
2. Deep navy `#0E1B2A` base on all 7 slides — no slide switches to a different base color.
3. The gold hairline (`#C79A4B`) motif must appear in some form on every slide (vertical divider, horizontal timeline, column outline, bridge line, split-glyph line, or clause underline) — it is the visual thread that carries the reader through the carousel.
4. Column header treatment ("CALLAWAY" = steel gray, "GOOD GOOD" = warm white on charcoal) is fixed wherever those labels recur (Slides 1, 3, 4, 5) — never swap the color assignment.
5. Bullet formatting (gold en-dash, warm white text, same left indent) is identical on Slides 3 and 4.
6. No slide numbers, dates, citations, watermarks, or corner labels on any slide, per standing brand instruction.
7. Headline type size may vary by copy length but must never drop below a size that renders comfortably at typical in-feed thumbnail scale (roughly 60–72pt effective at full 1080 width for single-line headline words).

## 9. Accessibility & Contrast Check

- Warm white `#F4F1E9` on deep navy `#0E1B2A`: contrast ratio ≈ 14.7:1 — passes WCAG AAA for all text sizes.
- Warm white `#F4F1E9` on steel gray `#3C4A59` (Slide 3 panel): contrast ratio ≈ 8.1:1 — passes AAA for normal text.
- Warm white `#F4F1E9` on charcoal `#141B22` (Slide 4 panel): contrast ratio ≈ 15.8:1 — passes AAA.
- Energy gold `#C79A4B` on deep navy `#0E1B2A`: contrast ratio ≈ 6.4:1 — passes AA but not AAA at small sizes. **Rule: gold text is reserved for large display words only (bold, ~28pt-equivalent or larger)** — "Lost Everything.", "Not protection.", "Save this." All gold usage below that size is restricted to the hairline/structural motif, not body text.
- No color pair in this palette is used as the sole differentiator of meaning without also being reinforced by position/panel fill (e.g., Callaway vs. Good Good is never gray-vs-gold alone — it is also left-column vs. right-column, and different panel fills), supporting readers with color-vision deficiencies.
