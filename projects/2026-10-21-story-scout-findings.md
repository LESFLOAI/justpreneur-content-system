## CANDIDATE 1 — RECOMMENDED: Corner Store goes from SoHo cult favorite to Vegas Strip flagship

**Headline:** "The Hardest Reservation in SoHo Just Became a Vegas Headliner"

**What happened:** The Corner Store — the Tilman Fertitta/Eugene Remm (Catch Hospitality Group) sandwich-and-caviar-roll concept that became a notoriously hard-to-book NYC hotspot after its late-2024 SoHo debut — is opening its first-ever outside-NYC location at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas on November 9, 2026. Reservations opened October 2, 2026, with a Vegas-exclusive menu expansion (a dedicated "Butcher's Counter" steak section) built in partnership with MGM Resorts.

**Date of development:** Reservations/opening-date announcement: October 2, 2026. Opening: November 9, 2026 (18 days out from our posting date — active anticipation window).

**Why it's gaining attention now:** It's the brand's first expansion outside New York City, timed into Vegas's high-traffic Strip tourist economy, and multiple hospitality trade outlets (NRN-adjacent, casino/Vegas trade press) have been actively covering reservation availability and the menu reveal through mid-October.

**Potential JustPreneur lesson:** How to export scarcity. A concept that built its identity on being nearly impossible to book in one city is now testing whether that same scarcity-and-hype playbook travels to a market built entirely on tourists and instant gratification — and doing it through a structured partnership (MGM Resorts) rather than solo risk.

**Best format:** Carousel — slide 1 "the SoHo original," slide 2 "what's new for Vegas" (Butcher's Counter, signature dishes), slide 3 the lesson (proof-of-concept before you scale into a new market).

**Expected audience response:** Strong — food/lifestyle crossover audience, recognizable names (Fertitta), aspirational dining content performs well on IG.
