## Approved Facts Block (verbatim, used by all downstream agents)

Getty Images Holdings, Inc. and several U.S. subsidiaries filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on October 21, 2026, in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, implementing a prepackaged restructuring plan under a Restructuring Support Agreement (RSA) signed October 12, 2026. Under the RSA, members of the Getty family and affiliates of Koch Equity Development (KED), the investment arm of Koch Industries, have committed to invest up to $533 million in new equity and are set to own 100% of the reorganized, private company. All existing public equity interests, including common stock, are expected to be cancelled with no recovery. Holders of approximately 83.5% of Getty's unsecured notes agreed to eliminate $270 million of senior unsecured notes, while secured lenders (representing ~89% of secured funded debt and 100% of revolving loans) agreed to extend maturities and reduce interest costs. Getty reported total debt of approximately $2.1 billion as of June 30, 2026 (Reuters, citing company financials). Getty's Class A common stock was suspended from the NYSE on September 29, 2026, and had traded on OTC Pink since September 30, 2026. This restructuring follows Getty's termination, in early July 2026, of its proposed $3.7 billion merger with Shutterstock, after the UK Competition and Markets Authority conditioned clearance on Shutterstock divesting its editorial business (Shutterstock Editorial, Backgrid, Splash) — a condition Getty declined to accept; the U.S. Department of Justice had already cleared the deal unconditionally. Getty's CEO stated the company incurred more than $100 million in professional fees and financing costs over more than 18 months pursuing that merger (this was sunk deal cost, not a contractual breakup fee; the merger agreement's actual termination fees were $32.7 million and $40 million under specified scenarios). Vote solicitation on the prepackaged plan began October 20, 2026. Getty expects the restructuring to be completed by year-end 2026.

**Not included (unverified):** a reported additional ~$143 million Koch debtor-in-possession financing commitment; any claim that the $533 million nets to ~$433 million in "new" equity after debt conversion.
