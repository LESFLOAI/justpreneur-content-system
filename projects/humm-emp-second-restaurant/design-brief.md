# Visual Design Brief — "Daniel Humm Isn't Reversing Course. He's Opening a New One." (8-slide carousel)

Status: DRAFT MODE — no images generated yet (no image-generation API key available in this cloud environment). All content below is ready for `/justpreneur-finish` to execute later.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a mini business-editorial explainer, closer to a Bloomberg/Eater/Fast Company visual style than a motivational-quote template. The visual arc moves through five beats: Bet → Cost → Reversal → New Venture → Analysis/Takeaway/CTA. Each slide is a photographic, moody, restaurant/hospitality-world background image with type laid over generous negative space — never text baked fully into the AI image. Slide 6 ("Our Read") gets a deliberately different, cooler, more graphic/conceptual treatment plus an explicit "EDITORIAL TAKE" badge so it cannot be mistaken for a reported fact or Humm's own words.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: single dominant focal image element (dish, hearth, doorway, notebook, etc.) placed off-center in the lower half or one side, with a large clean negative-space zone (usually the top 35–45%) reserved for headline + body copy. Hierarchy per slide, top to bottom:
1. Small kicker/section label (e.g., "THE BET") — top margin
2. Headline/body copy block — upper-to-mid frame
3. Photographic focal subject — lower half
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, consistent position across all 8 slides (bottom-center or bottom-right, ~60px above the absolute bottom safe margin)

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface: a bold, high-contrast serif or modern slab-serif (e.g., a Tiempos/Canela/GT Sectra-style editorial serif) for slide 1 headline and section kickers — signals "premium media," not generic Instagram-template sans.
- Body copy typeface: a clean grotesk/sans (e.g., a Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style sans) for all body paragraphs — maximizes mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Kickers ("THE BET," "THE COST," etc.): sans, all-caps, letter-spaced (~150–200 tracking), small size (~28–32px equivalent), gold or warm-white.
- Headline sizing: large enough to be legible at thumbnail size on a phone — no line should exceed ~34 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on a 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (not justified) for readability.
- Slide 6 disclaimer line: same sans body font, smaller size (~30–34px), italic or medium-gray-on-navy to visually subordinate it from the main analysis sentence while remaining fully readable — must still pass contrast check (see section 9).

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220 to #10172A range) — dominant background/shadow tone across all slides for consistency
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces, kicker/badge fills
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 or #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds
- Energy gold (#C9A24B / #D4AF37 range) — kickers, accents, hearth/flame highlights, CTA emphasis, wordmark
- Slide 6 gets a slightly cooler navy-to-steel-gray shift (less gold, more gray-blue) to visually separate "analysis" from "reporting" beats — the badge itself uses gold-outline-on-steel or steel-fill-on-navy to stand out as a flagged element.

## 6. Image treatment

- All background photos: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm highlight pops (gold/warm-white) — consistent color grade across all 8 slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying photo — this is a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, or real identifiable people/faces should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all 8 slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge (left/right/top/bottom) at minimum; Instagram's UI (profile row, caption, like/comment icons) can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px on the feed preview — keep headline anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~840px wide (leaving ≥120px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size, identical kicker style/position (top-left, gold, small-caps) on slides 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8; slide 6 intentionally varies via the added badge.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color on every slide (bottom-center) for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/gray/gold consistent) except slide 6's intentionally cooler cast.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across slides 2–5 and 7; slide 1 headline and slide 8 CTA may be centered as the "bookend" slides.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, not just the general background.
- Avoid placing gold (#C9A24B) text directly on light/warm-white photo areas — gold is reserved for kickers/accents on dark navy/steel backgrounds only, where contrast is strong.
- Slide 6's smaller disclaimer line must still meet at least AA-level contrast (do not rely on italic + gray-on-gray alone) — use warm-white or light steel-gray (not mid-gray) on the navy overlay.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas (roughly 14–16pt equivalent at full-size phone viewing) to remain legible when scaled down to feed thumbnail size.
