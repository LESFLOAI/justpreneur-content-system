## 0. Hard constraints carried into every visual decision

These override the general brand defaults wherever they conflict. All are non-negotiable — flag them clearly to whoever runs generation locally.

1. **No real-person likenesses.** André 3000 and Big Boi are named real individuals. No prompt asks an image generator to render a recognizable face for either of them, or for any other real person. Any human presence in generated art is hands-only, rear-view, silhouetted, or anonymized crowd-at-a-distance — never a posed portrait or an attempt at a specific likeness.
2. **No reproduction of real OutKast or Waffle House logos, wordmarks, or trade dress.** Waffle House's actual yellow-and-black signage system and OutKast's actual logos/album art are protected trademarks and instantly recognizable — no generated image contains a readable "OutKast" or "Waffle House" wordmark, no literal yellow diner sign, no reproduction of the *Stankonia* or *So Fresh, So Clean* cover art. Diner coding is abstracted to a **generic** checkerboard-floor, stainless-steel-counter, yellow-and-black-accent diner aesthetic; music-archive coding is abstracted to **generic** vinyl/cassette/archive-crate imagery — evocative of the category, never an attempted reproduction of a specific real mark.
3. **No fabricated news chyrons, mastheads, "BREAKING" banners, citation marks, dateline stamps, or screenshot-style UI baked into any image.** This is a JustPreneur editorial carousel, not a simulated news or social-media screenshot. All dates, labels, and the ANALYSIS/CAVEAT tags are added afterward as JustPreneur's own type layer (see §3, §11).
4. **No text baked into any generated image.** All headlines, kickers, dates, tag badges, and body copy are added after generation as type layers — see the separate layout prompt in §11.
5. **Slide 3 — the two time blocks must never visually merge.** The 10 a.m. ET online drop and the 11 a.m.–1 p.m. in-person pop-up are two separate, independently time-stamped events (different channel, different hours, different city detail). The generated visual and the layout must keep them as two clearly divided panels — never a single blended scene implying one event.
6. **Slide 4 — the ANALYSIS label must read as visually distinct from reported-fact slides.** This slide is JustPreneur's inference about sell-out timing and crowd size, not a verified fact (sources vary on crowd estimates). It gets its own badge treatment (outlined, not solid-filled — see §3, §4, §9) so a viewer can tell at a glance "this is interpretation" vs. the solid gold fact-kickers on Slides 2–3.
7. **Slide 7 — the caveat/disclaimer gets the same "not a confident fact" badge language as Slide 4**, for visual consistency, since both slides are flagging "this is not a disclosed/verified claim."

## 1. Overall creative concept

This reads like a **Fast Company or Modern Retail "channel strategy" explainer**, not a nostalgia reel about OutKast and not a diner fan post. The visual spine is **archive-to-storefront**: imagery moves from analog/archival texture (vinyl, cassette, tape reels, archive crates — representing "26-year-old catalog IP") toward clean, owned-retail texture (a controlled storefront, a single checkout, a single door) — visually dramatizing the copy's real point: *old IP, new owned channel*.

A persistent **top tag-badge system** runs across Slides 2–7 (absent on the pure hook/CTA bookends, Slides 1 and 8) so a fast-scrolling viewer can instantly read what kind of claim each slide is making:
- **Solid gold filled badge** = reported fact, sourced and dated (Slides 2, 3).
- **Outlined, dashed-border steel-gray badge** = JustPreneur's own labeled interpretation or an explicit disclaimer, not a verified claim (Slides 4, 7).
- **No badge** = narrative/lesson/action slides that aren't making a dated factual claim (Slides 5, 6).

Two visual textures alternate with the story's channels:
- **Archive texture** (vinyl, cassette, tape, crate, analog warmth) — Slides 2, 5, 6 — "this is old catalog material."
- **Owned-storefront / controlled-retail texture** (clean checkout counter, single door, single register, generic diner-adjacent counter for the pop-up half of Slide 3) — Slides 3, 4 — "this is where and how it actually sold."

Slide 1 opens on a clean navy field with a softly blurred archive texture as atmosphere (not a literal scene) so the hook headline dominates. Slide 8 closes the same way it opened — a clean navy-and-gold quote-card, no competing texture, for maximum "save this" legibility.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

- **Cover (Slide 1):** Headline + sub-line centered in the top ~55% of the frame on a clean navy field with a restrained, softly blurred archive-crate/vinyl texture as background atmosphere. No badge, no kicker. Headline is the single dominant element.
- **Interior slides (2, 4, 5, 6, 7):** Tag badge (where applicable) sits in the top safe zone (~y:120–190). Below it, a short gold kicker (the date/label) at ~y:220–300. Below the kicker, body copy in warm white, word-for-word from approved copy. The generated visual occupies the lower ~55–60% of the frame, full-bleed to the side margins.
- **Slide 3 (two-channel split):** Top safe zone carries a single kicker ("FRIDAY, OCTOBER 2"). Below it, the frame splits into **two stacked, clearly divided panels**: an upper panel labeled with its own small timestamp ("10:00 A.M. ET — ONLINE") over the storefront/checkout visual, and a lower panel labeled with its own timestamp ("11 A.M.–1 P.M. — IN-PERSON POP-UP, ATLANTA") over the diner-counter visual, separated by a visible thin gold divider line/gutter. Body copy sentences sit within each panel's own text zone, never spanning across the divider.
- **Final CTA (Slide 8):** No photographic background — a clean navy-and-gold quote-card layout, generous margin, the two CTA lines centered with equal weight.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Headline/cover typeface:** bold, semicondensed grotesk (same family used across prior JustPreneur carousels — keep consistent). Sentence case for the Slide 1 headline and sub-line.
- **Badges (Slides 2, 3 vs. 4, 7):** small all-caps label type, letter-spaced. "REPORTED" badges (Slides 2, 3) are solid gold fill with navy text — confident, factual. "ANALYSIS" and "CAVEAT" badges (Slides 4, 7) are outlined with a dashed steel-gray border, steel-gray text, transparent/navy interior — deliberately less confident-looking than the solid gold badges, signaling "read this as interpretation, not verified fact."
- **Kicker (dates/labels, all interior slides):** all-caps, bold, gold, large enough to read at a glance in a fast scroll — same size/weight treatment across Slides 2–7 for consistency.
- **Body copy:** warm white, regular-to-medium weight, generous line-height, word-for-word from the approved copy — no paraphrasing, no shortening, no added words (including not altering "ANALYSIS:" capitalization on Slide 4, which is part of the approved copy itself and also echoed as a design badge).
- **Slide 3 panel timestamps:** smaller gold caption-weight label ("10:00 A.M. ET — ONLINE" / "11 A.M.–1 P.M. — IN-PERSON POP-UP, ATLANTA") sitting at the top of each respective panel, distinct from the shared top kicker.
- **Slide 6 list:** three lines, each starting with an em-dash exactly as in the approved copy, consistent left alignment, comfortable line-height between items so it reads as a checklist, not a cramped paragraph.
- **Slide 8 CTA:** both lines large and centered, warm white for the first line, gold for the closing question — the "save this" slide, most typographically confident frame after Slide 1.
- No script fonts, no diner-menu-board lettering, no retro-funk pastiche typography anywhere — this stays in JustPreneur's own premium business-editorial voice, not a "nostalgia throwback" pastiche.

## 5. Color palette

**Brand base (unchanged):** deep navy, steel gray, warm white, energy gold — used for all text, kickers, wordmark, and UI chrome across every slide.

**Story-specific accent layer (background photographic/textural art only, not text):**
- Warm analog-amber for archive-texture slides (2, 5, 6) — vinyl/cassette/crate warmth, suggesting "catalog material."
- Clean steel-navy for the owned-storefront half of Slide 3's split (upper panel) — controlled, modern, "their platform."
- A restrained, desaturated warm yellow-and-black diner accent (generic, non-trademarked) for the pop-up half of Slide 3's split (lower panel) and for Slide 4's analysis visual — just enough to read as "diner" without imitating a specific real brand's signage palette.
- Slide 4's analysis visual additionally carries a muted, slightly uncertain gray-blue wash (variance/uncertainty cue) distinct from the confident golds of Slides 2–3.
- Slide 7 (caveat) is the quietest slide in the set — flat steel-gray and navy only, no amber warmth, no diner accent — visually "neutral administrative" to match a disclaimer.
- Slides 1 and 8 are pure brand navy field with gold accents only — no photographic color grade at all.

## 6. Image treatment

- Photographic/editorial-documentary texture for Slides 2–6; restrained abstract/textural treatment for Slides 1, 7, 8.
- Archive-texture slides (2, 5, 6): warm, tactile detail — vinyl grooves, cassette spools, tape reels, cardboard archive-crate edges, shallow depth of field, soft practical light. No readable labels, no album art, no real logos anywhere on any object.
- Storefront/pop-up slides (3, 4): clean, modern retail-checkout texture (upper half of Slide 3) contrasted with a generic stainless-steel-and-checkerboard diner-counter texture (lower half of Slide 3; also present in Slide 4's analysis visual) — both with entirely blank, unmarked signage and menu boards.
- No text baked into any generated image anywhere — all copy, kickers, badges, and timestamps are added as layers after generation (see §11).
- No reproduction of real OutKast or Waffle House logos, wordmarks, album covers, or specific signage (see §0.2). No fabricated substitute logos either — all signage/menu-board surfaces in frame are blank, turned away, or softly out of focus.
- Human presence, where used at all, is hands-only, rear-view, distant/anonymized crowd silhouette, or absent entirely — no posed portraits, no recognizable faces, no attempt at a likeness of any real person (see §0.1).
- Crowd/line imagery (Slide 3 lower panel, Slide 4) is a soft, anonymized silhouette crowd at a distance — never individuated faces, never a specific headcount implied by the art itself (headcount uncertainty is a text-layer point, not something the image should try to depict precisely).

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

**Carousel (1080x1350):** Minimum 72px margin on all sides for any text or wordmark. Interior slides (2, 4, 5, 6, 7): keep the top ~35% of the frame (through y ≈ 380) clean and low-detail for the badge + kicker + body copy stack; the generated visual occupies the lower ~55–60%, full-bleed to the side margins. **Slide 3 is the exception**: reserve the top ~12% (through y ≈ 160) for the shared kicker only, then split the remaining frame into two roughly equal panels (each ~y:160–755 and ~y:755–1350) separated by a visible ~8–12px gold divider line with at least 24px of additional breathing room on each side of the divider before any text or image content begins — this prevents the two timestamped events from visually bleeding into each other. Cover (Slide 1): headline block centered with equal top/bottom breathing room, no badge, no kicker. Slide 8: entirely typographic — reserve generous margin around the two CTA lines (at least 100px top/bottom beyond the 72px minimum). JustPreneur wordmark centered in the bottom safe margin, identical size/position, on all 8 slides.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Badge style (solid-gold-fill "REPORTED" vs. outlined-dashed-steel "ANALYSIS"/"CAVEAT") stays pixel-identical in position, size, and treatment everywhere it appears — only the label text and fill/outline state change, per §0.6, §0.7, §3, §4.
- Kicker style (position, size, gold color, all-caps) stays identical across all slides that carry one; only the label content changes.
- The archive-texture vs. owned-storefront/diner-texture alternation (§5, §6) must be visually consistent every time a given texture reappears, so a viewer feels "old catalog material" vs. "controlled modern channel" as a rhythm without rereading the copy.
- JustPreneur wordmark treatment (size, position, opacity) is identical across all 8 slides.
- Grotesk type family is identical throughout — no font switching between slides.
- Slide 3's divider-line gold color and weight should visually match the divider/accent weight used nowhere else in the set — it is a unique element reserved for this slide's two-channel split, so it reads as a deliberate structural device, not decoration.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm-white text (#F5F3EE-equivalent) on a navy scrim (#0B1E32-equivalent) at ≥70% scrim opacity clears WCAG AA for large text at carousel scale.
- Gold kicker/badge-fill text (energy-gold, e.g., #D4A017-equivalent) is reserved for short, large-size labels — never for full paragraph body copy, since gold-on-navy body text at small sizes drops below comfortable contrast on compressed mobile screens.
- The outlined "ANALYSIS"/"CAVEAT" badges use steel-gray text on a near-transparent navy fill with a dashed steel-gray border — confirm this still clears AA contrast against its background at the small badge size; if local review finds it too low-contrast, lighten the steel-gray toward warm white before shrinking the badge, rather than filling it solid (filling it solid would defeat the "this is interpretation, not confident fact" visual signal).
- Body copy is never placed directly over high-frequency photographic texture (vinyl grooves, crate edges, checkerboard tile, crowd silhouettes) without a navy scrim between text and image — scrim is mandatory on every interior slide, not optional, especially on Slide 3 where two scrims are needed (one per panel) and Slide 4 where the copy block is the longest in the set.
- Minimum body-copy size renders no smaller than is comfortably legible at arm's length on a phone; Slide 4 (longest copy) and Slide 3 (two independent text blocks in half the vertical space each) should be checked first during local assembly since they have the least margin for error.
