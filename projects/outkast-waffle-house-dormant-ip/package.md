## Story / Facts Summary

On Tuesday, September 29, 2026, OutKast (Big Boi and André 3000) and Waffle House teased a merchandise collaboration with a social post referencing the lyric "gator belts and patty melts" from OutKast's 2000 single "So Fresh, So Clean" (off the album *Stankonia*). The full collection — including items such as T-shirts, a hoodie, bucket hat, jersey, socks, belts, and kitchen-themed pieces (mugs, spatulas, aprons) — went on sale exclusively on outkast.com at 10 a.m. ET on Friday, October 2, 2026. A companion in-person pop-up ran from roughly 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. that day at a Waffle House location on Andrew Young International Boulevard near Centennial Olympic Park in downtown Atlanta, featuring limited-edition "André 3000" and "Big Boi" meals, free souvenir menus, and coupons. Multiple outlets reported the online collection sold out within minutes, with remaining items shifting to pre-order with shipping estimates into early 2027; crowd-size estimates at the pop-up varied by outlet from "hundreds" to "thousands." The story was covered within 48 hours by CBS News Atlanta, Atlanta News First, Fox5 Atlanta, 11Alive, theGrio, Hoodline, Complex, Axios Atlanta, Inc., AJC, WABE, and GPB, among others. **No licensing, revenue-share, or other financial terms of the OutKast–Waffle House arrangement have been publicly disclosed by either party. No source ties this drop to any album anniversary.**
