## 1. Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, verbatim from Fact Verifier)

The Corner Store — the SoHo restaurant from Catch Hospitality Group (Tilman Fertitta, a 50% stakeholder in the group since 2017, and Eugene Remm, who co-founded the predecessor company with Mark Birnbaum in 2011) — opened in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood at 475 West Broadway in September 2024 and quickly became known as a hard-to-book reservation, drawing celebrity visits including Taylor Swift.

The Corner Store is set to open its first location outside New York, at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas, on November 9, 2026, in a deal announced in partnership with MGM Resorts (which operates, but does not own the real estate of, The Cosmopolitan). Reservations opened October 2, 2026, bookable via the Cosmopolitan's website or by phone at (702) 675-9828. The restaurant will serve dinner seven nights a week: Sunday–Thursday 5 p.m.–10 p.m., Friday–Saturday 5 p.m.–late.

Per the restaurant's own announcement, the Las Vegas menu carries over SoHo signatures — Lobster & Caviar Rolls, Five Cheese Pizza Rolls, and a Wagyu French Dip — and adds a Las Vegas-exclusive "Butcher's Counter" steak program featuring a Rosewood Ranch Wagyu Tomahawk and Wagyu Porterhouse, plus a Vegas-only Dirty Martini variant alongside the carried-over Tomato Martini and Sour Cream & Onion Martini. Rockwell Group, which designed the original SoHo space, also designed the Las Vegas location, which takes over the former Blue Ribbon American Grill & Oyster Bar space on the Cosmopolitan's third floor and includes a Martini Room and a train-car-inspired dining room.

Catch Hospitality Group and MGM Resorts have an existing relationship dating to 2018 (Catch at ARIA Las Vegas); this Corner Store deal is described by the companies as an expansion of that partnership, not a new corporate relationship.

**As of October 21, 2026, the Las Vegas location has not yet opened — it is an announced, scheduled opening, not a completed one.**

Hard constraints that shaped all copy below: never say Vegas "opened"/"is open" (future tense only); "first expansion outside NYC" scoped to the Corner Store brand only (Catch already runs Catch at ARIA since 2018); no unverified "hardest reservation" superlative; never "West Coast debut"; both Eugene Remm and Mark Birnbaum named if founders are named.

## 2. Entrepreneurial Angle

**"Brand System Replication"** — The Vegas expansion isn't about copying a restaurant, it's about replicating a system: a fixed, non-negotiable core (signature dishes, Rockwell Group design, service identity) plus deliberate, limited localization (Vegas-only Butcher's Counter steak program, Vegas-only Dirty Martini). Lesson: scalable brands run on a fixed core + deliberate limited localization — not improvisation, not rigid copy-paste.

The Corner Store is bringing its SoHo hit to Las Vegas — but it's not copying the restaurant. It's replicating a system.

Set to open November 9, 2026, inside The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas, the new location keeps the non-negotiables: the Lobster & Caviar Rolls, the Five Cheese Pizza Rolls, the Wagyu French Dip, and the Rockwell Group design that helped make the SoHo original (475 West Broadway, opened September 2024) one of the toughest reservations in SoHo — drawing visits from celebrities including Taylor Swift.

This is the brand's first location outside New York — announced in partnership with MGM Resorts, extending a relationship between Catch Hospitality Group and MGM that dates back to 2018's Catch at ARIA. Catch Hospitality Group traces back to a predecessor company co-founded by Eugene Remm and Mark Birnbaum in 2011; Tilman Fertitta has been a 50% stakeholder in the group since 2017.

The lesson for anyone scaling a brand: don't improvise everything, and don't copy-paste everything. Fix the core. Flex the rest — deliberately.
