### FINAL CAPTION

ElevenLabs' valuation doubled in eight months — $11B to $22B, via a $300M employee tender offer closed September 30, 2026, led by first-time investors Wellington and T. Rowe Price, with Goldman Sachs, GIC, EQT, OTPP, Sapphire Ventures, and BDT & MSD also joining. (This is ElevenLabs' second employee tender, after a $100M tender at $6.6B last September.)

But a tender offer is a secondary sale, not a funding round — existing shareholders selling shares to investors, no new capital onto the balance sheet. The number that actually moved first, and faster, was usage and revenue. ElevenLabs says ElevenAgents now handles 15M+ conversations a week (roughly 3x its February pace), and that ElevenAgents ARR has more than tripled over the same period. Company-reported, not independently audited.

Valuation is downstream. Usage and revenue velocity lead it — not the other way around.

**Save this post.** Then open your own deck: are you leading with a valuation-style vanity number, or with usage, retention, and repeat revenue? Start tracking the number that moves first.
