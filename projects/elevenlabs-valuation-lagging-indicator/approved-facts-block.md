# Approved Facts Block — ElevenLabs $300M Tender Offer / $22B Valuation

**Status:** Cleared with qualifications (Fact Verifier, 2026-10-01)

## Verbatim approved facts

ElevenLabs closed a $300 million employee tender offer on September 30, 2026, setting a new $22 billion valuation for the AI voice-agent company — double the $11 billion valuation set at its Series D round in February 2026. A tender offer is a secondary sale: existing shareholders (chiefly employees) sell shares to investors, and no new capital goes onto the company's balance sheet — unlike a primary funding round. The tender was led by first-time investors Wellington and T. Rowe Price, with additional new investors Goldman Sachs, GIC, EQT, OTPP, Sapphire Ventures, and BDT & MSD. Existing investors Andreessen Horowitz, Lightspeed, ICONIQ, D.E. Shaw, Evantic, Disruptive, and Alkeon also participated. This was ElevenLabs' second employee tender offer, following a $100 million tender at a $6.6 billion valuation in September 2025. ElevenLabs says its ElevenAgents product now handles more than 15 million conversations per week — roughly triple its pace in February 2026 — and that ElevenAgents ARR has more than tripled over the same period (company-reported figures, not independently audited).
