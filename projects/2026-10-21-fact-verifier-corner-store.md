## Claims That Must Be Removed or Softened

1. Remove/replace "late 2024" for the SoHo opening — use "September 2024."
2. Remove any phrasing implying this is "Catch Hospitality Group's first expansion outside NYC" — it is specifically **The Corner Store brand's** first location outside New York; Catch Hospitality Group already operates outside NYC (including at MGM's own ARIA since 2018).
3. Soften "the hardest reservation in SoHo" to an attributed/hedged superlative ("one of the hardest reservations," "widely reported as one of SoHo's toughest tables") — no independent ranking data confirms the absolute claim.
4. Do not state the restaurant "opened" or "is now open" in Las Vegas — as of Oct 21, 2026 it is **announced/scheduled** to open Nov 9, 2026, which is still in the future.
5. Do not call Las Vegas a "West Coast debut" — geographically inaccurate; this is press-kit language only.
6. If founders/owners are named, do not omit Mark Birnbaum; "Fertitta and Remm" alone is incomplete as a full-ownership statement (though accurate as the two names most directly tied to this specific Las Vegas announcement and quote).
7. Attribute the "Butcher's Counter," Wagyu Tomahawk/Porterhouse, and other menu specifics as sourced to the restaurant's/MGM's own announcement, not as independently verified editorial reporting.

## Missing Context That Could Materially Change Interpretation

- Catch Hospitality Group and MGM Resorts have an **existing partnership dating to 2018** (Catch at ARIA). This is not a brand-new relationship between the two companies — only The Corner Store concept itself is new to Vegas/MGM. Framing this as an entirely new corporate relationship would be misleading.
- Fertitta's role is as a **50% stakeholder since June 2017** in the parent company (then EMM Group, now operating as Catch Hospitality Group), not as an original founder of the Corner Store concept or of Catch itself. Remm and Birnbaum (with Michael Hirtenstein) founded the original company in 2011.
- The Cosmopolitan's real estate is not MGM-owned; MGM operates it under a long-term lease from a Blackstone/Stonepeak/Cherng Family Trust ownership group. This doesn't affect the Corner Store story directly but matters if any copy implies MGM "owns" the Cosmopolitan.
- The Corner Store location takes over the former **Blue Ribbon American Grill & Oyster Bar** space (third floor, Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas), which closed to make way for it — useful, verifiable color detail if the Strategist wants a "replacing X" angle.

## Approved Facts Block (verbatim for downstream use)

> The Corner Store — the SoHo restaurant from Catch Hospitality Group (Tilman Fertitta, a 50% stakeholder in the group since 2017, and Eugene Remm, who co-founded the predecessor company with Mark Birnbaum in 2011) — opened in Manhattan's SoHo neighborhood at 475 West Broadway in **September 2024** and quickly became known as a hard-to-book reservation, drawing celebrity visits including Taylor Swift.
>
> The Corner Store is set to open **its first location outside New York, at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas, on November 9, 2026**, in a deal announced in partnership with MGM Resorts (which operates, but does not own the real estate of, The Cosmopolitan). Reservations opened **October 2, 2026**, bookable via the Cosmopolitan's website or by phone at (702) 675-9828. The restaurant will serve dinner seven nights a week: Sunday–Thursday 5 p.m.–10 p.m., Friday–Saturday 5 p.m.–late.
>
> Per the restaurant's own announcement, the Las Vegas menu carries over SoHo signatures — Lobster & Caviar Rolls, Five Cheese Pizza Rolls, and a Wagyu French Dip — and adds a Las Vegas-exclusive **"Butcher's Counter"** steak program featuring a Rosewood Ranch Wagyu Tomahawk and Wagyu Porterhouse, plus a Vegas-only Dirty Martini variant alongside the carried-over Tomato Martini and Sour Cream & Onion Martini. Rockwell Group, which designed the original SoHo space, also designed the Las Vegas location, which takes over the former Blue Ribbon American Grill & Oyster Bar space on the Cosmopolitan's third floor and includes a Martini Room and a train-car-inspired dining room.
>
> Catch Hospitality Group and MGM Resorts have an existing relationship dating to 2018 (Catch at ARIA Las Vegas); this Corner Store deal is described by the companies as an expansion of that partnership, not a new corporate relationship.
>
> **As of October 21, 2026, the Las Vegas location has not yet opened — it is an announced, scheduled opening, not a completed one.**
