# Visual Design Brief — "The Deal Isn't the Money. It's the Control." (7-slide carousel)

Status: DRAFT MODE — no images generated yet (no image-generation API key available in this cloud environment). All content below is ready for `/justpreneur-finish` to execute later.

Story: TSG Consumer Partners' undisclosed strategic-stake investment in Los Tacos No. 1 (Chelsea Market-founded NYC taco counter, est. 2013), and why the real variable to watch is operational continuity (who stays in the room), not the check size.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a business-editorial deconstruction in the register of Bloomberg Businessweek or Eater's business desk — never a foodie/tabloid post and never a generic motivational template. The visual spine is a single recurring device: **the stainless-steel counter / kitchen pass-through**, shot as a premium editorial still, standing in for "who's actually running the room." That device opens warm and populated (the cover), turns into an abstract "ownership stake" motif for the deal mechanics (Slides 2, 6), grounds the origin story in Chelsea Market's market-hall architecture (Slide 3), stages a quiet two-figure continuity moment at the same counter (Slide 4), becomes an editorial city-map infographic for scale (Slide 5), and returns to the counter, now in daylight, for the sign-off (Slide 7). Street-food subject matter (steel counters, market-hall light, grill glow) is contrasted against finance-editorial motifs (redacted ledger lines, negotiation-table documents, a stake/ownership diagram) — kept restrained and dignified, not mocking either side.

No logos, no readable "Los Tacos No. 1" signage, no TSG Consumer Partners wordmark, and no photorealistic depictions of the named real individuals (Christian Pineda, Tyler Sanders, Kyle Cameron, Jacobo Ackerman) — all human presence in the generated images is anonymized/silhouetted or generic, since we have no verified likeness rights and do not want to fabricate faces attributed to real people.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: one dominant graphic/photographic device placed to leave a large clean negative-space zone for headline + body copy — text is added in layout, never baked into the AI-generated image. Hierarchy per slide, top to bottom:
1. Small kicker/section label (e.g., "THE DEAL," "THE FOUNDERS") — top margin, gold, all-caps, letter-spaced (Slide 1 cover and Slide 7 CTA use no kicker; see Slide 3 below)
2. Headline/body copy block — upper-to-mid frame
3. Graphic/photographic focal device — lower half, or as a full-bleed background field behind the copy block
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, identical position across all 7 slides (bottom-center)

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface: a bold, high-contrast editorial serif (Tiempos Headline / Publico-style) for Slide 1's headline — signals "premium business media," not a generic Instagram template.
- Body copy typeface: a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style) for all body paragraphs on Slides 2–7 — maximizes mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Kickers ("THE DEAL," "THE FOUNDERS," "WHO STAYS IN THE ROOM," "THE NUMBERS," "THE TAKEAWAY"): sans, all-caps, letter-spaced (~150–200 tracking), small size (~28–32px equivalent), gold or warm-white. Slides 1 and 7 use no kicker (cover/sign-off bookends).
- Headline sizing on Slide 1: large enough to be legible at thumbnail size on a phone — no line should exceed ~34 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on a 1080-wide canvas. Given the headline runs two sentences, break the line naturally after "Money." for a two-line stack.
- Body copy sizing (Slides 2–7): minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (Slides 2–6) or centered (Slides 1, 7) for readability.
- Slide 4's two continuity facts and Slide 5's four facts should each start on their own line/paragraph break (not run together) so the "who/what" scanning is instant on mobile.
- Slide 5's citation line ("per Time Out New York and NPR's The Indicator from Planet Money") should be set at the same body size as the rest of the paragraph — not shrunk into a tiny footnote — since it is approved copy, not a production mark.
- Slide 7's "Save this…" line and "Follow @justpreneur…" line should be visually separated (a line break or slightly larger gap) so each reads as a distinct action cue.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A) — dominant background/shadow tone across all seven slides for consistency
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — stainless-counter surfaces, ledger/term-sheet paper tone, market-hall structural elements
- Warm white (#F5F1E8/#FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds, paper highlight tone, market-hall skylight glow
- Energy gold (#C9A24B/#D4AF37) — kickers, accents, the pin-markers on Slide 5's map, the circled "control" clause on Slide 6, wordmark
- A single sparing warm accent — grill-char amber (#B5651D range) — reserved for the service-line/grill glow on Slides 1, 4, and 7 only, to nod to the street-food subject without turning the palette tabloid-warm; never used for text
- No red, no bright saturated color anywhere — this is a calm, continuity-focused story, not a collapse story, so the palette stays warmer and steadier than a "deal fell apart" post

## 6. Image treatment

- All background graphics/photos: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm gold/amber highlight pops, consistent color grade across all seven slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying graphic — a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, or real identifiable faces should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated backgrounds supply mood, motif, and negative space only.
- No depiction of "Los Tacos No. 1," "Los Mariscos," or "TSG Consumer Partners" signage, wordmarks, or logos in any image — use only the generic/abstract devices described above (stainless counter, market-hall architecture, redacted ledger, negotiation table, abstract city map).
- No photorealistic rendering of Christian Pineda, Tyler Sanders, Kyle Cameron, or Jacobo Ackerman — any human presence is anonymized (silhouette, backlit, empty stools) rather than a specific likeness.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge (left/right/top/bottom) at minimum; Instagram's UI (profile row, caption, like/comment icons) can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px on the feed preview — keep headline/kicker anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~860px wide (leaving ≥110px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080x1350) across all seven slides.
- Identical kicker style/position (top-left, gold, small-caps, ~150–200 tracking) on Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 use the centered cover/sign-off treatment (no kicker) as bookends.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center) on every slide for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe; Slide 7's wordmark may step up slightly in size as the sign-off.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/steel/gold/warm-white consistent, amber reserved for counter-glow slides) — no slide should read cooler/harsher than the others; this is a "watch closely" story, not a "collapse" story.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 use centered alignment as cover/sign-off bookends.
- The stainless-steel counter device recurs on Slides 1, 4, and 7 (night → still/backlit → daylight) so the carousel visually bookends and the continuity theme reads even at a glance.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text on every slide, especially Slide 5 where the map graphic has more visual texture.
- Avoid placing gold (#C9A24B) text directly on light/warm-white areas (e.g., the ledger-paper motif on Slides 2 and 6) — gold is reserved for kickers/accents/pin-markers on dark navy/steel backgrounds only, where contrast is strong.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas (roughly 14–16pt equivalent at full-size phone viewing) to remain legible when scaled down to feed thumbnail size.
- Slide 5's citation line must remain the same size and contrast as the rest of the body paragraph — do not shrink it to the point of illegibility on mobile.
