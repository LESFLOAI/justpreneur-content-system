## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, cleared with qualifications by Fact Verifier)

Los Tacos No. 1, the Tijuana-style taco concept that began as a single stand with no seating inside Chelsea Market, announced on September 3, 2026 that it had received a strategic investment from TSG Consumer (TSG Consumer Partners), a private-equity firm specializing in consumer brands with roughly $14 billion in assets under management. Financial terms of the deal — including any dollar amount or the size of the stake TSG acquired — were not disclosed by either party. Cofounder and CEO Christian Pineda will continue to lead day-to-day operations and strategy; the companies' announcement also names Jacobo Ackerman as continuing in his role, reported as "chief operating partner." Los Tacos No. 1 was founded in 2013 by three cofounders — Christian Pineda (a Tijuana native) along with Tyler Sanders and Kyle Cameron (both from Brawley, California) — starting as a stand-up taco counter in Chelsea Market. As of the September 2026 announcement, the company operates 10 locations across New York City, plus a sister seafood concept, Los Mariscos, also in Chelsea Market, serving Baja-style fish tacos, shrimp tacos, and ceviche. The company opened a Financial District (NYC) location in February 2025. Per the companies, the new investment is intended to fund expansion "beyond New York," with no specific new markets or timeline disclosed. Ropes & Gray LLP served as legal counsel to TSG Consumer Partners on the transaction. Following the announcement, some customers and fans expressed wariness on social media about whether private-equity ownership would change the brand, a reaction covered by outlets including Time Out New York and NPR's "The Indicator from Planet Money" — this is a reported public reaction, not a confirmed operational change, and no price increases, recipe changes, or quality changes have been confirmed as of this writing.

**Note on sourcing limits:** The Fact Verifier could not directly load several primary pages (tsgconsumer.com, qsrmagazine.com, ropesgray.com, lostacos1.com) due to network egress restrictions, and relied on cross-source triangulation instead. Ackerman's exact title and the precise current location count should get a final primary-source check if the environment allows before this goes to press. "Capital Digest" was excluded as an unverifiable source; no quotes from it were used anywhere in this package.
