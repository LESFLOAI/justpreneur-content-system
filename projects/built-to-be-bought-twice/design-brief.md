## 1. Overall creative concept

"The Exit Blueprint" — a premium, architectural/schematic motif. Picture a technical blueprint or ledger diagram rendered in gold line-work on a deep navy field: thin connecting lines, node points, and a single recurring **abstract authority mark** (a simple geometric seal/badge shape — think a minimal hexagonal or circular emblem, never a real logo) that appears in both the Exit #1 and Exit #2 panels. That repeated mark is the visual metaphor for the copy's core claim: "the one thing he never changed." The rest of each slide's composition (grid lines, connector lines, panel frames) borrows from editorial data-journalism / deal-memo aesthetics — think Puck, The Information, or a sharp M&A one-pager — not startup-meme energy and not a generic "hustle quote" template.

Slides 2 and 5 (the two exits) are built as literal **two-panel diptychs**: a left "before" panel and a right "after" panel joined by a thin gold connector line/arrow, each panel carrying the same abstract authority mark so the eye registers "same shape, different stage" without needing real logos. The remaining slides (1, 3, 4, 6, 7) are single-panel but keep the same blueprint grid language so the set reads as one continuous schematic, not seven unrelated templates.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

Every slide uses the same three-band system, consistent with JustPreneur brand convention:
1. **Headline/kicker band** (top ~28%) — largest, highest-contrast text; read first on a phone thumbnail.
2. **Image stage** (middle ~55%) — the generated blueprint/schematic artwork, always with a deliberate negative-space zone sized for the slide's specific text block.
3. **Brand footer band** (bottom ~15%) — JustPreneur wordmark lockup, consistent position, never competing with the headline.

Focal hierarchy on every slide: (1) headline, (2) the one key fact/line that slide exists to deliver, (3) the blueprint/schematic image mood, (4) JustPreneur mark last. No slide should ask the eye to do more than look at two things.

- JustPreneur wordmark: bottom-center, baseline at y≈1290px, consistent size (~34–40px cap height), consistent warm-white or gold color across all 7 slides. No tagline, no handle, no slide number, no date stamp.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Headlines/kickers:** a bold, slightly condensed grotesque/sans (Neue Haas/Druk/Archivo Black family) throughout — no decorative or script fonts anywhere in this set; the "blueprint/deal-memo" tone calls for precision, not flourish.
- **Body/supporting copy:** clean humanist sans (Inter, Söhne, or similar), warm white, generous line height (1.3–1.4x), never smaller than ~34px effective size at this resolution so it reads on a phone thumbnail.
- **Client names (Slide 4) and panel labels (Slides 2, 5):** set in the same bold sans as headlines, not a separate "logo-style" font — these are company/brand *names as words*, deliberately typographic rather than mimicking any real brand's visual identity.
- **Hierarchy discipline:** maximum two typefaces per slide, maximum three weight/size steps. No italics on headlines.

## 5. Color palette

JustPreneur brand default palette, used consistently across all 7 slides (no story-specific departure needed here — this is a straightforward business/strategy story, not a cultural-aesthetic one):

- Deep navy base: `#0A0E14`
- Steel gray (secondary text, grid lines, dividers): `#6B7280`
- Warm white (primary body text, panel backgrounds in schematic art): `#F5F1E8`
- Energy gold (kickers, the recurring authority mark, connector lines/arrows, JustPreneur wordmark): `#D4AF37`

No red, teal, or other accent hues — this story's visual job is to feel authoritative and consistent (echoing the "narrow, not broad" thesis in the palette itself: fewer colors, used precisely).

## 6. Image treatment

Clean, premium, editorial-technical mood — think a sharp M&A deal memo or architecture blueprint, not a photo-real business scene and not a cluttered infographic. Thin, precise gold line-work (1–2px equivalent weight) on deep navy fields, with warm-white panel/card shapes for contrast. Minimal grain, no fog/haze, no dramatic cinematic lighting — this story is calm, disciplined, and exact, and the imagery should feel the same way. No photoreal human faces, no real logos, no legible numbers/prices, no readable fine print. Every "panel" or "card" shape in the artwork stays blank/abstract so all real wording is added cleanly in layout.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

For all 7 slides (1080 x 1350):
- Side margins: keep all text and critical graphic elements at least 80px from the left/right edges.
- Top margin: keep headline/kicker baseline no higher than 90px from the top edge; nothing critical in the top ~40px (feed-preview crop risk).
- Bottom margin: keep the JustPreneur wordmark and any text at least 100px from the bottom edge; nothing critical in the bottom ~60px.
- Center-safe zone: the primary message of each slide should read correctly even if a viewer only sees the middle 80% of the frame.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Same 3-band grid (headline/kicker band, image stage, footer band) on all 7 slides — never swap layouts mid-carousel.
- Same JustPreneur wordmark size, color, and bottom-center position on all 7 slides.
- Same kicker treatment (gold, caps, top-left) on Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 are the only centered/no-kicker-label slides (cover and final takeaway), an intentional bookend.
- The abstract authority mark (the recurring geometric seal/badge shape) must appear identically on both panels of Slide 2 and both panels of Slide 5, and may recur in reduced form on Slides 3, 4, and 6 to reinforce continuity — it should never change shape, only scale/position.
- Blueprint grid line-weight, color (`#D4AF37` on `#0A0E14`), and spacing must stay visually consistent across all 7 slides so the set reads as one continuous schematic, not seven unrelated templates.
- No slide may introduce a new decorative motif (new icon style, new border treatment, new color) not already established in Sections 5–6.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Primary body text (`#F5F1E8` warm white) on deep navy (`#0A0E14`) background: contrast ratio ≈ 16:1 — passes WCAG AAA for all text sizes.
- Gold (`#D4AF37`) kickers/labels on navy background: contrast ratio ≈ 7.5:1 — passes AA for normal text and AAA for large text; gold is restricted to short kicker labels, panel labels, and the final takeaway's lead line (never dense body paragraphs) to stay safely in the large-text contrast band.
- Steel gray (`#6B7280`) is used only for grid lines/dividers and secondary micro-labels, never for primary body copy, since its contrast on navy is lower.
- Minimum effective text size kept large enough to read in a 3-inch-wide phone feed thumbnail (no text block should require pinch-zoom).
- No flashing/strobing or high-frequency pattern effects in any prompt (fine grid lines only, no moiré-risk textures).
