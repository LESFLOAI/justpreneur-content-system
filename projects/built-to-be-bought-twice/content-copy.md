## Final Caption

Brendan Gahan has sold a company before. Epic Signal went to Mekanism.

Instead of chasing scale the second time, he went narrower. He built Creator Authority — a B2B creator-marketing agency with a short, specific client list: Amazon, Adobe, IBM, HubSpot.

This year, Creator Authority was acquired by BDB Group in an undisclosed, multimillion-dollar, all-cash deal with an earnout.

Two exits. Same move both times: narrow instead of broad, credentialed instead of generic.

This isn't a story about a fast flip — it's years of positioning that eventually became impossible to ignore.

If you want future options — an acquisition, a partnership, a seat at a bigger table — start by auditing your own positioning. Undifferentiated is hard to value. Specific is easy to want.
