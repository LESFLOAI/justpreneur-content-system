## 6. APPROVED FACTS BLOCK (verbatim-safe for downstream use)

> Thomas Straker is a British chef who built a large following on TikTok and Instagram during the COVID-19 pandemic through his "All Things Butter" cooking videos, and later opened a restaurant called Straker's on Golborne Road in London's Notting Hill area in 2022. In April 2025, Straker announced on Instagram that he had "secured the keys" to a new restaurant space at 59 Grand Street in SoHo, New York — the former home of Lucky Strike, the French bistro Keith McNally (also known for Balthazar, Minetta Tavern, and Pastis) ran from 1989 until its forced permanent closure in April 2020 during the pandemic. The space sat vacant for roughly five years before Straker's lease. On September 10, 2026 — the first day of New York Fashion Week — Net-a-Porter CEO Heather Kaminetsky hosted an "Incredible Women" cocktail event at the still-not-yet-officially-open Straker's NYC, honoring Cindy Crawford, PORTER magazine's September 2026 cover star. Press coverage of that event described the restaurant's official public debut as coming "later this month" (September 2026). As of this report (October 12, 2026), no source could be found confirming that Straker's NYC has since opened to the public, nor any update on its status — this should be treated as unresolved and checked fresh before publication. Reported guests at the September 10 event, corroborated across multiple outlets, include Serena Williams, Katie Holmes, Emmy Rossum, Nara Smith, Aurora James, Willy Chavarria, Wes Gordon, and Christopher John Rogers; some outlets additionally reported designer Joseph Altuzarra and stylist Kate Young, while others instead named Athena Calderone — this discrepancy is unresolved and should not be stated as settled fact. Straker's social following has been reported in the range of roughly 1.5 to 2.7 million across TikTok and Instagram in press coverage from 2022 through 2026; an aggregate "billions of views" claim for his butter-video content appears in press coverage (including The Spectator) but is not a platform-verified statistic and should be attributed as such. In July 2023, Straker's London restaurant faced public criticism after he posted a photo of an all-white, all-male kitchen team; he initially responded dismissively, drew further criticism for that response, and then apologized and said he would work to improve diversity in hiring. This episode belongs to the London restaurant's history, not the New York opening.
