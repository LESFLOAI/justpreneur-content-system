## 4. Final Instagram Caption

Thomas Straker secured the keys to a SoHo space in April 2025 — the former home of Keith McNally's Lucky Strike, empty for about five years after closing in 2020. The restaurant still hasn't officially opened. And it already had one of the buzziest nights of New York Fashion Week.

On September 10, 2026, Net-a-Porter's CEO hosted an "Incredible Women" event inside Straker's NYC, honoring Cindy Crawford. Serena Williams, Katie Holmes, Emmy Rossum, Nara Smith, Aurora James, and more reportedly came through. Press covered it like an opening. It wasn't one.

Straker built his following (reportedly 1.5-2.7 million across TikTok and Instagram) on butter videos during the pandemic — the kind of content press has described as racking up "billions of views," though that figure comes from coverage, not a verified platform stat. That audience, plus a room someone else was already filling with press and guests, did more pre-launch work than any party Straker's could have thrown on its own.

The lesson isn't "host a great event." It's: find the room that's already full of your future customers, and get invited in before you launch.

One honest note — as of this post, Straker's NYC's public opening still isn't confirmed. Worth checking before you make a reservation.

Save this if you're building toward a launch of your own.
