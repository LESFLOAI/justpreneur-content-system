## Required Corrections (ranked by severity)

### 1. (Moderate) Reserve Padel club count overstates footprint by implying a 7th club

**Location:** Slide 3 body copy — appears identically in three places: the approved copy block, Design Brief Section 3 ("Slide 3"), and Design Brief Section 11 (verbatim layout text).

**Current text:** *"Reserve Padel: six clubs across Manhattan and Miami, plus one 'opening soon' in Palm Beach."*

**Problem:** The Approved Facts Block states Reserve Padel operates **six clubs total**: two in Manhattan, three in the Miami area, and **one** "opening soon" in Palm Beach County (2 + 3 + 1 = 6). The phrase "six clubs across Manhattan and Miami, *plus* one ... in Palm Beach" reads as six already across Manhattan/Miami **plus an additional** Palm Beach club — i.e., seven total. This is exactly the overstatement the Fact Verifier explicitly warned against in its own "Claims That Must Be Removed or Softened" section: *"SOFTEN: Reserve Padel's '6 clubs' — note that one of the six (Palm Beach County) is 'opening soon,' not yet operational, so '6 open clubs' would overstate current footprint."* The hedge word "opening soon" is present, but the surrounding arithmetic still inflates the count.

**Required fix (apply in all three locations):** Change to something like: *"Reserve Padel: five clubs across Manhattan and Miami, plus one 'opening soon' in Palm Beach"* (5 + 1 = 6, matches facts), or equivalently *"Reserve Padel: six clubs total across Manhattan, Miami, and Palm Beach — one of them (Palm Beach) still 'opening soon.'"* Either phrasing keeps the total at six and keeps the "opening soon" hedge intact without implying a seventh location.

**Note:** The caption does not mention Reserve Padel at all, so this error does not appear there — correction is scoped to Slide 3 copy and the two design-brief echoes of it.
