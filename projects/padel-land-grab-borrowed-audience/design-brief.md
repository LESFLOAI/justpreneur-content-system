## 1. Overall creative concept

This is a strategy-pattern story, not a padel trend piece and not a luxury lifestyle post. The visual job is to show one repeatable move — an existing brand redirecting an audience it already owns into a hot adjacent category — playing out at two completely different scales (a mass-market Colombian gym chain and an ultra-luxury NYC streetwear label), while a sea of venture-funded padel startups raises real money around them. Treat it like a business-strategy desk breakdown (Puck / The Information / Business of Fashion feel): disciplined navy/steel/gold palette, confident editorial type, premium architectural photography. No padel-brand logos, no cartoon court icons, no real-person likenesses, no implied dollar signs baked into imagery.

The carousel has three visual movements:
- **Slides 1–2 (The Pattern Named, Then Shown at Scale 1):** the cover states the thesis; Slide 2 shows it at mass-market scale — a gym environment with a padel court glowing just beyond it.
- **Slides 3–5 (The Market Context, Then the Pattern Shown at Scale 2, Then the Explicit Contrast):** Slide 3 situates the reader in a genuinely hot, capital-flush category; Slide 4 shows the same pattern at ultra-luxury scale; Slide 5 puts both scales in one frame, split down the middle.
- **Slides 6–7 (The Universal Takeaway + the CTA):** a quiet, resolved architectural image carrying the insight, then an inviting, calm closing court image that holds space for the audit prompt.

A single recurring graphic device ties all seven slides together: a thin horizontal gold line along the bottom edge of every slide (echoing a padel court sideline), carrying a small gold directional chevron/arrow motif that tracks the "redirected audience" idea — never literal icons (no dumbbell, no hanger, no racket symbol), just a clean geometric line-and-chevron mark:
- Slide 1: one gold chevron enters from the left edge of the line and bends inward toward center — the thesis arriving.
- Slide 2: the chevron sits bent at left-of-center, pointing rightward into the gym/court scene (the gym-chain case).
- Slide 3: the single line fans into three to four thin parallel chevrons moving steadily left to right (capital flowing into the category) — one of these chevrons is rendered visibly dimmer/paused partway, not yet reaching the end of the line (the Reserve Padel Palm Beach "opening soon" hedge, shown honestly as unfinished, never as arrived).
- Slide 4: a second gold chevron sits bent at right-of-center, pointing leftward into the luxury-club scene (the streetwear case), mirrored in weight and brightness to Slide 2's chevron — neither case is visually favored.
- Slide 5: both chevrons (from Slides 2 and 4) appear together, meeting at a vertical center line that splits the frame — visual proof the same move happens at both scales.
- Slide 6: the two chevrons simplify and merge into a single resolved gold line with no bend — the pattern, stated plainly.
- Slide 7: the same single resolved gold line now extends calmly toward the right edge of the frame, into open space — an invitation, not a conclusion.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

Every slide follows the same top-to-bottom hierarchy:
1. Headline, kicker, or body-copy block — upper portion of frame
2. Photographic focal environment — lower half to two-thirds of frame
3. Thin gold line + chevron graphic — fixed bottom-edge band, state advancing per slide as described above
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, fixed bottom-center position, identical placement across all seven slides, sitting just above the gold line/chevron band

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline/statement typeface (Slides 1, 6, 7): a bold, high-contrast editorial serif with a business-press feel (Tiempos Headline / Canela / Freight Display style) — premium strategy journalism, not a motivational template.
- Kicker (Slide 1): a lighter-weight small-caps or reduced-size sans, clearly secondary to the headline, set directly above it.
- Body copy typeface (Slides 2–5): a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne / Inter / Neue Haas style) for maximum mobile legibility, especially given the fact-dense sentences on Slides 2, 3, and 4.
- No slide numbers, no date tags, no citation marks, no small corner labels added as separate text anywhere — all facts, dates, dollar figures, and sourcing phrases ("first reported by Feed Me, corroborated by multiple outlets," "at least... disclosed," "opening soon") appear only within the approved copy sentences themselves, rendered at full body-copy size and weight — never shrunk, italicized-away, or demoted to a small label.
- Headline sizing (Slide 1): no line should exceed ~38–42 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~60px effective headline size on the 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing (Slides 2–5): minimum ~38–42px effective size at 1080 width, line-height ~130–140%, left-aligned (never justified). Slides 2, 3, and 4 carry the longest sentences in the set — give these slides a heavier contrast overlay and the most generous vertical copy allowance rather than shrinking type below the minimum. Hedge phrases ("reportedly," "first reported by," "at least... disclosed," "opening soon") must render in the exact same weight and size as the surrounding sentence — never set smaller, lighter, italic-only, or visually de-emphasized in any way that could read as a disclaimer/footnote.
- Statement sizing (Slides 6, 7): target ~50–60px effective size, generous line-height (~135–145%), left-aligned or centered per slide.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A range) — dominant background/shadow tone, identical across all seven slides
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces (mall structure, club architecture, broadcast-style skyline glow)
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 / #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds
- Energy gold (#C9A24B–#D4AF37) — court floodlight glow, the bottom line/chevron graphic, the accent word "Redirected" on Slide 1, wordmark
- No red, no green, no pink, no padel-brand-specific colors, no additional accents — keep the palette disciplined so the mass-market and ultra-luxury cases read as structurally equal in treatment even though their environments differ in texture/mood.

## 6. Image treatment

- All imagery graded toward navy/steel/gold night-and-dusk lighting so every slide feels like one continuous visual world.
- Apply a subtle dark navy gradient overlay (~40–55% opacity) behind every text zone on every slide to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying photo, with a heavier overlay (~55–65%) behind the longest paragraphs on Slides 2, 3, and 4.
- No text, logos, brand names (Kith, Spin Padel, Padel Haus, Pro Padel League, Reserve Padel), real signage, scoreboard digits, dollar/number call-outs, or real identifiable faces generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy, all brand names, and all figures are added in a design tool afterward per this brief. Ronnie Fieg is referenced only through the approved copy text; never through a rendered likeness, silhouette styled to resemble him, or any human figure in Slide 4's environment.
- Slide 3's "one dim/unfinished court" detail is a deliberate, literal honoring of the Reserve Padel Palm Beach hedge ("opening soon" / not yet operational) — it must read as visibly unfinished/dark/scaffolded, never as lit, active, or indistinguishable from the other courts.
- Slide 5's split-frame must be built with genuinely matched camera height, lens, and overlay treatment on both halves so the only visual differences are environment and mood (busy/bright vs. sparse/moody) — not quality, polish, or favoritism.
- No depiction of financial charts, stock tickers, cash, or numeral-bearing graphics anywhere in the set — the capital/funding context on Slide 3 is conveyed only through the multi-court skyline environment and the chevron motif, never through rendered numbers or currency symbols.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of at least 96px from every edge. Instagram's feed UI can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px of the preview — keep headline/body anchor points starting no higher than ~150–180px from the top (or ~130px when a kicker precedes the headline, as on Slide 1), and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge (the gold line/chevron band itself can sit lower, in the ~1290–1310px band, since it is a thin graphic device rather than text).
- Keep body copy line length under ~900px wide (leaving ≥90px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading, with extra care on Slides 2, 3, and 4, where body copy runs longest and is fact-dense.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080 x 1350) on all seven slides.
- Identical thin gold line position along the bottom edge on every slide; chevron state advances in this exact order: one entering chevron bending to center (1) → bent chevron pointing right into the gym/court scene (2) → three to four parallel chevrons with one dimmed/paused (3) → bent chevron pointing left into the luxury-club scene, mirrored to Slide 2 (4) → both chevrons meeting at a vertical center divider (5) → chevrons simplified into one resolved straight line (6) → the same resolved line extending into open space on the right (7).
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center, just above the gold line/chevron band) on every slide, slightly more prominent only on the final Slide 7.
- Identical navy/steel/gold color grade across all seven backgrounds — no deliberate grade shift in this set, since the story is one continuous argument rather than a before/after narrative.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention on Slides 2–5; Slides 1, 6, and 7 share the headline/statement serif treatment.
- Slide 2's gym/court environment and Slide 4's luxury-club environment must be treated as a matched pair in lighting discipline and overlay weight (even though their textures differ by design) so neither case reads as the "real" or "more important" one — Slide 5's split-frame is the visual proof of this parity.
- No slide may include a rendered likeness of Ronnie Fieg or any other real named person; no slide may include real brand logos/signage, a scoreboard, or a rendered dollar figure/numeral.
- Slide 3's one dim/unfinished court must remain visually distinct from the other lit courts on every pass of this slide — it must never be revised to look open/active, per the Reserve Padel Palm Beach hedge.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) text over the navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, especially over the glowing mall/court glass on Slide 2 and the skyline glow on Slide 3.
- Gold (#C9A24B) is reserved for large display text (the word "Redirected" on Slide 1, the bottom line/chevron graphic, the wordmark) on dark backgrounds only — do not set gold body copy at small sizes, where contrast drops.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~38px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas; no hedge phrase, date, or dollar figure may be set smaller than the surrounding sentence.
- Slides 2, 3, and 4 carry the longest, most fact-dense paragraphs in the set (brand/venue/date detail; three-company funding recap with explicit hedges; venue/price/sourcing detail) — prioritize a slightly heavier gradient overlay behind text on these three slides, and confirm the overlay does not visually compress or shrink any hedge phrase relative to the rest of the sentence.
