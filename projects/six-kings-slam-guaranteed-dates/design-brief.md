## 1. Overall Creative Concept

This is a numbers-and-names story, not a photo story — the copy deck itself specifies clean typographic/text-card layouts. The visual concept is **"The Ledger"**: each slide behaves like a page from a premium financial/sports-business dossier — the kind of high-contrast, big-number editorial layout you'd see in a Bloomberg or Business of Sport feature, not a motivational quote template. Numbers and dates are treated as the hero visual element (oversized, tabular-style figures), with a recurring abstract court-line motif (not a literal tennis court photo, not any player) used as a quiet structural/background device to keep the "tennis" context legible without photography or likenesses.

No real logos, no player photos or photoreal portraits, no rendered Netflix/ATP/tournament marks. Attribution and brand names referenced only as plain text where the copy calls for it (e.g., "Netflix" and player names appear as typeset words, never as logos or faces).

## 2. Composition and Focal Hierarchy

Consistent across all 6 slides, in this priority order:
1. **Headline/key figure or date** — largest element, top-to-upper-middle third, within safe margins.
2. **Supporting copy** — mid-to-lower section, generous line spacing, left-aligned for scannability.
3. **Abstract court-line graphic device** — background/edge element only, never competing with text, always at reduced opacity (15–25%) or confined to a margin strip.
4. **JustPreneur wordmark** — fixed position every slide (see section 3), small but fully legible, never competing with headline.

No icons, no decorative illustration beyond the court-line motif, no clutter.

## 4. Typography Direction

- **Display/headline face:** a bold, condensed or semi-condensed geometric sans (e.g., something in the family of Söhne Breit, Neue Haas Grotesk Display Bold, or Helvetica Now Display Extra Bold) — confident, editorial, premium business-media register. Avoid rounded or "friendly" sans fonts; avoid script/serif decoration.
- **Numerals:** tabular/lining figures, set large and tight-tracked for dollar figures and dates so they read as data, not decoration.
- **Body/supporting copy face:** a clean grotesk at regular/medium weight (e.g., Inter, Neue Haas Grotesk Text) for all paragraph-length copy — optimized for legibility at small size.
- **Hierarchy via size and color weight, not multiple typefaces** — max two type families per slide (one display, one text).
- Minimum effective body-copy size at 1080px-wide export: no smaller than roughly 32px equivalent, so nothing drops below comfortable mobile-thumbnail legibility.

## 5. Color Palette

Brand default, used consistently across all 6 slides:
- **Deep navy** (#0B1B2B or similar) — primary background.
- **Warm white** (#F5F1E8 or similar) — primary text color for body copy and most headlines.
- **Energy gold** (approx #D4AF37–#E0A82E range) — accent color reserved for the single most important figure or phrase per slide ($13.5 Million, $1.5M, "Zero no-shows," "Sometimes certainty is the product," the date numerals).
- **Steel gray** (#6E7A85 or similar) — secondary/supporting text, tags, lead-in lines, the slide-6 closing caveat line.
- Gold is used sparingly — one hero emphasis per slide maximum — so it doesn't flatten the hierarchy by over-highlighting.

## 6. Image Treatment

No photography of players, venue, or any real person. No AI-generated photoreal faces or silhouettes resembling identifiable athletes. The only generated visual asset per slide is an **abstract court-line / ledger-grid background texture** — thin geometric lines suggesting a tennis court's boundary geometry and/or a financial-ledger grid, rendered as pure abstract line art, low-opacity, confined to backgrounds or margins so it never competes with or obscures text. Treated identically in style (same line weight, same restrained navy/gold palette) across all 6 slides for carousel consistency, with only the compositional emphasis (center vs. edge vs. corner) varying slide to slide.

## 7. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 px for all 6 slides.
- Keep all text and essential graphic elements inside: 72px left/right margins, 96px top margin, 120px bottom margin (this bottom zone also avoids the profile-row/caption-icon overlap on the feed UI).
- JustPreneur wordmark sits just inside the bottom safe margin, never clipped by Instagram's UI chrome.
- No essential content within 40px of any edge, ever — treat that as true bleed-only space for the background texture only.

## 8. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

- Identical canvas size, margin grid, color palette, and type families across all 6 slides.
- JustPreneur wordmark: same size, same position, same color treatment on every slide.
- Energy gold reserved for exactly one hero emphasis element per slide — never more, never zero (slide 1's "$13.5 Million" counts as its one).
- Abstract court-line/ledger motif present on every slide in the same rendering style, varying only in placement/crop so the carousel feels like one continuous dossier rather than six unrelated cards.
- No slide numbers, dates-of-posting, source citations, or footer marks on any slide — per copy deck instruction, that attribution lives only in the caption.
- Paragraph-style copy (slides 2, 4, 5, 6 supporting lines) uses the same left-aligned text block width and line-height across slides for a steady reading rhythm when swiping.

## 9. Accessibility and Contrast Check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) on deep navy (#0B1B2B): contrast ratio well above WCAG AA for large text (>7:1) — safe for all headline and body use.
- Energy gold (#D4AF37) on deep navy: contrast ratio comfortably above 4.5:1 at the large/bold weights specified here — acceptable for emphasis headline figures; avoid using gold for small supporting copy or thin-weight text, where contrast margin shrinks.
- Steel gray (#6E7A85) on deep navy: lower contrast by design (intentionally secondary) — restrict steel gray strictly to lead-in lines, tags, and the slide-6 caveat line, all set at a size no smaller than ~32px effective, and never for primary dollar figures or headlines.
- No text ever placed directly over the busiest part of the abstract line texture — texture opacity is capped at 25% specifically so any text overlay region remains high-contrast.
- All critical figures ($13.5M, $1.5M, $6M, $5M, ~$90M, 128 players, the three October dates) are rendered at headline/sub-headline type weight — never shrunk to fine-print size, per the content-constraint requirement that dollar figures and the structural caveat stay visually legible.
