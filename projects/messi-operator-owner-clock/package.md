Lionel Messi just retired from his national team. He still can't own part of the club he plays for.

On Aug. 31, 2026, Lionel Messi retired from Argentina's national team — that was international soccer. He has not retired from club play. He remains an active Inter Miami player, reportedly under contract through 2028.

So why no equity yet? Club officials say current MLS rules don't allow an active player to hold a stake in a club. Inter Miami's chief business officer, Xavier Asensi, told Marca in an October 2026 interview that Messi is expected to join the ownership group once he's no longer an active player — not before.

Not merit. Not money. Timing.

Every operator building toward equity hits a version of this rule: the clock for doing the work and the clock for owning the asset don't always run together — until a defined transition point kicks in.

If you're working toward equity inside something you currently operate: go find the actual trigger language in your deal right now. Don't wait until you try to collect to learn what it says.
