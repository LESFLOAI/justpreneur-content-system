## 1. Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block — Fact Verifier, status: Cleared with qualifications)

On October 2, 2026, Paramount Skydance Chairman and CEO David Ellison announced via a post on X that the company formed by Paramount's pending acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery — a deal with an **enterprise value** of roughly $110 billion (equity value ~$81B), first announced February 27, 2026 — will be named "Skydance" (legal name: Skydance Corporation) once the merger closes. The merger is **expected to close October 6, 2026**, following a September 21, 2026 settlement with a coalition of roughly a dozen state attorneys general (led by California AG Rob Bonta) and the Writers Guild of America, which U.S. District Judge Araceli Martínez-Olguín approved on September 30, 2026 (the settlement secured **behavioral remedies**, such as minimum film-release commitments, not the structural remedies originally sought). According to Ellison, Paramount and Warner Bros. will continue operating as distinct brands beneath the new Skydance parent name. At closing, the company's Class B shares are expected to move from Nasdaq (ticker PSKY) to the NYSE under the new ticker "SKYD," effective on or about market open October 6, 2026 — a separate, unrelated stock split for PSKY shares is scheduled for October 5, 2026. Ynon Kreiz, former Chairman and CEO of Mattel, was named Co-CEO alongside Ellison on or about September 30, 2026, effective at closing; Ellison leads strategy/creative/technology, Kreiz leads day-to-day operations/integration.

**As of this writing (October 2, 2026), the merger has not yet closed** — every outcome above is announced/expected, pending the October 6 close.
