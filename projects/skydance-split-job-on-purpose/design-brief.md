## 0. Hard constraints carried into every visual decision

These come directly from the approved copy and override general brand defaults wherever they conflict:

1. **Skydance must read as "pending close," never as an established, final brand.** The deal is expected to close Oct 6 — it has not closed yet. Every appearance of the word "Skydance" (beats 2, 5, 6) must be visually marked as provisional: grayed-out fill, a dashed/bracketed container, and/or a "PENDING CLOSE" stamp. No prompt or layout instruction may present a clean, confident, final "Skydance" logo lockup. This is a factual-accuracy constraint, not a style preference.
2. **No real logos.** Paramount, Warner Bros. Discovery, and Skydance are real, trademarked brands. No image-generation prompt asks for their actual logos, wordmarks, or brand-specific color systems. All company references in generated art use neutral, abstract corporate-emblem motifs (described per-beat below), never literal marks. The word "Skydance" itself is added later as a plain text layer in the design tool (per brand convention of not relying on AI to render exact text), not generated as a logo.
3. **No real-person likenesses.** David Ellison and Ynon Kreiz are named public figures. No prompt asks an image generator to render either man's face or a recognizable likeness. Any human presence in generated art is hands-only, silhouette, or fully abstracted icon-based — never a posed portrait. Their names and titles are added as text/lower-third graphics after generation, not baked into the image.
4. **Ynon Kreiz's Mattel reference is biographical, not visual.** "(ex-Chairman/CEO, Mattel)" is a credential line, added as text only. No Mattel or toy-brand imagery, logos, or toy objects appear anywhere in this piece — that would misrepresent his role at Skydance as toy-related.
5. **No invented org-chart titles or additional copy.** Only the nine approved beats are visualized. No slide counters, timestamps, citation marks, or "pending" badges on anything other than the Skydance reference itself.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a **business-news explainer**, visually closer to a Bloomberg/CNBC business-desk graphics package than a motivational template — befitting a story about deal structure and org design, not a lifestyle moment. The visual spine is a **split-screen device**: the frame divides into two vertical lanes that build independently (left = vision/strategy, right = operations/integration), then lock together at a center point where the provisional "Skydance" marker sits — bracketed, grayed, stamped "PENDING CLOSE." This single device carries the entire piece's logic: two distinct jobs, one shared (not-yet-finalized) company.

Two visual "lanes" recur with consistent color coding so the eye tracks "which job is this" without rereading text:
- **Vision Lane (left, gold-accented)** — strategy/creative/tech, built from compass, lightbulb, and circuit-node icon motifs on a warm navy-gold field.
- **Operations Lane (right, steel-accented)** — day-to-day operations/integration, built from gear, checklist, and interlocking-node icon motifs on a cooler navy-steel field.

Beats 7 and 8 (the lesson/takeaway) strip all of this away for a **text-only, high-contrast minimal treatment** — flat deep navy, no split, no icons — so the piece's thesis statement lands with maximum clarity after six beats of visual build-up. This contrast (built structure → stripped-down clarity) is itself the editorial device, echoing how business-news graphics "clear the board" before the analyst's takeaway line.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

- Full-bleed background visual per beat (animated graphic or Ken-Burns-treated still).
- A single line (or short two-line stack) of kinetic on-screen text per beat, placed in the **lower-third safe zone** for build/context beats, or **vertically centered** for the two punch/lesson beats (7, 8), matching JustPreneur's established Reel rhythm convention.
- Text sits on a dark navy scrim/gradient (~65–75% opacity, feathered, never a hard box) so it stays legible over the icon-built backgrounds without looking like a static template.
- JustPreneur wordmark appears small and steady in the **top-safe-zone corner** from beat 2 through beat 8 (consistent position/opacity, barely-there watermark presence), then appears large and centered only in beat 9's closing sting.
- Beats 3 and 4 additionally use a **broadcast-style lower-third name/title card** (name in bold warm white, title/role in gold caps beneath, on a short navy bar) — a deliberate nod to business-news chyron graphics, reinforcing the "editorial," not "meme," register the subject matter demands.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Kinetic body text (all beats):** bold grotesk/sans (the family already established across JustPreneur Reels — e.g., a Neue-Haas-Grotesk-Condensed-style weight), sentence case, warm white, centered or lower-third per §3.
- **Beat 1's "$110B" figure:** rendered as a larger, extra-bold numeral treatment within the same sentence, in energy gold, so the dollar figure reads as the visual anchor of the hook. QC correction: attach a small "(EV)" micro-label immediately after the figure (smaller type size, same gold color, e.g. "$110B(EV)") so the enterprise-value qualifier is visible in beat 1 itself, not delayed until beat 2's full "(enterprise value)" spell-out.
- **Broadcast-style name/title cards (beats 3, 4):** name in bold warm white (e.g., "David Ellison"), role/credential directly beneath in smaller gold all-caps letter-spaced type (e.g., "VISION, STRATEGY, CREATIVE, TECH" / "EX-CHAIRMAN/CEO, MATTEL — DAY-TO-DAY OPERATIONS & INTEGRATION"), set on a short solid or 80%-opacity navy bar for a clean, legible chyron look.
- **"SKYDANCE — PENDING CLOSE" marker (beats 2, 5, 6):** condensed grotesk, all-caps, set inside a dashed or bracketed container, fill at ~50–60% gray opacity against the navy background so it visibly reads as "not fully real yet" — never full-opacity warm white or gold, which would read as a confirmed, finished brand.
- **Beats 7–8 lesson lines:** same grotesk family, heaviest available weight, largest optical size used anywhere in the piece (these are the thesis statements — they should be the most dominant single lines in the whole Reel), warm white, centered.
- No script fonts, no decorative/legal-document lettering despite the "deal" subject matter — keep the editorial grotesk system consistent throughout; business-news graphics use clean sans type, not ornamental serif.

## 5. Color palette

**Brand base (unchanged):** deep navy, steel gray, warm white, energy gold — used for all text, wordmark, and chyron bars throughout.

**Story-specific coding layer (background art only):**
- **Vision Lane (beat 3 and left half of beats 1, 5):** warm navy-gold — gold icon linework and gold rim light on a navy field.
- **Operations Lane (beat 4 and right half of beats 1, 5):** cooler navy-steel — steel-gray icon linework on a slightly cooler navy field.
- **Pending-close gray:** a desaturated mid-gray (distinct from steel gray) reserved exclusively for the "SKYDANCE — PENDING CLOSE" marker, so it never gets confused with the Operations Lane's steel-gray coding.
- **Beats 7–8 (text-only):** flat deep navy only — no gold, no steel accents — to maximize contrast and signal "this is the takeaway, strip everything else away."
- **Beat 9 (end card):** navy field with a restrained gold accent behind/around the JustPreneur wordmark, matching the brand's standard sting treatment.

## 6. Image treatment

- Clean, flat-lit, graphic-design-forward treatment throughout (not photographic/cinematic) — this is a data/org-chart explainer, so backgrounds should read as premium motion-graphics plates, not lifestyle photography.
- Icon motifs (compass, lightbulb, circuit-nodes, gear, checklist, interlocking-nodes) rendered as simple, bold, line-based or softly-lit 3D-icon graphics — never cluttered, never more than 2–3 icons visible at once per lane.
- Abstract company marks (for Paramount/WBD references in beats 2 and 6) are generic geometric emblem shapes only — a simple abstract hexagon form for one side and a simple abstract rounded-square form for the other — chosen specifically to avoid echoing either company's actual trade-dress iconography (no peak/mountain or globe/ring motifs), with no resemblance to either company's actual logo, wordmark, or brand colors.
- No text, logos, or real identifiable people/faces generated inside any AI image — all copy, names, titles, and the "SKYDANCE — PENDING CLOSE" marker text are added in the design/motion tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated backgrounds supply motif, mood, and negative space only.
- No depiction of any Mattel/toy branding or imagery anywhere in the piece.

## 8. Slide-to-slide (beat-to-beat) consistency rules

- Every beat uses the same navy scrim treatment and the same grotesk type family — no font or scrim-style switching mid-piece.
- The Vision Lane (gold) ↔ Operations Lane (steel) color coding must be visually identical every time each lane reappears (beats 1, 3, 4, 5) so a viewer tracks "which job is this" instantly without rereading text.
- The "SKYDANCE — PENDING CLOSE" marker uses the identical gray/bracket/stamp treatment every time it appears (beats 2, 5, 6) — same gray value, same bracket style, same "PENDING CLOSE" wording — so its provisional status reads as a consistent visual rule, not a one-off graphic.
- JustPreneur wordmark treatment (size, position, opacity) stays identical across beats 2–8, then scales up only for beat 9's sting.
- Beats 7 and 8 share one identical background asset and one identical minimal type treatment, reinforcing that they are a single two-part thesis statement.

---

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm-white text (#F5F3EE-equivalent) on navy scrim (#0B1E32-equivalent) at ≥70% scrim opacity comfortably clears WCAG AA contrast for large text.
- Gold text/elements (energy-gold, e.g., #D4A017-equivalent) are reserved for short labels, the "$110B" figure, and chyron role lines only — never full paragraph body copy — since gold-on-navy body text at small sizes drops below comfortable contrast on compressed mobile screens.
- The "SKYDANCE — PENDING CLOSE" gray marker is a graphic element, not primary body text; its internal label text (added in layout) must still sit on a dark-enough navy/bracket background to remain independently legible, not just gray-on-gray.
- No text is ever placed directly over high-frequency icon-line detail without the navy scrim between them — scrim is mandatory on every beat, not optional.
- Minimum single-line text size for all beats should render at no less than ~64px cap-height equivalent at 1080px width, and beats 7–8's thesis lines should be the largest text in the piece, legible on a phone at arm's length mid-scroll.
