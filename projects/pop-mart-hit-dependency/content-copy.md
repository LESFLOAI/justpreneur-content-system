## Final Caption

Pop Mart's Aug 21, 2026 earnings call made headlines for the wrong reason: a caution that growth may fall short of its 20% target, tied to an overseas sales slowdown — plus a CNY 2–5 billion buyback to steady the stock.

But the headline buried the real story. Labubu's share of Pop Mart's revenue had already fallen from 38.1% in FY2025 to roughly 26% in H1 2026 — before the slowdown ever became news.

The market panicked twice this year, in March and May, each time the stock falling 20–23% on fear that one character was too big to fail. Pop Mart wasn't reacting to that fear. It had already cut its own dependency on schedule.

Diversification isn't a crisis response. It's a decision made early enough that the crisis doesn't define you.
