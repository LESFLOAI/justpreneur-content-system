## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, cleared with qualifications by Fact Verifier)

Aaron Paul and Bryan Cranston's agave spirits brand Dos Hombres — a mezcal brand launched in July 2019 that expanded into tequila in April 2026 — opened a new permanent, standalone branded bar on the sixth floor of SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, CA. The bar debuted Monday, September 21, 2026, coinciding with the Los Angeles Rams' Monday Night Football home opener against the New York Giants. Paul and Cranston appeared at the bar serving cocktails to fans, including a signature drink called "GO FOR DOS!" (mezcal, fresh lemon juice, organic agave, cherry, dehydrated lemon). The bar is intended to operate during all SoFi Stadium events, not just Rams games. This is described by Dos Hombres and SoFi Stadium as the brand's first permanent, dedicated branded bar — it should NOT be described as the brand's first-ever branded bar activation, since the brand has previously run temporary branded pop-ups at Four Seasons properties in Maui (2023) and Denver (2024). Constellation Brands took an undisclosed-terms minority stake in Dos Hombres in June 2021 and continues to hold a minority stake; there is no evidence Constellation was involved in financing or negotiating the SoFi Stadium bar. In December 2025, Dos Hombres separately announced a $15 million growth investment (investor identity not fully disclosed); this raise has not been shown to be connected to the SoFi Stadium bar deal. ## Final Caption

Most celebrity-backed spirits brands stop at the label.

On Monday night, Aaron Paul and Bryan Cranston didn't.

Their brand, Dos Hombres, opened its first permanent, standalone bar — on the sixth floor of SoFi Stadium — timed to the Rams' Monday Night Football home opener against the Giants. Instead of showing up for a photo op, the founders worked the bar themselves, serving fans a signature drink they created: "GO FOR DOS!" (mezcal, fresh lemon juice, organic agave, cherry, dehydrated lemon).

A few honest notes: Dos Hombres has done branded activations before — pop-ups at Four Seasons properties in Maui (2023) and Denver (2024) — so this is their first *permanent* bar, not their first-ever branded moment. They've been a Rams partner since 2023 in the mezcal category specifically; the Rams' official tequila partner is a separate brand, 1800 Tequila. And there's no indication Constellation Brands' minority stake or the $15M growth raise Dos Hombres announced in December 2025 had anything to do with financing this bar — that's a separate story.

Here's the actual takeaway: in a market flooded with celebrity name-brands, the founders literally standing behind the bar — pouring drinks, talking to fans — is the marketing. No campaign buys that kind of trust.

Save this. Then find the one customer-facing moment in your business where showing up in person would matter more than any ad spend.
