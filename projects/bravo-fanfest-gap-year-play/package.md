## Story summary (Approved Facts Block, Fact Verifier: cleared with qualifications)

Bravo Fan Fest Charleston is a one-day Bravo/NBCUniversal fan event taking place Saturday, October 24, 2026, at the North Charleston Performing Arts Center (North Charleston, SC). The event features more than 40 Bravo personalities ("Bravolebrities") from shows including Below Deck, Real Housewives, Southern Charm, Southern Hospitality, and Summer House. Ahead of the main event, Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen taped four episodes over two nights (Oct. 22–23, 2026) at the same venue; those tapings, along with Fan Fest general admission and VIP tickets, are reported sold out by Bravo's own event coverage and the venue's official listing (not independently verified by this team).

According to the Charleston Post and Courier, general admission tickets were priced at $475 and VIP at $755, plus taxes and fees, at original on-sale (current resale prices run well above face value — a separate, unspecified figure, never conflated with face value). Bravo/Peacock's event coverage lists seven brand sponsors: Ally Bank, Colgate Optic White, Genesis, Planet Fitness, State Farm, Wayfair, and Wendy's — attributed to that coverage, not an independently confirmed press release. Peacock is reportedly streaming main-stage panels live and on-demand.

Fan Fest Charleston follows Bravo's biennial mega-convention BravoCon, which drew an estimated/approximately 30,000 attendees in Las Vegas in November 2025 (up from an estimated/approximately 27,000 in 2023); neither figure is an audited count. Andy Cohen announced at NBCUniversal's May 2026 upfront presentation that BravoCon will return in fall 2027, though no specific dates or venue have been announced yet.

No official revenue or profit figures have been disclosed by NBCUniversal/Bravo for either BravoCon or Fan Fest. The "business model" framing below (ticket pricing, sponsorship, streaming as parallel monetization layers used to bridge the gap between flagship events) is an analytical inference from public information, not a disclosed financial figure.

## Angle (JustPreneur Strategist)

**"Between-Cycles Community Retention."** Bravo's real move isn't the convention — it's refusing to let the ~2-year gap between BravoCons go monetization-dead, using a smaller, sold-out, single-city event to keep the fan relationship (and revenue) alive in between. Primary takeaway: the businesses that win long-term aren't the ones with the biggest flagship moment — they're the ones who build smaller, consistent touchpoints so the audience relationship never goes quiet between the big moments.
