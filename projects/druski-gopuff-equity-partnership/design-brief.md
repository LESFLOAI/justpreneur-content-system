## 1. Overall Creative Concept

A premium business-media visual essay built on a single recurring motif: a **vertical split panel** that physically divides each relevant slide into two unequal zones — a narrower, cooler, flatter "SPOKESPERSON" zone and a wider, warmer, deeper "EQUITY PARTNER" zone. The split literalizes the copy's core argument (flat fee vs. folded-in ownership) without using any real photography, logos, or likenesses. Typography and abstract iconography carry the entire visual argument — this should read like a Fast Company / Adweek explainer graphic, not a motivational quote card.

## 2. Composition and Focal Hierarchy

- **Primary focal point on every slide:** the headline/slide title, set large, top-third or centered, always the first thing the eye hits.
- **Secondary focal point:** the split-panel graphic (slides 2, 3, 4, 6) or the quote/checklist block (slides 5, 7).
- **Tertiary, constant element:** the JustPreneur wordmark, placed in the same position on every slide (bottom-center, inside safe margin) at a size that is clearly legible, not a tiny corner mark — roughly 3-4% of canvas height.
- No slide should ever have two elements competing for first-glance attention. Hedge/disclosure text is treated as body copy, not as a footnote — same type family, same weight tier as the surrounding sentence it qualifies (see guardrail section 9).

## 3. Slide-by-Slide Layout

### Slide 1 — Cover
- Full-bleed deep navy background with a subtle diagonal gold seam running from lower-left to upper-right (foreshadows the split-panel motif to come, doesn't fully divide the slide).
- Headline, centered upper-middle, in warm white with the words "Folded In" set in energy gold for emphasis: **"Druski Didn't Just Get Paid. He Got Folded In."**
- Subhead directly below, smaller, steel gray/light gray: "Gopuff's new deal with Druski isn't a typical ad campaign."
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center.
- No faces, no logos — background texture only (abstract gold line + soft grain).

### Slide 2 — Split Panel: Define the Two Lanes
- Vertical split, left panel ~40% width in flat steel gray, right panel ~60% width in deep navy with gold accent edge.
- Left header "SPOKESPERSON" (steel gray type, warm white), body: "Face. Reach. A flat fee." Icon: a single abstract silhouette bust outline with a dollar-sign-style flat line beneath it (simple geometric icon, not a real face).
- Right header "EQUITY PARTNER" (gold type, warm white body), body: "Companies. Products. A seat in the business." Icon: an abstract interlocking geometric form (two shapes nesting into a larger one) suggesting merger/ownership.
- Micro-line spanning both panels at the bottom, centered, equal weight: "Most creators stay on the left. Druski moved to the right."
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center, below the micro-line, within safe margin.

### Slide 3 — The Deal
- Single navy background (no split needed here — this is the factual anchor slide).
- Headline treatment: "THE DEAL" as a short kicker in gold, all-caps, small but not tiny (eyebrow style, ~28-32px equivalent).
- Body copy, warm white, generous line height, centered block with wide margins:
  "Gopuff announced on October 9, 2026 a multi-year partnership with Druski (Drew Desbordes) that, according to the company, includes an equity stake for him."
  "The size of the stake — and the rest of the financial terms — has not been disclosed."
- CRITICAL: "according to the company" and "has not been disclosed" render in the identical font, size, weight, and color as the rest of the sentence — no parentheses-as-footnote styling, no gray-out, no reduced opacity.
- Abstract icon: a simple document/handshake-style geometric glyph (no text, no logo) as a small supporting graphic above the kicker.
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center.

### Slide 4 — What He Brought
- Split panel returns, but asymmetric: left narrow strip (steel gray, ~25%) labeled "AD DEAL," right wide field (navy + gold, ~75%) labeled "INFRASTRUCTURE."
- Body copy sits in the right/main field, warm white:
  "Per Gopuff's announcement, Druski's own ventures — Coulda Been Records and 4Lifers Entertainment — are being folded into the partnership to co-create products and consumer experiences with Gopuff."
- Closing line, set larger/bolder as the slide's punch: "That's not a face for an ad. That's infrastructure a brand can build with."
- "Per Gopuff's announcement" rendered in the same weight/size as the rest — not a disclaimer.
- Icon: abstract building-block / module shapes stacking into a larger structure (echoes slide 2's merger icon for consistency).
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center.

### Slide 5 — The Company's Framing
- Single navy background, quote-card treatment.
- Large abstract quotation-mark glyph (geometric, not a logo) top of frame, gold.
- Quote, warm white, large and centered: "A true creative and commercial partnership."
- Attribution line directly below, same visual weight as body text elsewhere (not shrunk): "— Yakir Gola, Gopuff co-founder and co-CEO, per Gopuff's announcement"
- Disclosure line immediately below attribution, SAME size/weight/contrast as the attribution line (per guardrail): "A statement from the company, not an independent interview."
- No depiction of any person — typographic quote card only.
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center.

### Slide 6 — The Audition
- Split panel, horizontal this time (top/bottom instead of left/right) for rhythm variation: top band labeled "THE AD" (steel gray), bottom band labeled "THE EQUITY" (navy/gold).
- Top band text: "Druski previously appeared alongside Tom Brady in Gopuff's 'HARD PASS' campaign." — rendered as text only; represent the campaign with two abstract silhouette marks side by side under a spotlight-shaped geometric glyph (no likeness, no recognizable features, no real ad recreation).
- Bottom band text: "The ad got him in the room. The companies he already owned are what got him equity."
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center.

### Slide 7 — The Takeaway
- Navy background, checklist layout, left-aligned text block centered on canvas with generous margins.
- Kicker: "IF YOU WANT EQUITY INSTEAD OF A CHECK, BRING:" (gold, all-caps eyebrow).
- Three checklist rows, each with a simple gold checkmark/square-bullet glyph:
  — A company or product line, not just a following
  — Audience infrastructure a brand can plug into
  — A track record the brand can co-build on, not just promote
- Closing line beneath checklist, set apart with slightly more weight: "Attention gets you booked. Ownership gets you folded in."
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center.

### Slide 8 — CTA
- Deep navy background, simplest slide in the set — maximum legibility for the call to action.
- Line 1, large, warm white: "Save this before your next brand pitch."
- Line 2, medium, gold: "Ask: 'What am I bringing besides my reach?'"
- Line 3, smaller but still fully legible (not a tiny footer mark): "Follow @justpreneur for more breakdowns like this."
- JustPreneur wordmark/logo treatment here can be slightly larger than other slides since this is the branded closing card — e.g., centered above the three lines or integrated as a clean sign-off mark.

## 4. Typography Direction

- **Headline/kicker typeface:** a bold, condensed-to-medium geometric sans (e.g., a Neue Haas/Inter/Söhne-style face) for authority and modern business-media feel.
- **Body copy typeface:** a clean, highly legible humanist sans at a slightly lighter weight than headlines, generous line-height (1.3–1.4x) for mobile reading.
- Maintain no more than two typefaces total (one display/headline, one body) — avoid script or decorative fonts entirely.
- Minimum effective body text size at final export: large enough to read clearly on a phone at arm's length and to still be legible at Instagram feed thumbnail scale (roughly equivalent to 44-56px+ at 1080px canvas width for body copy, larger for headlines).
- All hedge/disclosure language uses the exact same font, size, weight, and color as its surrounding sentence — this is enforced on every slide where it appears (3, 4, 5, and implicitly 6's sourcing).

## 5. Color Palette

- **Deep navy** (#0B1C33 approx.) — dominant background, "equity partner" panel color.
- **Steel gray** (#5B6670 approx.) — "spokesperson" panel color, secondary text.
- **Warm white** (#F5F2EC approx.) — primary text/headline color.
- **Energy gold** (#D9A431 approx.) — accent, kicker labels, emphasis words, checkmarks, dividing seams.
- Contrast pairing rule: gold and warm white only ever sit on navy or steel gray (never gold-on-white or gold-on-light-gray, which would fail contrast).

## 6. Image/Graphic Treatment

- No real photography of any person. No logos or trademark recreations (Gopuff, Coulda Been Records, 4Lifers Entertainment, Adweek, etc. appear as plain text only, never as styled wordmarks or logo-like treatments).
- All supporting visuals are abstract geometric glyphs/icons: silhouette busts (generic, non-identifiable), interlocking shapes, building blocks, quotation marks, checkmarks, a simple spotlight shape. Treat these like an editorial infographic's icon set — clean line weight, consistent stroke width, gold or warm-white on navy/gray.
- No clutter: one icon concept per slide maximum, generous negative space around it.
- No borders of flags, badges, or "verified" marks.

## 7. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 px per slide.
- Keep all text and essential graphics within a 60px margin on all four sides (i.e., live text area roughly 960 x 1230 px, centered).
- Account for Instagram's UI overlay: keep the bottom 250px relatively uncluttered of critical text where possible (profile row/caption affordances can crop close on some surfaces) — the JustPreneur wordmark at bottom-center should sit above this zone, not flush to the absolute bottom edge.
- Split-panel seams and background color blocks may bleed to the canvas edge; only typography and iconography must respect the safe margin.

## 8. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

- Every slide uses the same navy/gray/white/gold palette — no slide introduces a new color.
- JustPreneur wordmark appears in the same position (bottom-center) and same relative size on slides 1–7; slide 8 may scale it up slightly as the closing brand moment, but it must remain the same mark/typeface treatment.
- The split-panel motif (vertical on slides 2 and 4, quote-card variant on slide 5, horizontal on slide 6) should use matching gold divider-seam styling so the viewer recognizes it as one consistent visual system across the carousel, not disconnected templates.
- Kicker labels (all-caps, gold, small eyebrow style) are used consistently wherever a slide needs a short category tag (slides 3, 7).
- Icon stroke weight and style stay consistent across all slides that use icons (2, 3, 4, 6).

## 9. Accessibility and Contrast Check

- Warm white (#F5F2EC) on deep navy (#0B1C33): contrast ratio well above WCAG AA for large text (~13:1) — passes for headline and body use.
- Gold (#D9A431) on deep navy: contrast ratio approx. 6:1+ — acceptable for headline-sized and kicker text; avoid using gold for small dense body paragraphs where possible, reserve it for short emphasis words, kickers, and icon strokes.
- Warm white on steel gray (left panel, slide 2): verify ratio stays above 4.5:1 at build time; if steel gray is rendered lighter than spec, darken it slightly rather than lightening the text, to preserve the "flatter/cooler" visual meaning of that panel.
- No text ever sits directly on a photographic image with variable contrast — all text sits on flat or near-flat color fields or simple gradients, since no photography is used.
- Hedge/disclosure sentences are never rendered in a lower-contrast tone than neighboring factual sentences — this is both an editorial-integrity requirement and an accessibility requirement, and is called out per-slide above.
