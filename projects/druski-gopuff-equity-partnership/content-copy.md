### Slide 3 — The Deal
Gopuff announced on October 9, 2026 a multi-year partnership with Druski (Drew Desbordes) that, according to the company, includes an equity stake for him.
The size of the stake — and the rest of the financial terms — has not been disclosed.

### Slide 4 — What He Brought
Per Gopuff's announcement, Druski's own ventures — Coulda Been Records and 4Lifers Entertainment — are being folded into the partnership to co-create products and consumer experiences with Gopuff.
That's not a face for an ad. That's infrastructure a brand can build with.

### Slide 5 — The Company's Framing
From Gopuff's announcement, co-founder and co-CEO Yakir Gola is quoted calling it:
"A true creative and commercial partnership."
(A statement from the company, not an independent interview.)

### Slide 6 — The Audition
The deal builds on an earlier relationship: Druski previously appeared alongside Tom Brady in Gopuff's "HARD PASS" campaign.
The ad got him in the room. The companies he already owned are what got him equity.

### Slide 7 — The Takeaway
**If you want equity instead of a check, bring:**
— A company or product line, not just a following
— Audience infrastructure a brand can plug into
— A track record the brand can co-build on, not just promote
Attention gets you booked. Ownership gets you folded in.

Druski didn't just land an ad deal — according to Gopuff's own October 9, 2026 announcement, he landed an equity stake, with Coulda Been Records and 4Lifers Entertainment folded directly into the partnership to co-create products and experiences.

The size of that stake isn't public. The terms aren't confirmed beyond Gopuff's statement. This is widely reported based on one company announcement — not independently verified deal terms.
