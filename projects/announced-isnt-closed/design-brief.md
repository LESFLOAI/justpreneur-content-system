# Visual Design Brief — "Announced Isn't Closed" (4-slide carousel)

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a business-editorial deconstruction, closer to a Bloomberg/Axios/Fortune "what actually happened" explainer than a motivational-quote template. The visual spine is a single financial-editorial device — a stamped, oversized dollar figure — that opens the carousel intact-looking and closes it voided, bookending the four beats: Hook (headline number, questioned) → Announcement (deal facts, media coverage) → Collapse (timeline reversal) → Takeaway/CTA (the lesson + save prompt). Slides 2 and 3 use a matched "mirror" composition (a converging visual motif vs. a diverging/breaking version of the same motif) so the before/after collapse reads instantly even at thumbnail size, without needing to read every word.

Disney and OpenAI are referenced only through neutral, abstract domain motifs — never through logos, wordmarks, or trademark-adjacent imagery. Disney's domain is suggested via an abstracted film-reel/sprocket-and-light-beam motif (generic cinema/entertainment, not any specific franchise character or mark). OpenAI's domain is suggested via an abstracted neural-network/node-and-line motif (generic AI/tech, not any specific product UI). No AI-generation or video-camera gimmick imagery (no robot hands, no glowing "AI" text, no camera-lens clichés) — keep it in the language of finance/deal-making, not tech-demo visuals.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: single dominant graphic/typographic device (stamped numeral, converging motif, diverging motif, or ledger/pen device) placed to leave a large clean negative-space zone for headline + body copy — never text baked fully into the AI-generated image. Hierarchy per slide, top to bottom:
1. Small kicker/section label (e.g., "THE HOOK," "THE ANNOUNCEMENT") — top margin, gold, all-caps, letter-spaced
2. Headline/body copy block — upper-to-mid frame
3. Graphic/photographic focal device — lower half or as a background field behind the copy block
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, identical position across all 4 slides (bottom-center)

## 3. Exact placement of headline, supporting copy, imagery, branding

**Slide 1 — Cover / The Hook (1080x1350)**
- Background: deep navy field with a large, bold, stamped-style numeral treatment reading "$1,000,000,000" (or "$1B") rendered as a photographic/graphic background element (financial-ledger / rubber-stamp aesthetic), with a diagonal strike-through or "VOID"-stamp visual crossing through it, positioned in the lower two-thirds of the frame
- Headline "Zero Dollars Moved. The Headline Still Said $1 Billion." — set in upper-middle band, roughly y=220–620px, centered or left-aligned, max width ~880px (100px side margins)
- Subhead "Announced isn't closed. Here's what actually happened." — directly beneath headline, smaller weight, y≈650–760px
- JustPreneur wordmark: bottom-center, y≈1250px, small but legible

**Slide 2 — The Announcement**
- Kicker "THE ANNOUNCEMENT" top-left, y≈120px, gold, letter-spaced, small caps
- Body copy block beneath kicker, y≈180–620px, left-aligned, max width ~840px, structured as a short lead line ("December 2025: OpenAI + Disney announce a deal.") followed by the three bullet facts (3-year license for Sora + ChatGPT Images across 200+ Disney/Marvel/Pixar/Star Wars characters; Disney's planned $1B equity investment plus warrants; coverage by CNBC, Bloomberg, Axios, NPR)
- Background/graphic device: the two abstract domain motifs (film-reel/sprocket-and-light-beam for the entertainment side; neural-network node-and-line for the AI side) shown converging toward each other, like two beams meeting at a bright point — lower third or right side of frame, symbolizing a deal coming together
- Wordmark bottom-center, consistent position

**Slide 3 — The Collapse**
- Kicker "THE COLLAPSE" top-left, same style as Slide 2
- Body copy block, y≈180–580px, left-aligned, max width ~840px: "March 2026: OpenAI announces it's shutting down Sora. Disney then withdraws from the partnership. The $1B investment never closed. No funds. No warrants. Nothing transferred."
- Background/graphic device: the same two motifs from Slide 2, now visibly diverging/breaking apart — the network nodes going dark/disconnecting on one side, the film-reel/light-beam fraying or dimming on the other, with a visible gap or fracture where they used to meet — reinforcing "this reversed"
- Wordmark bottom-center, consistent position

**Slide 4 — Takeaway / CTA**
- Kicker "THE LESSON" (or "YOUR MOVE") top-left
- Body copy, y≈180–700px, left-aligned or centered, max width ~860px: "Announced isn't closed. Until the ink is dry and the money moves, a deal is worth zero. Build on what's actually in hand — not what's in the headlines." followed by a visually separated closing instruction line: "Save this. Then go audit your own LOIs, MOUs, and 'partnership announced' posts."
- Background/graphic device: a close, quiet still-life device echoing Slide 1's stamp motif but resolved — e.g., an unsigned contract page with a pen resting beside an empty signature line, or the same "$1B" stamp now rendered as a faded/voided ledger entry with a hand-drawn "$0" beside it — visual bookend to Slide 1's crossed-out billion
- Add a simple "SAVE THIS" visual cue near the bottom of the copy block (small bookmark mark) — included only because the approved copy explicitly instructs "Save this," kept small, single, and functional, not decorative
- JustPreneur wordmark, bottom-center — may be slightly larger/more prominent as the sign-off slide

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface: a bold, high-contrast slab-serif or condensed display serif with a "financial press" feel (e.g., a Tiempos Headline / Publico-style editorial serif) for Slide 1's headline and the stamped numeral — signals "premium business media," not a generic Instagram template.
- Body copy typeface: a clean grotesk/sans (e.g., a Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style sans) for all body paragraphs and bullet facts — maximizes mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Kickers ("THE ANNOUNCEMENT," "THE COLLAPSE," "THE LESSON"): sans, all-caps, letter-spaced (~150–200 tracking), small size (~28–32px equivalent), gold or warm-white.
- Stamped numeral on Slide 1: extra-bold monospaced-or-slab numeral style evoking a ledger/rubber-stamp, large enough to dominate the lower two-thirds but never overlapping or fighting the headline zone above it.
- Headline sizing: large enough to be legible at thumbnail size on a phone — no line should exceed ~34 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on a 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (not justified) for readability. Bullet facts on Slide 2 should use a consistent bullet marker style (small gold dash or dot, not an emoji icon).
- Slide 4's "Save this" instruction line: same sans body font, set as a clearly separate short line (slightly bolder or gold-accented) beneath the main takeaway sentence so it reads as an action cue, not buried mid-paragraph.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220 to #10172A range) — dominant background/shadow tone across all four slides for consistency
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces, kicker backdrop, ledger/contract paper tone
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 or #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds, and the "paper"/ledger highlight tone
- Energy gold (#C9A24B / #D4AF37 range) — kickers, accents, the stamp/strike-through device, wordmark, and the converging light-beam in Slide 2
- Slide 3 shifts slightly cooler (more steel-gray, dimmer gold) versus Slide 2's warmer convergence, to visually reinforce the collapse without breaking overall carousel cohesion
- Reserve a small amount of desaturated red or ink-red (#8C3B3B range, used sparingly) only for the "VOID"/strike-through stamp accents on Slides 1 and 4, so the "never closed" concept reads instantly — do not use red anywhere else in the carousel

## 6. Image treatment

- All background graphics/photos: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm highlight pops (gold/warm-white), consistent color grade across all four slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying graphic — this is a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, or real identifiable people/faces should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy (including the stamped numeral wording and bullet facts) is added or finished in a design tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated backgrounds should supply mood, motif, and negative space only.
- No depiction of any Disney character, Marvel/Pixar/Star Wars imagery, or OpenAI/ChatGPT/Sora product UI, wordmark, or logo in any image — use only the neutral abstract motifs described above.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all four slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge (left/right/top/bottom) at minimum; Instagram's UI (profile row, caption, like/comment icons) can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px on the feed preview — keep headline anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~840px wide (leaving ≥120px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080x1350), identical kicker style/position (top-left, gold, small-caps) on Slides 2, 3, 4; Slide 1 uses a centered cover treatment as the "bookend" open.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center) on every slide for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/gray/gold consistent) except Slide 3's intentionally cooler/dimmer cast to sell the collapse, and the sparing red stamp accent confined to Slides 1 and 4.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across Slides 2–4; Slide 1's headline/subhead may be centered as the cover.
- The converging-motif device (Slide 2) and diverging-motif device (Slide 3) must use the same two visual elements (film-reel/light-beam + neural-network nodes) so the "coming together vs. falling apart" contrast is immediately legible as a matched pair.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, not just the general background.
- Avoid placing gold (#C9A24B) text directly on light/warm-white areas (e.g., the ledger-paper motif on Slide 4) — gold is reserved for kickers/accents on dark navy/steel backgrounds only, where contrast is strong.
- The sparing red/ink-red stamp accent (Slides 1 and 4) is decorative graphic only, never used for body text, so it does not create a text-contrast risk.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas (roughly 14–16pt equivalent at full-size phone viewing) to remain legible when scaled down to feed thumbnail size.
