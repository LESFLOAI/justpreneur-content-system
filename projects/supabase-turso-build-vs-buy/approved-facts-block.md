# Approved Facts Block — Supabase / Turso (2026-10-10, 10:00 AM slot)

**Fact Verifier status: Cleared with qualifications**

On October 2, 2026, Supabase announced it had agreed to acquire Turso, a SQLite-based ("Rust-rewrite") cloud database startup, for an undisclosed price. Turso will continue operating its existing platform. Turso's founder, Glauber Costa, is joining Supabase along with the Turso team.

Simultaneously, Supabase announced a $150 million funding round led by Singapore's sovereign wealth fund GIC, with participation from Alphabet's independent growth fund CapitalG, IronArc, and SquarePeg.

This round follows Supabase's $500 million Series F, announced in June 2026, which valued the company at roughly $10.5 billion post-money (also reported as $10 billion pre-money) — making the October announcement roughly four months later.

Supabase says approximately 70% of new databases created on its platform are now initiated by AI agents or AI-driven developer tools rather than directly by humans — up from a company-reported figure of "more than 60%" in June 2026. This percentage is a company-reported figure and has not been independently verified by outside analysts. Must carry "Supabase says..." attribution in all copy.

The price Supabase is paying for Turso has not been disclosed by either company, and no outlet has reported a figure. Neither company has announced a closing date, SLA, or specific future pricing terms for existing Turso customers post-acquisition. Several industry commentators have flagged this as an open question for Turso's customer base, but no source documents a confirmed "pricing-model incompatibility" between the two companies — this framing must NOT be used.
