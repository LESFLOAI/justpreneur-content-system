## 2. Slide-by-Slide Copy

**Slide 1 — Cover**
Subway Had One Month to Launch a Campaign.
So They Reopened a Drawer.

**Slide 2 — The Clock**
August 2026: The Martin Agency wins Subway's U.S. creative account — unseating incumbent Leo (Publicis Groupe) after a review.

One month later, on September 21, they launch a national campaign.

No blank-slate pitch. No time for one.

**Slide 3 — The Move**
They didn't invent something new.
They reopened a drawer.

In 2018, Subway ran a 3-day guerrilla-style teaser called "SUBconscious." It never became a full campaign. It sat there — shelved, with equity nobody was using.

**Slide 4 — Before / After**
BEFORE — 2018
3-day guerrilla teaser stunt.
No sustained media plan.
Quietly shelved.

AFTER — 2026
"Feed Your SUBconscious."
Will Arnett voices five "inner voice" characters.
A sustained, celebrity-fronted platform.
First major work from a new agency.

**Slide 5 — Leadership Backing**
This wasn't just a creative call. It had leadership behind it.

Jeff Klein — a former Popeyes president — joined Subway in March 2026 as SVP of Marketing, then was elevated to U.S. CMO after Subway eliminated its Global CMO role and shifted to a regional marketing structure.

The prior Global CMO, Greg Lyons, departed as the role was eliminated.

**Slide 6 — The Backdrop**
What trade press is watching:

631 U.S. location closures in 2024.
729 in 2025.
Royalty revenue reportedly down ~10% over two years — since Roark Capital's acquisition (reportedly ~$9.6B) closed in April 2024.

But net income reportedly rose to ~$688M in 2025, up from ~$397M in 2024.

**Slide 7 — The Reframe**
That combination matters.

This reads as a positioning move from a business trade press describes as getting more profitable — not a panic move from one that's failing.

Reviving what already had equity = speed and discipline. Not a lack of originality.

**Slide 8 — Takeaway / CTA**
Before you commission something new —
audit what you already own.

Go back through the last 2-3 years. Which shelved idea, tagline, or positioning attempt still has equity nobody finished building?

## 5. Final Caption

Subway's new campaign launched one month after its agency won the account.

That's not enough time to build something from nothing. So The Martin Agency didn't try to.

They went back to "SUBconscious" — a concept Subway teased for three days in 2018 and then shelved — and rebuilt it into a sustained, celebrity-fronted platform starring Will Arnett.

It's landing alongside a new marketing structure under CMO Jeff Klein, and a business trade press reports is more profitable than it was two years ago, even as store counts shrink.

Read that combination correctly: this is a deliberate positioning move, not a scramble.

Before you brief someone to create something new — go check the drawer. What did you already build that never got the execution it deserved?

Audit your last 2-3 years of shelved ideas. Tell us in the comments what you find.
