## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, cleared with qualifications by Fact Verifier)

On September 21, 2026, Subway launched a new brand platform, "Feed Your SUBconscious," starring Will Arnett as five different "inner voice" characters who talk consumers out of settling for lesser food choices (drive-thru burgers, overpriced smoothies, unappetizing microwaved food). The campaign is the first major work from The Martin Agency, which won Subway's U.S. creative account in August 2026 — about a month before launch — after an SRI-led review that unseated incumbent agency Leo (part of Publicis Groupe). It is also the first major campaign released under Jeff Klein, a former Popeyes president who joined Subway in March 2026 as SVP of Marketing and was elevated to U.S. CMO after Subway eliminated its Global CMO role and shifted to a regional marketing structure under CEO Jonathan Fitzpatrick; the prior Global CMO, Greg Lyons, departed as the role was eliminated (sources differ on voluntariness — treated neutrally throughout). The "SUBconscious" branding revives a concept/tagline Subway first used in a 3-day guerrilla-style teaser campaign in 2018 — never a full campaign, quietly shelved — now reimagined as a sustained, celebrity-fronted platform; this is a structurally different execution, not a literal rerun. Separately, Roark Capital acquired Subway in a deal that closed April 30, 2024, reportedly valued at approximately $9.6 billion (subject to an earn-out provision). Trade press reports 631 U.S. location closures in 2024 and 729 in 2025, with royalty revenue reportedly down approximately 10% over two years — even as Subway's reported net income rose to roughly $688 million in 2025 from roughly $397 million in 2024.

## Angle

**Dormant IP Revival** — the fastest brand comeback isn't a new idea, it's recognizing which of your old ideas already has equity, then giving it real budget, leadership backing, and better execution. A month after winning the account, The Martin Agency didn't pitch a blank-slate concept — they reopened a shelved 2018 tagline and turned it into a sustained, celebrity-fronted platform. Framed as a deliberate positioning move from a business trade press describes as getting more profitable, not a panic move from one that's failing — a deliberately different lesson category than the last four JustPreneur posts (which centered on capital/access control).
