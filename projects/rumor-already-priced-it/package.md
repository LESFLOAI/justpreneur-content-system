## Facts summary (Approved Facts Block, Fact Verifier: Cleared with qualifications)

On October 28, 2026, DoorDash, Inc. (NASDAQ: DASH) and Maplebear Inc. d/b/a Instacart (NASDAQ: CART) signed a definitive merger agreement under which DoorDash will acquire Instacart for $65.00 per share — an equity value of approximately $17 billion — representing a reported 26% premium to Instacart's October 22, 2026 closing price. DoorDash filed an 8-K disclosing the agreement on October 29, 2026. Instacart shareholders may elect $65.00 cash, 0.3372 shares of DoorDash Class A common stock, or a mixed election of $48.75 cash plus 0.0843 DoorDash shares, subject to proration so stock consideration does not exceed 25% of total deal consideration (at least 75% cash). Instacart's board unanimously approved the deal; holders of roughly 19% of Instacart shares have agreed to vote in favor. The companies state the deal is expected to close by the end of 2027 or in H1 2028 (their own target), subject to Instacart stockholder approval, HSR antitrust clearance, and Canada/Israel competition approvals; the agreement includes a $650 million termination fee payable by Instacart under specified circumstances. The companies estimate (their own figure, not independently verified) that merging DashPass + Instacart+ into one membership would save combined members ~$100M/year.

Instacart's current CEO is **Chris Rogers** (since Aug 2025) — **not** Fidji Simo, who departed for an OpenAI role. No individual is quoted anywhere in this package.

On October 26, 2026 — the day Reuters/WSJ first reported DoorDash was in "advanced talks" to acquire Instacart — Instacart shares closed up ~7.9% and DoorDash shares closed down ~6.1%. This reaction is tied strictly to the Oct 26 talks report, **not** to the Oct 28-29 signing. (QC correction: no verified figure exists for Oct 28-29 price movement — copy states only that the reaction had already happened two days earlier, not that the market "barely moved" on signing day, since that specific claim was never fact-checked.)

Per Reuters/WSJ anonymous-sourced reporting (unconfirmed by either company), Uber had earlier held preliminary talks with Instacart, reported inactive by ~Oct 26 — kept hedged throughout.

As of today (Oct 30, 2026), the deal is pending — no termination, regulatory block, lawsuit, or renegotiation reported. Full sourcing: research/2026-10-30_story-scout.md, research/2026-10-30_fact-verifier.md.
