# Visual Design Brief — "The Deal Was Priced Before It Was Signed" (7-slide carousel)

Status: DRAFT MODE — no images generated yet (no image-generation API key available in this cloud environment). All content below is ready for `/justpreneur-finish` to execute later.

Posting cycle: 10:00 AM, 2026-10-30.

Story: DoorDash signed a definitive merger agreement on Oct 28, 2026 to acquire Instacart for ~$17B ($65.00/share); the market had already reacted to the Oct 26 "advanced talks" report (Instacart +7.9%, DoorDash −6.1%) two days before the deal was actually signed. Angle: "The Rumor Already Priced It" — the market moves on the signal, not the official announcement.

**Format note:** The approved copy includes two formats — a primary Reel (45-60 sec, split-screen stock-chart timeline, 7 beats) and this 7-slide carousel as the static-pipeline fallback. Since this cloud draft cycle is built around static slides/images (no video rendering here), this brief covers the carousel only. A Reel edit using real footage/stock-chart motion-graphics assets would be a separate production step once that pipeline and licensed/real chart data exist — explicitly out of scope for this draft.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a markets-and-strategy editorial deconstruction in the register of a Bloomberg or Axios business-desk chart post — never a finance-app screenshot and never a generic motivational template. The visual spine is a single recurring device: **a split-screen stock-line chart on a dark trading-terminal-style navy plate**, standing in for "the signal vs. the signature." It opens mid-divergence on the cover (foreshadowing the story), spikes dramatically on Slide 2 (the rumor reaction), shifts to a formal paperwork/signature still life on Slide 3 (the actual signing), returns to the chart now flat/settled on Slide 4 (the muted reaction), becomes a clean editorial term-sheet plate for Slide 5 (the disclosed terms), and resolves into a quiet, minimal JustPreneur-style card — with the chart line ghosted faintly in the background — for Slides 6 and 7 (takeaway and CTA), bookending the cover.

No DoorDash or Instacart logos, wordmarks, app icons, or trademarked brand colors/mascots anywhere in any image. No readable ticker symbols (e.g., "DASH," "CART") rendered inside the AI-generated images — those are not part of the approved slide copy, so they are omitted entirely rather than invented. No named real individuals are involved in this story's approved copy, so no human likeness is required; any human presence (none planned) would otherwise need to be anonymized.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: one dominant graphic/photographic device placed to leave a large clean negative-space zone for headline + body copy — text is added in layout, never baked into the AI-generated image. Hierarchy per slide, top to bottom:
1. Small kicker/section label (e.g., "THE RUMOR," "THE SIGNATURE") — top margin, gold, all-caps, letter-spaced (Slides 1 and 7 use no kicker — cover/sign-off bookends)
2. Headline/body copy block — upper-to-mid frame
3. Graphic device (chart, document still life, or minimal card texture) — lower half, or as a full-bleed background field behind the copy block
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, identical position across all 7 slides (bottom-center)

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface: a bold, high-contrast editorial serif (Tiempos Headline / Publico-style) for Slide 1's headline — signals "premium business media," not a generic Instagram template.
- Body copy typeface: a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style) for all body paragraphs on Slides 2–7 — maximizes mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Kickers ("THE RUMOR," "THE SIGNATURE," "THE REACTION," "THE TERMS," "THE TAKEAWAY"): sans, all-caps, letter-spaced (~150–200 tracking), small size (~28–32px equivalent), gold or warm-white. Slides 1 and 7 use no kicker (cover/sign-off bookends).
- Headline sizing on Slide 1: large enough to be legible at thumbnail size on a phone — no line should exceed ~34 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on a 1080-wide canvas. Break the line naturally after "Priced" for a two-line stack (e.g., "The Deal Was Priced" / "Before It Was Signed").
- Body copy sizing (Slides 2–7): minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (Slides 2–6) or centered (Slides 1, 7) for readability.
- Slide 5's dense terms paragraph should break into short sentence groups with paragraph spacing (cash/stock election line, then board-approval/commitment line, then close-target line) rather than one dense block — essential for mobile scanning given this is the copy-heaviest slide.
- Slide 2's stat line ("Instacart closes up ~7.9%. DoorDash closes down ~6.1%.") should visually read as its own short paragraph, distinct from the lead sentence, for instant scanning.
- Slide 7's two CTA lines should be visually separated (a line break or slightly larger gap) so each reads as a distinct cue.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A) — dominant background/shadow tone across all seven slides for consistency
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — chart-plate frame, document surfaces, term-sheet rule lines
- Warm white (#F5F1E8/#FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds, paper highlight tone, chart gridlines
- Energy gold (#C9A24B/#D4AF37) — kickers, accents, the rising chart line, wordmark, term-sheet dividers
- A single restrained cool accent — slate-blue/steel (#64748B range, not red) — reserved for the falling chart line only, keeping the palette editorial rather than a finance-app red/green cliché
- No red, no bright saturated color anywhere — this is a measured, analytical story about market signal timing, not a collapse or crisis story, so the palette stays calm and steady throughout

## 6. Image treatment

- All background graphics: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm gold highlight pops, consistent color grade across all seven slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying graphic — a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, tickers, or real identifiable branding should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated backgrounds supply mood, motif, and negative space only.
- No depiction of DoorDash or Instacart logos, wordmarks, app icons, brand colors used as trademark identifiers, or any other company signage in any image — use only the generic/abstract devices described above (unlabeled split-chart lines, unbranded signature document, unbranded term-sheet plate, minimal card texture).
- No photorealistic rendering of named real individuals — none are named in the approved carousel copy, so no human likeness is required in any slide.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge (left/right/top/bottom) at minimum; Instagram's UI (profile row, caption, like/comment icons) can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px on the feed preview — keep headline/kicker anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~880px wide (leaving ≥100px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080x1350) across all seven slides.
- Identical kicker style/position (top-left, gold, small-caps, ~150–200 tracking) on Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 use the centered cover/sign-off treatment (no kicker) as bookends.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center) on every slide for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe; Slide 7's wordmark may step up slightly in size as the sign-off.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/steel/gold/warm-white consistent, slate-blue reserved for the falling-line accent only) — no slide should read as a crisis/collapse story; this is an analytical "watch the signal" story.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 use centered alignment as cover/sign-off bookends.
- The split-chart line device recurs on Slides 1, 2, and 4 (diverging → spiking → flat/settled), then resolves into a ghosted echo of the same line on Slides 6 and 7, so the carousel visually bookends and the "signal vs. signature" theme reads even at a glance.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text on every slide, especially Slide 2 and Slide 5 where the underlying graphics carry more visual texture.
- Avoid placing gold (#C9A24B) text directly over the rising gold chart line itself — keep gold text confined to the clean negative-space zones, never overlapping the chart lines.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas (roughly 14–16pt equivalent at full-size phone viewing) to remain legible when scaled down to feed thumbnail size.
- Slide 5's dense terms paragraph must retain full-size, full-contrast text for every line — do not shrink any line to fit, even the close-target sentence; if needed, prioritize the paragraph-break formatting over keeping it in one visual block.
