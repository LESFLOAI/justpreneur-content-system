## Final Caption (QC Correction #1 applied — Reuters attribution and full quote restored)

"Anthropic's S-1 does something most IPO filings don't: it refuses to name a number.

No internal valuation target — not even privately, per FT reporting — while outside estimates swing from $1.5T to $3T. Meanwhile, per Reuters reporting on the filing's required risk factors, the plainest possible version of the downside is in print: the product 'could pose catastrophic or existential risks to humanity,' and growth 'may not be sustainable over time.' (Reported characterizations of the filing's language — not independently confirmed verbatim.)

That's not a company hiding something. It's a company that wrote its own uncertainty down before someone else could surface it for them.

The $80.7B net loss headline is real — but per secondary reporting, most of it is non-cash accounting, not cash burning out the door.

The takeaway for builders isn't the number. It's the posture: disciplined candor holds up better than hype, because it can't be walked back.

Before your next pitch, fundraising update, or launch post — find the one thing you're overstating and the one risk you're underselling. Rewrite both with specifics."
