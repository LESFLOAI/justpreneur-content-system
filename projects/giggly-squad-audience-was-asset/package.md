## Final Caption

In 2020, Hannah Berner and Paige DeSorbo started going live on Instagram. No studio. No plan for a headline-making deal. Just two friends showing up, consistently, for six years.

That consistency became "Giggly Squad" — a podcast that just won Podcast of the Year (a fan-voted category) at the 2026 iHeartPodcast Awards at SXSW.

On September 17, 2026, SiriusXM announced a new multi-year agreement (reported as a four-year deal) moving "Giggly Squad" from Acast to SiriusXM, with exclusive distribution and global ad-sales rights. Per The Ankler/Like & Subscribe, citing sources familiar with the process, SiriusXM, Amazon, and Acast all reportedly competed for the show. Reported deal value: $140 million–$160 million, per trade reports — not officially confirmed by SiriusXM. New exclusive programming is planned for 2027.

Nobody bids on an audience that isn't real. And no one builds one overnight.

The six years before the headline is the part worth studying.
