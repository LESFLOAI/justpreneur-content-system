## Final Caption

Netflix didn't just sell tickets to its new "KPop Demon Hunters: The Immersive Experience" — it sold access to the right to buy them, in three deliberate waves.

Mastercard cardholders first (Sept. 22, 2026). Netflix members next (Sept. 29, 2026). Everyone else last (Oct. 1, 2026). The experience itself opens Nov. 12 at Netflix House Dallas and Nov. 20 at Netflix House near Philadelphia — the first "Immersive Experience"-format attraction added to either venue since they opened. General admission starts at $39 for adults ($34 for kids 3–12); VIP starts at $89–$99 depending on the day.

The lesson for builders isn't the price. It's the sequence. Who gets to buy first tells your audience exactly who you value most — before a single ticket sells.

Swipe through the funnel. Then ask: when you launch next, who goes first?
