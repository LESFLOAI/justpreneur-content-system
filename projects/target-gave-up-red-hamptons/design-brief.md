## 1. Overall Creative Concept

This is a story about a real company deliberately trading a negotiable piece of its identity for market access — so the deck itself should *perform* that trade, visually, slide by slide, rather than just describe it. The organizing device is a **"Red Fade System"**: a muted, editorial red (standing in for Target's own brand red, abstractly — never a literal reproduction of its registered logo) is present at meaningful visual weight on Slide 1, recedes through Slides 2–3, disappears completely once the story reaches the actual redesigned storefront (Slide 4 onward), and never returns. By Slide 8, the palette has settled into JustPreneur's own fixed navy/gold/white/steel system — a quiet visual echo of the piece's own thesis: some things are negotiable, some things are fixed, and you can tell which is which by watching what survives to the end.

The aesthetic target is modern business-and-culture media — think Puck, The Information, Morning Brew, or a retail-strategy feature in a design magazine — not a quote-card template. No photography is used to *claim* documentation of the real Southampton store (we have no licensed photo of it); every image is a restrained abstract/editorial illustration or render, so nothing in this deck can be mistaken for an actual photo of the real location, the real Kmart, or the real Architectural Review Board.

A single recurring shape — a generic, abstract concentric-ring "bullseye-style" emblem, deliberately not matching the real registered logo's exact proportions — appears across the deck and is the visual spine of the series: split red/gray on Slide 1, absent through the middle, reappearing whole and fully resolved (gray/white/gold, no red) on Slides 6 and 8. That shape surviving the whole deck, unbroken, is the visual argument for "the shape stayed — color is what moved."

## 2. Composition and Focal Hierarchy (applies to every slide unless noted)

1. **Headline / lead line** — top portion of frame, largest type, highest contrast — first thing the eye hits.
2. **Supporting body copy** — directly beneath, secondary weight, generous line spacing, left-aligned.
3. **Visual motif** (emblem, silhouette, blueprint line-art, toggle icon, modules) — occupies a clearly bounded zone, never placed directly behind text without a clean negative-space buffer or solid-navy zone.
4. **JustPreneur wordmark** — small, fixed position, every slide — a steady signature, never competing for attention.

Reading order every slide: Headline → Body → Visual motif → Wordmark (wordmark is a steady anchor, not a stop on the reading path).

No slide carries a slide-count numeral, date stamp, hashtag, or small corner label beyond the wordmark — none of that was requested in the approved copy, so none is added.

---

## 3. JustPreneur Branding — Exact Placement

- Wordmark "JustPreneur" in clean energy-gold (or warm white where gold would lose contrast — see Slide 8 note), sans serif, one fixed size across all 8 slides.
- Position: **bottom-left corner**, inside the safe margin, identical x/y coordinates on every slide.
- Size: legible at phone-feed thumbnail size, like a channel signature — not a tiny footnote.
- Always sits on a solid-navy or gradient-darkened zone for contrast; never placed over detailed imagery without a scrim.
- Name only — no tagline, no handle, no slide number, no date.

## 4. Slide-by-Slide Direction

**Headline (verbatim):** Target Gave Up Red to Get Into the Hamptons

**Lead stat (verbatim, largest type):** ~90,000 sq. ft.
**Supporting lines (verbatim):** Opened to the public Oct. 6, 2026. Official grand-opening celebration: Oct. 11, 2026.

- No headline sentence is invented for this slide — the approved copy is a pure stat list, so the design leads with the square-footage figure set in the largest numeral treatment in the deck, with the two date lines beneath it at a secondary weight. **Lead line (verbatim, largest):** No red bullseye. No red bollards.
**Supporting copy (verbatim):** Gray shingle exterior, white trim. A white bullseye — lit from above, not glowing from within, like every other Target.

- This is the visual payoff of the whole red-fade system — the slide where red is explicitly, pointedly absent.

A faint, almost-invisible warm-red ghost halo (very low opacity) can sit just behind the white disc as a quiet "what used to be here" cue — restrained enough that it reads as a shadow, not a second logo.
- Composition: facade detail fills the lower half to two-thirds; headline/body sit in the clean upper portion.
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-left.

### Slide 5 — The Redesign, Not the Exception

No readable labels, numbers, or annotations on the blueprint itself.
- Composition: blueprint motif occupies one side of the frame (left or right), the opposite side stays clean navy for the two text lines, kept in their original sentence order with only type-size signaling which line is the "payoff."

- Visual: a clean, oversized abstract toggle-switch icon, shown flipped fully to its muted gray/white side (no red), paired with the recurring ring-emblem reappearing **whole and unsplit** for the first time since Slide 1 — now rendered entirely in gray/white/gold. ## 5. Typography Direction

- **Headline font:** bold, modern geometric/grotesque sans serif (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk Bold / Söhne Breit / Inter Black) — confident, business-editorial, not script or decorative.
- **Body font:** clean humanist sans serif at a lighter weight (e.g., Inter Regular/Medium or Söhne Buch).
- Headline sizes must stay legible at Instagram feed thumbnail size — no headline line below roughly 60-70pt equivalent at 1080px width.
- Never more than two typefaces total across the deck.
- Left-align body copy; avoid centered or justified long paragraphs — ragged-right reads faster on mobile, especially on text-dense Slides 2, 6, and 7.
- No invented words, tags, or labels anywhere — every word on every slide is pulled verbatim from the approved copy above.

---

## 6. Color Palette

- **Primary:** deep navy (#0E1A2B) — dominant background, all 8 slides.
- **Accent/brand constant:** energy gold (#C9A24A) — JustPreneur wordmark, rim-light accents, headline emphasis words, CTA slide.
- **Primary text:** warm white (#F5F1E8) — most headline and body copy.
- **Secondary neutral:** steel gray (#8A93A1) — supporting lines, the "gray shingle" visual family from Slide 4 onward, de-emphasized context lines.
- **Story-only accent — muted editorial red** (approx. #A6362C, a desaturated brick-red, deliberately *not* a pixel-match to any company's registered brand red): used **only** as a receding visual device on Slides 1–2, at reduced weight on Slide 2, and **never again** from Slide 3 onward. Never used for body text, never used to color the JustPreneur wordmark, never used to render any logo artwork.
- No other colors introduced — the discipline of the fade (red disappearing, never returning) is the point; introducing a second surprise color would undercut it.

## 7. Image Treatment

- Every image is an **abstract or editorial-illustration render** — line-art, flat shapes, soft gradients, restrained lighting. None of these are presented as documentary photography of the real Bridgehampton Commons store, the former Kmart, or any real Architectural Review Board meeting — we hold no licensed photography of any of it.
- No readable signage, no invented store-brand wordmarks, and no reproduction of Target's actual registered bullseye logo artwork (exact proportions/registered mark) anywhere — the recurring emblem motif is a generic, simplified concentric-ring shape used for editorial storytelling, not a logo reproduction.
- No human figures as hero subjects on any slide — this story doesn't require faces, and avoiding them removes any malformed-hands/duplicate-face/likeness risk entirely.
- Every image reserves a deliberate, genuinely low-detail negative-space zone sized to the text block that will sit on top of it, per the prompts in Section 11.
- Desaturate/tune generated images toward the fixed navy/gray/white/gold family in post so every slide sits naturally in the palette regardless of what the generator returns — except where the brief explicitly calls for the muted red accent on Slides 1–2 only.

## 8. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350px per slide.
- Keep all text and the JustPreneur wordmark inside a safe margin of **at least 80px from every edge**.
- Keep headline text clear of the **top 120px** and **bottom 150px**, where Instagram's UI chrome (profile bar, caption, swipe-dot indicator) is most likely to overlap.
- Do not place essential words within 60px of the left/right edges to protect against crop variance across devices.

---

## 9. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

1. Same canvas size (1080x1350) and same navy/gold/white/steel base palette, every slide, no exceptions.
2. Same JustPreneur wordmark size/position (bottom-left) on all 8 slides.
3. Same two typefaces (headline family / body family) across the whole deck; only color emphasis shifts intentionally (gold leads on Slide 1's "Hamptons" and Slide 8's CTA; white leads elsewhere).
4. The muted red accent appears **only** on Slides 1 and 2, at visibly decreasing weight, and **never** returns from Slide 3 onward — this is the single most important continuity rule in the deck and should not be relaxed for "visual interest."
5. The recurring abstract ring-emblem motif must be visually the same base shape every time it appears (Slides 1, 4 [as a smaller white version], 6, 8) so the audience recognizes it as "the one thing that didn't change."
6. No slide introduces a new accent color, a new font, a new wordmark position, or an invented label/tag not present in the approved copy.

---

## 10. Accessibility and Contrast Check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) on deep navy (#0E1A2B) comfortably exceeds WCAG AA contrast for large text.
- Energy gold (#C9A24A) on deep navy reads with strong contrast at headline size; gold is reserved for headline-scale or wordmark use, never small body copy, where its contrast margin narrows.
- Muted red (#A6362C) is used only as imagery/accent, never as text color, and never behind text — so it carries no readability burden.
- Steel gray is reserved for de-emphasized supporting/context lines (e.g., Slide 5's first sentence), never for the single most important message on a slide.
- Every slide should pass a squint test: headline legible as a shape/contrast block even blurred, before fine detail resolves — enforced by keeping headlines on flat navy or a clean gradient zone, never directly on a busy image area.
- No information in this deck depends on color alone — the red-fade system is reinforced by the words themselves ("No red bullseye," "the parts of identity that were negotiable," etc.), so it remains legible to color-vision-deficient readers.
