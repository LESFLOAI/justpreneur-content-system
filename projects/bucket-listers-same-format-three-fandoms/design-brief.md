## 1. Overall Creative Concept

The headline is the thesis: "Same Business." So the deck's job is to make that visually undeniable in under three seconds of swiping — not just assert it in text. The organizing device is **one recurring abstract shape — a generic admission-ticket stub (a simple rectangle with one perforated/scalloped edge and a circular punch-hole) — that appears on every slide, identical in silhouette, but re-skinned in a different color "chapter" each time a different IP partner is introduced.**

Three chapters, three palettes, one shape:
- **Barbie chapter (Slide 2):** warm pastel pink + warm white, light and glossy.
- **Golden Girls chapter (Slide 3):** dusty-rose, seafoam mint, butter yellow — a soft 1980s Miami lanai palette.
- **Stranger Things chapter (Slides 4–5):** near-black navy with neon red glow and cyan accent — a generic retro-diner-at-night mood (vinyl booth red, chrome trim, blank neon tubing), never a reproduction of the show's actual title card or any character.

The ticket-stub silhouette never changes — only its finish does. That is the whole visual argument. Slide 1 previews all three finishes side by side before the story starts; Slide 6 shows all three chips plugging into one larger, unified gray/gold "master ticket" or operating-system shape; Slide 7 resolves everything back into JustPreneur's own fixed navy/steel/white/gold palette, with the three chapter colors gone and only the one surviving shape left. The deck itself performs "three fandoms, one repeatable format" rather than just stating it.

Aesthetic reference: modern business-and-culture media (Puck, The Hustle, a culture-desk feature on the creator economy) — confident, clean, slightly playful given the pop-culture subject, but never a motivational-quote template and never fan merch.

## 2. Composition and Focal Hierarchy (applies to every slide unless noted)

1. **Headline / lead line** — upper portion of frame, largest type, highest contrast, first thing the eye hits.
2. **Supporting copy / list items** — directly beneath, secondary weight, generous line spacing, left-aligned.
3. **Visual motif** (the ticket-stub shape in its chapter finish) — occupies a clearly bounded zone, offset to one side or lower portion, never placed directly under text without a clean negative-space buffer.
4. **JustPreneur wordmark** — small, fixed position, every slide, a steady signature.

Reading order every slide: Headline → Body/List → Visual motif → Wordmark.

No slide number, date stamp, hashtag, or small corner label is added anywhere beyond the wordmark — none of that was requested in the approved copy.

## 5. Typography Direction

- **Headline font:** bold, modern geometric/grotesque sans serif (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk Bold / Söhne Breit / Inter Black) — confident and editorial, with enough playfulness in weight to suit a pop-culture subject without becoming a fan-template font.
- **Body/list font:** clean humanist sans serif at a lighter weight (e.g., Inter Regular/Medium or Söhne Buch).
- Headline sizes must stay legible at Instagram feed thumbnail size — no headline line below roughly 60-70pt equivalent at 1080px width.
- Never more than two typefaces total across the deck.
- Left-align body copy and the Slide 5 list; avoid centered or justified long paragraphs — ragged-right reads faster on mobile, especially on the text-dense Slides 5 and 6.
- Em-dashes in the Slide 5 list should render as clean typographic bullets/dashes, not decorative icons, so the list stays scannable.
- No invented words, tags, or labels anywhere — every word on every slide is pulled verbatim from the approved copy above.

## 6. Color Palette

- **Primary/connecting system:** deep navy (#0E1A2B) background on every slide; energy gold (#C9A24A) for wordmark, CTA emphasis, and the "master" ticket shape on Slides 1 (rim accents), 6, and 7; warm white (#F5F1E8) for most headline/body type; steel gray (#8A93A1) for the master-shape finish on Slides 6–7 and for secondary/supporting lines throughout.
- **Chapter 1 — Barbie (Slide 2 only):** warm pastel pink (#F5A9C8-range) + warm white, light, glossy — used only on the ticket-stub motif and its immediate accents, never on body text or the wordmark.
- **Chapter 2 — Golden Girls (Slide 3 only):** dusty rose (#D98C8C-range), seafoam mint (#9FC8B8-range), butter yellow (#E8D08A-range) — used only on the ticket-stub motif and its texture accent, never on body text or the wordmark.
- **Chapter 3 — Stranger Things (Slides 4–5):** near-black navy (#0A0E16) base with neon red glow (#E0303A-range) outline and a cyan accent stripe (#3FD6D1-range) — used only on the motif/booth-corner accent, never reproducing any official title typography or color-locked logo mark.
- Slide 1 previews all three chapter colors together (in the fanned ticket row only); Slide 6 reintroduces all three small chips together one more time before resolving into gray/gold; Slide 7 drops all three chapter colors entirely, returning fully to the navy/steel/white/gold home palette. This disappearance-and-return is deliberate — it visually proves "the system outlives each fandom skin."
- No other colors introduced beyond the above.

## 7. Image Treatment

- All imagery is abstract/editorial illustration or clean render style — flat or softly dimensional geometric shapes, no photography, no attempt to depict a real photographed location, real cast members, real dolls, or any licensed character.
- The recurring ticket-stub silhouette must stay pixel-consistent in shape (same proportions, same perforation/punch-hole placement) across Slides 1 through 7 — only finish/color/texture changes. This consistency is the single most important production note for whoever executes these prompts — if the shape drifts between slides, the deck's core visual argument breaks.
- No logos, no wordmarks belonging to Mattel, Disney, or Netflix, no character likenesses, no show title-card typography, anywhere in any generated image.
- No fabricated signage with real or implied addresses, no countdown timers, no "sold out" or scarcity badges, no price numerals rendered into the artwork (price stays in the type layer only, added at layout time).

## 8. Instagram Safe-Margin Instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 px (4:5 portrait) for all 7 slides.
- Keep all headline, body, and list text within a safe zone of 72px minimum from every edge (left/right/top/bottom).
- Reserve the top ~250px as a lighter-content buffer (Instagram's UI and the carousel-dot indicator sit near the top) — avoid placing critical headline text in the very top 72–100px band; start primary headlines around the 150–200px mark instead.
- Reserve the bottom ~220px as a buffer against caption/UI overlap in-feed — keep the JustPreneur wordmark and any final CTA text no lower than ~150px from the bottom edge.
- Keep the visual motif (ticket-stub shape) clear of the extreme left/right 72px margins so nothing is clipped by Instagram's carousel-swipe crop on edge devices.

## 9. Slide-to-Slide Consistency Rules

- Same canvas size (1080 x 1350) on all 7 slides.
- Same headline/body typography system and type sizes across the deck (chapter color changes the motif, never the type treatment).
- Same JustPreneur wordmark size and bottom-left position on all 7 slides (color may switch between gold and warm white per slide for contrast, per section 3).
- The ticket-stub silhouette is the one fixed visual element that must appear, same proportions, on every single slide — only its color/finish and scale change slide to slide.
- Chapter colors appear only on their assigned slides (pink = Slide 2 only; pastel retro = Slide 3 only; dark neon = Slides 4–5 only) and never bleed into the navy/gold/white/steel "home" system used for headlines, body copy, and the wordmark.
- No slide includes a slide-count numeral, date stamp, or production mark beyond the wordmark.

## 10. Accessibility and Contrast Check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text on deep navy (#0E1A2B) background exceeds WCAG AA contrast for large text on every slide's primary copy zone.
- On Slide 2 (pink chapter) and Slide 3 (pastel chapter), all headline/body text stays on the navy "home" zone, never directly over the pastel motif, so contrast is unaffected by the lighter chapter colors.
- On Slide 4–5 (dark-neon chapter), confirm the neon-red/cyan glow stays confined to the motif and booth-corner accent only — body text stays on the plain near-black/navy zone, not directly over the glow, to avoid low-contrast overlap.
- Gold-on-navy (wordmark, CTA line) meets AA for large text; if any layout tool renders gold at a smaller weight, switch to warm white per the judgment note in Section 3/Slide 7.
- No information is conveyed by color alone — every list item, chapter label ("First," "Then," "Now"), and takeaway line is also carried in the verbatim text, so the deck remains legible if viewed without color differentiation (e.g., color-blind readers, grayscale preview).
