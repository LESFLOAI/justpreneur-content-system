# Visual Design Brief — "The Seat No One Is Paying For" (9-slide carousel)

Status: DRAFT MODE — no images generated yet (no image-generation API key available in this cloud environment). All content below is ready for `/justpreneur-finish` to execute later.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a premium political-economy explainer in the style of a Bloomberg/Financial Times/Semafor visual essay — not a motivational template, and not scandal-coded tabloid framing. The visual arc moves through six beats: Commitment → Criticism → His Number → Appointment → Structural Overlap → Independent Number → Lesson → CTA. Every slide is a photographic, moody, architecture/finance/power-corridor background image with type laid over generous negative space — never text baked fully into the AI image. Two slides carry required special treatments:
- **Slide 4** (Vélez's own ~80% estimate) and **Slide 7** (ANIF's independently sourced 65%+ figure) must look like two distinct, separately sourced statistics — different badge style, different numeral treatment, different color temperature, different composition side. They must never be visually merged, stacked as a range, or implied to be the same measurement.
- **Slide 6** (the adviser-role tension) uses deliberately neutral, non-scandal-coded imagery — an architectural "connection" motif (a bridge/threshold joining two formal buildings), not a confrontation, courtroom, or "versus" graphic. This slide is a structural observation for a strategy case study, not an accusation.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: single dominant focal image element (skyline, building interior, architectural motif, abstract data element) placed off-center or in the lower half, with a large clean negative-space zone (usually the top 35–45%) reserved for headline/kicker/body copy. Hierarchy per slide, top to bottom:
1. Small kicker/section label (e.g., "THE COMMITMENT") — top margin, gold, small caps
2. Headline/body copy block — upper-to-mid frame
3. Photographic/graphic focal subject — lower half (or, on slides 4 & 7, the stat treatment occupies the mid-frame instead of a photo-only focus)
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, identical position across all 9 slides (bottom-center, ~60px above the absolute bottom safe margin)

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline/numeral typeface: a bold, high-contrast serif or modern slab-serif (Tiempos/Canela/GT Sectra-style) for the Slide 1 headline, section kickers, and the Slide 4/Slide 7 stat numerals — signals "premium financial media," not a generic Instagram template.
- Body copy typeface: a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style) for all body paragraphs and attribution lines — maximizes mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Kickers ("THE COMMITMENT," "THE TARGET," etc.): sans, all-caps, letter-spaced (~150–200 tracking), small size (~28–32px equivalent), gold or warm-white. Slide 6's kicker uses the identical plain style — deliberately not flagged or badge-wrapped, to avoid signaling "scandal alert."
- Headline sizing: no line should exceed ~34 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on a 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (not justified).
- Attribution/disclaimer lines (Slides 2, 4, 5, 7): smaller size (~30–34px), warm-white at reduced opacity (not mid-gray) so they remain fully AA-contrast-compliant while visually subordinate to the main copy.
- Slide 4 numeral ("~80%"): large solid-gold fill, ~340–420px effective height, centered-right.
- Slide 7 numeral ("65%+"): large hollow/outlined steel-gray-on-navy treatment, same approximate height range as Slide 4's numeral so neither stat reads as more or less important — the *style*, not the *size*, is what differentiates them.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220 to #10172A range) — dominant background/shadow tone across all slides for consistency
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces, Slide 7's badge fill and numeral outline
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 or #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds
- Energy gold (#C9A24B / #D4AF37 range) — kickers, accents, Slide 4's badge/numeral fill, CTA emphasis, wordmark
- Slide 4 leans slightly warmer (more gold, radial glow) to carry Vélez's own claim; Slide 7 leans slightly cooler (more steel-gray, flat/outlined) to carry the independently sourced figure — this temperature split is the primary tool for keeping the two stats visually distinct.
- Slide 6 uses the standard navy/steel/warm-white palette with minimal gold — deliberately calm and neutral, no red, no high-alert color coding.

## 6. Image treatment

- All background photos/graphics: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm highlight pops (gold/warm-white) — consistent color grade across all 9 slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set, except the intentional warm/cool split between Slides 4 and 7 described above.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying image — this is a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, real identifiable people/faces, or real identifiable named buildings/landmarks should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy and any building references stay generic/abstract; exact copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief.
- Slide 6 imagery must read as calm and administrative/architectural — explicitly no red color-coding, no gavels, no scales of justice, no "VS" split-screens, no handshake-with-money clichés, no courtroom/investigation cues.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all 9 slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge (left/right/top/bottom) at minimum; Instagram's UI (profile row, caption, like/comment icons) can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px on the feed preview — keep headline/badge anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~840px wide (leaving ≥120px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.
- On Slides 4 and 7, keep the badge fully inside the 150px top safe line — do not let it crowd the very top edge where Instagram's UI can clip it.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size, identical kicker style/position (top-left, gold, small-caps) on slides 2, 3, 5, 6, 8; slide 6 intentionally keeps the plain kicker style (no badge) as part of its neutral treatment.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color on every slide (bottom-center) for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/gray/gold consistent) except Slide 4's warmer/gold-glow treatment and Slide 7's cooler/steel-outline treatment — this pairing is the one deliberate, required break from strict visual consistency, and it exists specifically to keep the two stats distinguishable.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across slides 2, 3, 5, 6, 8; slide 1 headline and slide 9 CTA may be centered as the "bookend" slides.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.
- Slides 4 and 7 must never appear adjacent-merged (e.g., never place both numerals on one shared background or one shared badge shape) — they are two separate slides with two separate visual systems, by design.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, not just the general background.
- Avoid placing gold (#C9A24B) text directly on light/warm-white photo areas — gold is reserved for kickers/accents/Slide 4's numeral on dark navy/steel backgrounds only, where contrast is strong.
- Slide 4's gold-on-navy numeral and Slide 7's steel-gray-outline-on-navy numeral must both independently pass AA contrast for large text (3:1 minimum) — verify the outline weight on Slide 7's numeral is thick enough to read clearly at thumbnail size, since outlined/hollow numerals are more legibility-risky than solid fills.
- Attribution/disclaimer lines (Slides 2, 4, 5, 7) must still meet at least AA-level contrast — use warm-white or light steel-gray at reduced opacity (not mid-gray) on the navy overlay.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas (roughly 14–16pt equivalent at full-size phone viewing) to remain legible when scaled down to feed thumbnail size.
