# Visual Design Brief — "The Bananas Didn't Just Go Viral. They Built a League." (7-slide Banana Bowl / BBCL carousel)

Status: DRAFT MODE — no images generated (no image-generation API key available in this cloud environment). All content below is ready for `/justpreneur-finish` to execute later.

Target slot: 2026-10-11. Format: Carousel, 7 slides, 1080 x 1350 portrait throughout.

## 1. Overall creative concept

This is a market-structure story, not a highlight reel and not a "viral moment" recap. The visual job is to show one spotlight act turning into a league — six teams, rivalries, a scaled media deal, and a championship — without ever implying a winner, a dollar figure, or an ownership/financial-infrastructure story. Treat it like a sports-business desk breakdown (The Athletic / Sportico / Puck sports-business feel): night-stadium gold light, disciplined navy/steel palette, confident editorial type. No meme energy, no cartoon banana mascots, no logos.

The carousel has three visual movements:
- **Slides 1–2 (The Single Spotlight):** one team, one glow, one crowd — intimate and viral-feeling.
- **Slides 3–5 (The Structure Builds):** the frame widens — six teams, two rival spotlights, a scaled broadcast operation. This is where the "category, not a moment" argument is made visually.
- **Slides 6–7 (The Championship + Takeaway):** a symmetrical, non-partisan championship-night image and a calm closing statement.

A single recurring graphic device ties all seven slides together: a thin horizontal gold line along the bottom edge of every slide, carrying small circular "league nodes." The nodes track the structural arc described in the approved copy:
- Slide 1–2: **one** solid gold node, alone, center-left on the line (the single spotlight team).
- Slide 3: the line fans out to **six** small gold nodes spaced evenly in a shallow arc just above the line (the six-team league appears).
- Slide 4: **two** of the six nodes brighten/pulse slightly larger than the other four (the two rival draws), while the other four remain steady and equal — no node is ever shown larger than these two or dimmed to suggest elimination.
- Slide 5: a small radiating "signal" mark (three thin concentric arcs, like a broadcast signal, not a logo) appears just above the node arc, centered — representing the scaled media deal, with no numerals or text baked into the image.
- Slide 6: all six nodes draw thin lines converging to a single still, unlit trophy-shaped silhouette centered directly above the JustPreneur wordmark — the championship, with the convergence drawn symmetrically (no node's line is heavier, brighter, or more direct than another, so no team visually "wins").
- Slide 7: the same six-node-to-trophy convergence graphic, now fully resolved and evenly lit, holds steady as the closing visual — the finished category structure.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

Every slide follows the same top-to-bottom hierarchy:
1. Headline or body-copy block — upper portion of frame (and subheadline directly beneath the headline on Slide 1 only)
2. Photographic focal environment — lower half to two-thirds of frame
3. Thin gold line + node graphic — fixed bottom-edge band, state advancing per slide as described above
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, fixed bottom-center position, identical placement across all seven slides, sitting just above the gold line/node band

## 3. Exact placement of headline, supporting copy, imagery, and branding

**Slide 1 — Cover**
- Visual: a single stadium at night, one tight spotlight beam cutting through darkness onto home plate, the rest of the park in deep navy shadow — one team, one glow (see `slide-1-cover`)
- Headline: "The Bananas Didn't Just Go Viral. They Built a League." — y ≈ 170–480px, centered or left-aligned, max width ~900px, editorial serif, warm white, with "League" optionally set in gold as the single accent word of the slide
- Subheadline: "Six teams. One championship. A format turned into a category." — y ≈ 500–620px, directly beneath headline, smaller sans-serif, warm white at slightly reduced opacity (~85%)
- Gold line + one solid node, center-left: fixed bottom band, y ≈ 1290–1310px
- JustPreneur wordmark: bottom-center, y ≈ 1250px

**Slide 2 — The Moment Everyone Remembers**

**Slide 3 — Then the Structure Showed Up**

- Gold line fans to six evenly lit nodes, wordmark bottom-center

**Slide 4 — Rivals Need Their Own Draw**

- Gold line: two of the six nodes brighten slightly larger (the rival draws), wordmark bottom-center

**Slide 5 — The Media Deal Scaled With the League**

- Gold line + six nodes with small radiating broadcast-signal mark centered above them, wordmark bottom-center

**Slide 6 — Then Came the Championship**
- Body copy: "On October 10, 2026, the Savannah Bananas faced the Texas Tailgaters at Nationals Park in Washington, D.C. — the first-ever Banana Bowl, the BBCL's championship finale, streaming on Disney+." — y ≈ 160–620px, left-aligned, max width ~880px
- Visual: a wide, perfectly symmetrical night shot of a full stadium (styled to evoke Nationals Park without any readable signage, scoreboard digits, or score), two dugouts lit identically on left and right, home plate centered and gently spotlit, crowd filling the stands evenly on both sides — strictly no scoreboard numerals, no team logos, no visual cue favoring either side
- Gold line: all six nodes draw thin, equal-weight lines converging to a single still, unlit trophy-shaped silhouette centered above the wordmark — the structural "arrival" beat, no winner implied
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center, directly beneath the trophy silhouette

**Slide 7 — Final Takeaway**
- Headline/statement: "A league doesn't need its founder to win. It needs rivals, rules, and a championship that outlasts any one spotlight. That's the category move — not going viral once, building the structure others can compete inside." — treated as the primary large statement, y ≈ 220–760px, left-aligned or centered, editorial serif, warm white, max width ~900px
- Visual: a calm, wide, mostly-empty stadium at dawn/blue-hour (post-event stillness), home plate gently lit, no people, no scoreboard, no banners — a quiet "the structure remains after the spotlight fades" closing image
- Gold line + six nodes fully converged and evenly lit on the trophy silhouette, held steady as the closing state
- JustPreneur wordmark bottom-center, slightly more prominent as the closing slide

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline/statement typeface (Slides 1, 7): a bold, high-contrast editorial serif with a sports-business-press feel (Tiempos Headline / Canela / Freight Display style) — premium journalism, not a motivational template.
- Subheadline (Slide 1): a lighter-weight companion sans or a smaller cut of the same serif, clearly secondary to the headline.
- Body copy typeface (Slides 2–6): a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne / Inter / Neue Haas style) for maximum mobile legibility, especially given the longer factual sentences on Slides 3–6.
- No kicker labels, no slide numbers, no date tags added as separate small text anywhere — all facts (2026, six teams, February 24 2026, October 10 2026) appear only within the approved copy sentences themselves.
- Headline sizing: no line should exceed ~36–40 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on the 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, line-height ~130–140%, left-aligned (never justified). Slides 3 and 6 carry the longest sentences in the set (team list; date/venue detail) — give these two slides slightly more vertical copy allowance and a heavier contrast overlay rather than shrinking type below the minimum.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A range) — dominant background/night-sky/shadow tone, identical across all seven slides
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces (dugouts, stadium structure, broadcast equipment)
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 / #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds
- Energy gold (#C9A24B–#D4AF37) — stadium floodlight glow, the bottom line/node/trophy graphic, "League" accent word on Slide 1, wordmark. Note: energy gold also reads naturally as the "banana yellow" of the subject matter — this is an intentional, disciplined overlap, not an added color. No separate banana-yellow or cartoon-brand-yellow should be introduced; everything stays inside the existing navy/gray/white/gold brand system.
- No red, no green, no team-specific colors, no additional accents — keep the palette disciplined so all six teams read as structurally equal and the carousel stays one continuous brand set.

## 6. Image treatment

- All imagery graded toward navy/steel/gold night-stadium lighting so every slide feels like one continuous visual world.
- Apply a subtle dark navy gradient overlay (~40–55% opacity) behind every text zone on every slide to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying photo, with a heavier overlay (~55–60%) behind the longer paragraphs on Slides 3 and 6.
- No text, logos, team names, scoreboard digits, real identifiable faces, or dollar/number call-outs generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy and all six team names are added in a design tool afterward per this brief. Shane Victorino and Ryan Howard are referenced only through the approved copy text and the generic "Prime Time Coach" patch detail on Slide 4; never through a rendered likeness or jersey with their names/numbers.
- Slide 6 must be composed with strict left/right visual symmetry (lighting, crowd density, dugout treatment) so nothing in the image itself can be read as declaring a winner. No scoreboard, scoreboard digits, or score of any kind appears in any slide.
- No depiction of Fans First Entertainment, ownership structures, financial figures, stock charts, or "infrastructure" imagery (server racks, balance sheets, etc.) anywhere in the set — this is a category/market-structure story told through stadium, dugout, and broadcast imagery only.
- No reference to or depiction of the ABC showcase game or Autzen Stadium anywhere in the set — the only stadium environment on Slides 6–7 is styled toward Nationals Park/the Banana Bowl.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of at least 96px from every edge. Instagram's feed UI can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px of the preview — keep headline/body anchor points starting no higher than ~150–180px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge (the gold line/node band itself can sit lower, in the ~1290–1310px band, since it is a thin graphic device rather than text).
- Keep body copy line length under ~880px wide (leaving ≥100px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading, with extra care on Slides 3, 4, 5, and 6 where body copy runs longest.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080 x 1350) on all seven slides.
- Identical thin gold line position along the bottom edge on every slide; node state advances in this exact order: one solid node (1, 2) → fans to six evenly lit nodes (3) → two of six nodes brighten/enlarge equally, four remain steady (4) → signal mark appears above the six nodes (5) → all six nodes converge via equal-weight lines to a single still trophy silhouette (6) → same converged/trophy state held steady as closing image (7).
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center, just above the gold line/node band) on every slide, slightly more prominent only on the final Slide 7.
- Identical navy/steel/gold night-stadium color grade across all seven backgrounds — no deliberate grade shift in this set (unlike some prior carousels), since the story is continuous rather than a flashback.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention on Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 share the headline/serif treatment.
- Slide 4's two coach/dugout zones and Slide 6's two team dugouts must each be built as mirrored, equal-weight pairs — matched lighting, matched framing, matched scale — so no slide visually favors one side over the other.
- No slide may include a rendered likeness of Shane Victorino, Ryan Howard, or any other real named person; no slide may include a scoreboard, score, or dollar figure.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) text over the navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, especially over the floodlit crowd scene on Slide 2 and the broadcast-monitor glow on Slide 5.
- Gold (#C9A24B) is reserved for large display text (the word "League" on Slide 1, the bottom line/node/trophy graphic, the wordmark) on dark backgrounds only — do not set gold body copy at small sizes, where contrast drops.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas.
- Slides 3 and 6 carry the longest paragraphs in the set (full team list; date/venue/stream detail) — prioritize a slightly heavier gradient overlay behind text on these two slides.
