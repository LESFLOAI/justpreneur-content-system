## 2. Entrepreneurial Angle

**The Halo Product / Hidden Revenue Layer** — The viral pop-up bar everyone lines up for may not be the real business. Industry trade press has long described Miracle/Sippin' Santa's licensing network — recipes, décor, glassware, training, all sold to independent bars — as a distribution channel for founder Greg Boehm's actual manufacturing company, Cocktail Kingdom. The 2026 launch of "The Haunting" (the format's first expansion beyond Christmas, into Halloween) is read here as the same playbook being cloned into a second calendar season — evidence the model itself, not just one season of it, is repeatable. Lesson: before scaling your most visible, most-loved offer, decide what it's actually selling behind the counter — a beloved "halo" product can be the demand-generation engine for a separate, less glamorous core revenue line, by design.

The real question for any founder: what's your halo offer, and what's your actual revenue engine? Are they the same thing — or should they be two different offers entirely?
