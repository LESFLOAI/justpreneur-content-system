## GUARDRAILS (read before generating or laying out)

1. **No combined or implied counts.** Miracle = "150+ locations, 2026." Sippin' Santa = "50+ locations, 2026." These must stay visually and numerically separate — never shown side-by-side as if they sum to one figure, never on the same counter/badge graphic.
2. **No count or map for The Haunting.** Never show a number, pin count, or map implying a specific total of countries/cities for The Haunting. The only approved descriptor is "multiple countries, confirmed US + UK" — rendered as text, not as a map graphic or location badge.
3. **No real likenesses.** Greg Boehm must never be depicted photorealistically. Slide 4 (founder reveal) uses an anonymized silhouette figure or a pure text/graphic treatment — not an attempted portrait.
4. **No logo lockups on Slides 5–6.** Slide 5 (industry read) and Slide 6 (our analysis) must not show a Cocktail Kingdom logo, Miracle logo, or any lockup that would visually read as a company admitting the strategy itself. Imagery stays generic/stock-style (unbranded glassware, unbranded shipping boxes, abstract icons) so the visual never undercuts the on-slide attribution language ("industry trade press has described…" / "our read… not a confirmed company statement").
5. **No fabricated brand marks.** No real Miracle, Sippin' Santa, The Haunting, or Cocktail Kingdom logos/trade dress reproduced anywhere — all bar/glassware imagery is generic and evocative, not a copy of real branding.

## 1. Overall creative concept

A "two-sided ledger" carousel: every slide that isn't a pure-type slide is built as a literal split panel — one side is the visible, viral, warmly-lit world (the bar); the other side is the quieter, colder, structural world (the glassware/manufacturing business) that the story argues is where the real money lives. The split itself *is* the thesis, so it repeats as a locked visual grammar across the set rather than being a one-off device on Slide 1.

Tone: premium business-culture editorial — think a Puck or The Information treatment of a Christmas pop-up bar, not a holiday party flyer and not a generic "hustle quote" template. Festive warmth (string lights, amber glass glow) is allowed as texture, but it never slides into kitschy holiday-card territory; the navy/steel/gold brand system keeps it adult and analytical.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

Every split-panel slide (1, 3, 4, 5) uses the same locked grammar:
1. **Vertical split line** at center, dividing the frame into two equal 540px-wide panels (left = the visible/bar side, right = the structural/glassware side — except Slide 3, where left/right carry the two spinoffs per the copy's own direction).
2. **Headline/kicker band** sits across the top ~22% of the frame, bridging both panels in one continuous text block — visually stating that the split is one idea, not two.
3. **Supporting copy band** sits in a dedicated negative-space pocket (either centered across the split line, or anchored low on whichever panel is less visually busy).
4. **Brand footer band** (bottom ~10%) — JustPreneur wordmark, centered, identical across all 8 slides.

Full-bleed slides (2, 6, 7, 8) keep the same three-band logic (headline band / image or graphic stage / footer band) but without the vertical split, so they read as deliberate rhythm breaks: Slide 2 (the viral surface consuming the whole frame), Slide 6 (pure iconography, no photo), Slide 7 (typography only), Slide 8 (typography + one small icon).

Focal hierarchy on every slide: (1) headline/kicker, (2) the one fact or line the slide exists to deliver, (3) image/icon mood, (4) JustPreneur wordmark last.

- JustPreneur wordmark: bottom-center, baseline at y≈1290px, consistent ~34–40px cap height, warm white or gold, identical across all 8 slides. No tagline, no slide numbers, no dates, no handles.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Headlines/kickers:** bold condensed grotesque/sans (Archivo Black, Druk, or Neue Haas Grotesk Bold family) — consistent across all 8 slides, no decorative or script fonts anywhere (keeps this from tipping into "holiday flyer" territory).
- **Body/supporting copy:** clean humanist sans (Inter/Söhne or equivalent), warm white, line height 1.3–1.4x, never smaller than ~34px effective size at this resolution.
- **Numerals ("150+," "50+"):** always set in the bold brand sans, large enough to anchor as a standalone visual beat on Slides 2 and 3 — but never placed in the same text block or visual frame as each other, per the guardrail above.
- **Disclaimer/attribution line (Slide 6):** smaller weight, steel-gray, italics permitted here only (it's a short qualifying line, not a headline) to visually mark it as a caveat.
- Maximum two typefaces per slide, maximum three weight/size steps. No italic headlines.

## 5. Color palette

Brand default, warmed slightly for the cocktail/holiday subject without losing the editorial register:

- Deep navy base: `#0B1220`
- Steel gray (secondary text, hairline split, dividers): `#6B7280`
- Warm white (primary body text): `#F5F1E8`
- Energy gold (headlines, numerals, JustPreneur wordmark, key accents): `#D4AF37`
- Warm amber glow (cocktail/string-light accent, used only as image lighting, never as text color): `#E8A33D`

Palette logic: the "bar" panel/side of every split always carries the warm amber glow; the "glassware/manufacturing" panel/side always stays cooler — navy and steel, with only a single practical light source (not amber) — so the visual temperature itself reinforces the thesis (warm = visible/viral, cool = structural/profitable) without needing extra labels.

## 6. Image treatment

Cinematic, editorial-premium photography style — think a trade-magazine feature, not a party flyer. Shallow depth of field, soft practical lighting, light film grain for texture. Every bar/glassware image is generic and invented: a stock-style pop-up bar interior, stock-style glassware shelving, stock-style shipping boxes — never a real, identifiable venue, real storefront signage, or real brand marks. No readable text anywhere in generated imagery (any wording is added in layout). No real people's faces; where a figure is needed (Slide 4, Slide 2's bartender background staffage if any), use silhouette, back-of-head, or heavily backlit anonymized figures only — no attempt at any real person's likeness, and no duplicate/cloned figures.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

For all 8 slides (1080 x 1350):
- Side margins: all text and critical graphic elements at least 80px from left/right edges.
- Top margin: headline/kicker baseline no higher than 90px from the top; nothing critical in the top ~40px (feed-thumbnail crop risk).
- Bottom margin: JustPreneur wordmark and any text at least 100px from the bottom edge; nothing critical in the bottom ~60px.
- Center-safe zone: each slide's primary message must read correctly even if only the middle 80% of the frame is visible.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Same headline/kicker-band + footer-band grid on all 8 slides; split-panel grammar locked for Slides 1, 3, 4, 5; full-bleed/no-split grammar locked for Slides 2, 6, 7, 8 — no slide invents a third layout type.
- Same JustPreneur wordmark size, color, and bottom-center position on all 8 slides.
- Warm-amber-left/cool-navy-right (or equivalent paired framing) temperature logic repeats on every split slide — never inverted mid-carousel.
- Same two-typeface system throughout; the italic treatment is reserved exclusively for the Slide 6 disclaimer line and must not appear elsewhere.
- No slide introduces a new decorative motif (border style, icon style, badge shape) that doesn't recur elsewhere in the set.
- Numeral treatment ("150+," "50+") uses identical type size/weight/color on Slides 2 and 3 so the two counts read as deliberately parallel but separate — never merged.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (`#F5F1E8`) on navy (`#0B1220`): contrast ratio ≈ 15:1 — passes WCAG AAA at all sizes.
- Gold (`#D4AF37`) on navy: contrast ratio ≈ 7.3:1 — passes AA for normal text, AAA for large text; reserved for headlines/kickers/numerals only, never full paragraphs.
- Steel gray (`#6B7280`) disclaimer text on navy: contrast ratio ≈ 4.6:1 — meets AA for the Slide 6 caveat line only because it's set large enough (not shrunk below ~34px effective); do not shrink further.
- Amber glow (`#E8A33D`) is restricted to image lighting/background accent only, never set as text color — avoids a legibility failure against warm white body copy.
- No flashing/strobing implied in any prompt; all lighting is static warm/cool glow.
- Minimum effective text size kept legible in a 3-inch-wide phone feed thumbnail on every slide.
