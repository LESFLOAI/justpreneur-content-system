# Approved Facts Block — Manhattan Two-Tier Office Market

**Story:** Manhattan's trophy-tier office rents vs. the broader Class A market, Q3 2026.
**Verified by:** Fact Verifier
**Status:** Cleared with qualifications (one claim rewritten; see below)

## Approved facts (verbatim, safe for downstream use)

> Manhattan Class A office average asking rents reached $85.45 per square foot in the third quarter of 2026 (quarter ended September 30, 2026) — an all-time high that surpasses pre-pandemic levels, according to a Colliers report cited by The Real Deal on October 1, 2026. Tenants leased just over 10 million square feet of Manhattan office space in Q3 2026, marking the fourth consecutive quarter above the 10-million-square-foot mark — a streak last seen in 2002, per Colliers. Overall Manhattan office availability fell to 13.4% in Q3 2026, down 70 basis points from the prior quarter and 290 basis points year-over-year, the lowest level since 2020, per Colliers. Separately, in April 2026, The Real Deal and Commercial Observer reported that HBeyond, the holding company of Mexican businessman Gonzalo Hevia Baillères, signed a 10-year lease for 5,063 square feet on the 50th floor of 9 West 57th Street at $327.50 per square foot — reported at the time as the highest office rent on record in Manhattan, surpassing a prior $320/sf benchmark at One Vanderbilt. In June 2026, The Real Deal reported that landlord Soloviev was seeking (not yet confirmed as signed) as much as $400 per square foot for remaining space in the same building. Trophy-tier office availability in Manhattan has been reported in the single digits to low double digits through 2026 (approximately 6-10%, per JLL/Colliers), well below the market-wide 13.4% rate, underscoring a bifurcated market between top-tier trophy towers and the broader Class A/B stock.
