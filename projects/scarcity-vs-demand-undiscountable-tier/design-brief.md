**Format:** Instagram Carousel, 7 slides, 1080 x 1350 (portrait)

**Core tension (from approved copy):** a single market produces a market-wide average ($85.45/sf), a signed trophy-tier deal ($327.50/sf), and an unconfirmed trophy-tier ask ($400/sf) — the piece teaches viewers to read scarcity tiers, not headline averages.

## 0. Hard constraints carried into every visual decision

1. **No reproduction of real, specific building architecture.** 9 West 57th Street (distinctive black-granite-and-gold Emery Roth tower) and One Vanderbilt (distinctive crown/spire) are real, visually identifiable buildings named in the copy. No generated image attempts to depict either building's actual recognizable facade, crown, or signature material finish. All trophy-tower imagery is a generic, representative "trophy-tier Manhattan high-rise" form — evocative of the category, not a photographic or illustrative likeness of a specific real tower.
2. **No real company logos or wordmarks.** HBeyond, Soloviev, Colliers, JLL, The Real Deal, and Commercial Observer are real named entities. No generated image contains their logos, wordmarks, letterhead, or any fabricated substitute logo standing in for them. Any signage in frame is blank, turned away, or softly out of focus.
3. **No real-person likenesses.** No named executive is rendered as a recognizable face. Any human presence is hands-only, rear-view, silhouetted, or distant/anonymized — never a posed portrait that could be read as a specific real person.
4. **No fabricated news-outlet chyrons, mastheads, "BREAKING" banners, citation marks, or dateline stamps baked into any image.** Every number, date, and source line is added afterward as JustPreneur's own type layer (see §3, §11). Source attributions (Colliers, The Real Deal, JLL, Commercial Observer) appear only as small type-layer credit lines, never faked inside the generated art.
5. **No text baked into any generated image.** All headlines, figures, kickers, and body copy are added after generation as type layers — see the separate layout prompt in §11. This also avoids AI text-rendering artifacts on a numbers-heavy data story where garbled digits would be especially damaging to credibility.
6. **Numbers must stay exact.** $85.45/sf, 10M+ sf, 13.4%, ~6–10%, $327.50/sf, $400/sf, and all dates/attributions are carried verbatim into the layout layer — no rounding, no paraphrasing.

## 1. Overall creative concept

This is a **business-data editorial piece** — the visual language of a Bloomberg or Real Deal market-brief chart package, not a generic motivational "hustle" carousel and not a real-estate-agent listing flyer. The core visual idea: **one skyline, two economies.** Manhattan Class A office is rendered as a single, continuous steel-and-glass skyline texture running through the carousel, but the carousel visually separates an "average-market" register (cooler steel-gray, denser, more uniform massing) from a "trophy-tier" register (warmer gold-lit, taller, sparser, more negative space) — so a viewer feels the tiering before they finish reading the numbers.

A recurring infographic device — the **Tier Ladder** — anchors every interior slide (2–6) in the same position (lower-right safe margin, a slim vertical rail with three horizontal tick marks, bottom to top: **$85 MARKET → $327.50 SIGNED → $400 ASK**). As the story climbs tiers, the relevant node lights gold while passed nodes stay lit and future nodes stay dim. Critically, the **$400 ASK** node is rendered as a **dashed/outlined gold tick, never solid fill**, on every slide where it appears (Slides 6 and 7) — a deliberate, persistent visual cue that this number was never confirmed as a signed transaction, preserving the copy's own factual caution ("Not signed. Not confirmed. Just the ask.") in the design language itself, not just the text.

Slide 7 breaks from the photographic skyline entirely and resolves as a clean navy-and-gold data/quote card — the single moment of direct, uncluttered address, holding the three-number comparison and the closing question with maximum legibility and zero competing texture.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

- **Cover (Slide 1):** Full headline centered in the top ~60% of the frame on a clean navy field, with a restrained, softly blurred dusk Manhattan skyline silhouette anchoring the lower third — one taller, gold-lit tower rises quietly above a cooler steel-gray cluster, visually seeding the "one tier" theme before any copy is read. No Tier Ladder yet (the story hasn't started).
- **Interior slides (2–6):** Top safe zone (~y:120–260) holds a short gold kicker (the slide's defining number or label) in large, bold type. Below it (~y:280–520), 1–3 lines of body copy in warm white, word-for-word from approved copy, including the source attribution as a smaller credit line beneath the main copy block. The generated skyline/interior visual occupies the lower ~50–55% of the frame, full-bleed to the side margins, with the top safe zone reserved for type. The Tier Ladder sits in the lower-right safe margin, vertically spanning roughly y:950–1250, clear of the body-copy block and clear of the JustPreneur wordmark. JustPreneur wordmark sits small, centered, in the bottom safe margin on every slide.
- **Final takeaway (Slide 7):** No photographic background — a clean navy-and-gold data/quote-card layout. The completed Tier Ladder (all three nodes shown, $400 still dashed-outline) sits at the top-right as a quiet "here's the full ladder" callback. The three-line number comparison ("$85 is the market. $327.50 is what a scarce tier commands. $400 is what scarcity lets you ask.") sits large and centered. The instruction line ("Stop competing on demand. Build toward undiscountable.") sits below in a smaller warm-white weight. The closing question ("Which tier is your pricing in?") is set apart in gold as the final line before the wordmark.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Headline/cover typeface:** bold, semicondensed grotesk (same family used across prior JustPreneur carousels — keep consistent). Sentence case for the full headline on Slide 1.
- **Kicker (the defining number, interior slides):** large, bold, gold, set as the number itself ("$85.45/SF," "10M+ SF," "13.4%," "$327.50/SF," "$400/SF ASK") — numerals are the spine of this story and must register at a glance even in a fast scroll. "$400/SF ASK" kicker on Slide 6 may use a subtly lighter/outlined weight on "ASK" specifically to echo the dashed-ladder-node distinction, without inventing new copy.
- **Body copy:** warm white, regular-to-medium weight, generous line-height, word-for-word from the approved copy — no paraphrasing, no shortening. Source/credit lines are set smaller and in a muted steel-gray, clearly subordinate to the main copy block, never competing with it.
- **Slide 7 numbers/quote:** largest single-block treatment in the whole carousel — the three-number sentence in warm white, the instruction line smaller warm white, the closing question in gold, set apart as the final line. This is the "save this" slide and should be the most typographically confident frame.
- No script fonts, no literal "for lease" sign-lettering pastiche, no stock-chart-cliché tickers anywhere — this stays in JustPreneur's own premium-business-editorial voice, not a simulated Bloomberg terminal screenshot.

## 5. Color palette

**Brand base (unchanged):** deep navy, steel gray, warm white, energy gold — used for all text, kicker, wordmark, Tier Ladder, and UI chrome across every slide.

**Story-specific accent layer (background photographic/skyline art only, not text):**
- Cooler steel-gray, denser massing — "market-wide average" register (Slides 2, and the market-wide half of Slide 4)
- Warmer gold-lit, sparser, taller massing — "trophy-tier" register (Slides 5, 6, and the trophy half of Slide 4)
- Slide 3 (demand) sits in a warm-neutral middle register — busy, energetic, but not yet tiered into gold-vs-gray, since the copy at this point is about volume, not scarcity-tier pricing
- Slide 7 is a pure brand navy field with gold accents only — no photographic color grade at all

## 6. Image treatment

- Photographic/editorial-architectural texture throughout — believable glass-and-steel material, dusk-to-night city light, shallow-to-moderate depth of field, rather than flat vector/illustration or infographic-clipart style.
- Trophy-tier slides (5, 6, and the right half of 4) get a warmer, gold-rim-light grade with more negative space between towers (scarcity read as literal empty sky around fewer, taller forms); market-wide slides (2, and the left half of 4) get a cooler, denser, more uniform grade (abundance read as literal packed massing).
- No text baked into any generated image anywhere — all copy, kickers, numbers, credit lines, and the Tier Ladder are added as layers after generation (see §11).
- No reproduction of 9 West 57th Street's or One Vanderbilt's actual recognizable facades/crowns, and no real or fabricated logos for HBeyond, Soloviev, Colliers, JLL, The Real Deal, or Commercial Observer (see §0.1–§0.2).
- Human presence, where used at all, is hands-only, rear-view, distant/anonymized, or silhouetted — no posed portraits, no recognizable faces (see §0.3).

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

**Carousel (1080x1350):** Minimum 72px margin on all sides for any text or wordmark. Interior slides (2–6): keep the top ~38% of the frame (through y ≈ 520) clean and low-detail for the kicker + body copy + credit-line stack; the generated visual occupies the lower ~50–55%, full-bleed to the side margins; reserve a clear, uncluttered pocket at lower-right (roughly x:850–1010, y:950–1250) for the Tier Ladder so it never overlaps body copy or the wordmark. Cover (Slide 1): headline block centered with equal top/bottom breathing room, no ladder. Slide 7: entirely typographic — reserve generous margin around the number-comparison block (at least 100px top/bottom beyond the 72px minimum) so it reads as a deliberate data/quote card, not a cramped text dump. JustPreneur wordmark centered in the bottom safe margin, identical size/position, on all 7 slides.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Tier Ladder position, node size, and gold-solid/gold-dashed/dim styling stay pixel-identical across Slides 2–6 (and its completed form on Slide 7) — only which nodes are lit (and the Node-3 dashed-vs-dim state) changes.
- Kicker style (position, size, gold color, numeral-forward treatment) stays identical across all five interior slides; only the number/label content changes.
- Credit-line style (size, steel-gray color, position directly beneath body copy) stays identical on every slide that carries one (Slides 2–6).
- The cool-gray/market vs. warm-gold/trophy grade alternation (§6) must stay visually consistent every time a given register reappears, so a viewer feels the tiering rhythm without rereading the copy.
- JustPreneur wordmark treatment (size, position, opacity) is identical across all 7 slides.
- Grotesk type family is identical throughout — no font switching between slides.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm-white text (#F5F3EE-equivalent) on a navy scrim (#0B1E32-equivalent) at ≥70% scrim opacity clears WCAG AA for large text at carousel scale.
- Gold kicker/number text (energy-gold, e.g., #D4A017-equivalent) is reserved for short, large-size labels (kickers, the Tier Ladder, the Slide 7 closing question) — never for full paragraph body copy, since gold-on-navy body text at small sizes drops below comfortable contrast on compressed mobile screens.
- Body copy and credit lines are never placed directly over high-frequency photographic texture (glass reflections, dense window-grid patterns) without a navy scrim between text and image — scrim is mandatory on every interior slide, not optional.
- Minimum body-copy size renders no smaller than is comfortably legible at arm's length on a phone; Slide 5 and Slide 6 (longer copy blocks with embedded figures and building references) should be checked first during local assembly since they carry the most facts per slide.
- The dashed/outlined $400 ladder node must remain visible against both the cool-gray and gold backgrounds behind it — confirm during local assembly that the dashed gold outline doesn't disappear against a busy background; add a subtle dark scrim behind the ladder pocket if needed.

## Open design decisions for local finish
