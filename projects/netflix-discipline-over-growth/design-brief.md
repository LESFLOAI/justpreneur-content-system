## 0. Hard constraints carried into every visual decision

1. **No real executives depicted.** Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters (named in the Slide 2 memo attribution) must never appear as faces, recognizable likenesses, or named silhouettes. Any human presence in generated art is hands-only, abstract silhouette, or fully iconographic — never a posed portrait of anyone identifiable. Their names and title appear only as added typography (the memo attribution line), never requested inside an AI image prompt.
2. **No real logos or trademarked marks.** Netflix is a real, named company and its wordmark/logo/brand "N" mark are trademarks. No image-generation prompt asks for the Netflix logo, Netflix's red/black brand system, a streaming-app interface styled to resemble Netflix specifically, or any show/title art. All platform references in generated art use neutral, abstract, streaming-agnostic iconography (generic screen panels, generic org-chart/building shapes) — never a literal mark. The word "Netflix" itself is added only as typography in the design tool (it appears in the approved copy), never rendered by an image model.
3. **Primary visual metaphor: deliberate pruning, not crisis damage.** The spine of this carousel is a **healthy, thriving plant/tree being pruned with precision** — never wilting, broken, or diseased. This single device carries the entire editorial logic: a company that is winning still choosing to cut, calmly and on purpose, before anything forces it to. Avoid any imagery suggesting distress, collapse, or emergency (no fire, no falling charts, no red warning color, no cracked/broken objects) — the whole point is that nothing is broken.
4. **Dense factual copy must not compromise mobile legibility.** Slides 2, 3, and 5 carry longer factual text (quote + attribution; stat + context; comparison stat). Each uses a two-tier hierarchy — a short bold lead element at larger size, then supporting detail in a smaller but still clearly legible size — so no slide becomes a wall of same-size text. No wording is trimmed, reworded, or paraphrased; only size/weight/line-break hierarchy is applied to the verbatim approved copy.
5. **No invented labels beyond what's implied by the approved copy's own structure.** Use only the organizing words already implied by the copy itself (e.g., "The Memo," "The Number," "Where It Landed," "The Context," "What Stays," "The Takeaway," "Your Move" — short chyron labels that restate, not add to, each slide's actual content). No slide-count badges ("1/8"), no dates beyond the one already in the approved copy (Oct 14, 2026), no extra citation marks beyond what's in Slide 2's own attribution, no production marks.
6. **JustPreneur wordmark must be visible and correctly spelled** on every slide, in a small, consistent position, per §3/§7.
7. **No fabricated numbers or claims.** All figures (~1,000 jobs, ~6%, ~16,000 FTEs, ~450 cut across two rounds, ~11,000-person workforce) must render only as approved text added in the design tool — never as a different number invented by an image model's background data-vis.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a **business/culture-media explainer**, visually closer to a premium trade-press graphics package (Bloomberg/Puck-style explainer card) than a motivational quote template — appropriate for a story about operating discipline at a media company, not a lifestyle moment.

The carousel runs on a single **visual spine: the pruned tree**. A clean, premium, abstract tree/plant form — full, green, clearly thriving — appears across the carousel with a precise gold pruning cut (never a jagged break) marking the action. The tree's state **does not degrade** slide to slide; if anything it looks slightly fuller by the midpoint (Slide 6, "What Stays") to visually reinforce reallocation rather than shrinkage. This single device lets the reader feel "healthy thing, deliberately shaped" rather than "wounded thing, forced to shrink."

**Slide 7 (The Takeaway)** strips all of this away for a **text-only, high-contrast minimal treatment** — flat deep navy, no tree, no icons — so the piece's thesis statement lands with maximum clarity near the carousel's end, matching prior JustPreneur carousels' "cleared board" convention for the pivot/thesis beat.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

- Slides 1, 7: full-bleed minimal background, headline vertically centered — the two "punch" slides (hook and thesis).
- Slides 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8: a contained graphic motif occupies roughly the **top 30–38%** of the frame, with the remaining **62–70%** kept as a clean, low-detail navy field reserved for the two-tier text block. This ratio protects readability on the slides carrying the heaviest factual copy (2, 3, 5).
- JustPreneur wordmark: small, steady, top-left corner, identical position/size/opacity on every slide (1–8) — a consistent watermark presence, never competing with headline weight. Added as a text/logo layer in the design tool, not relied upon from image generation.
- Slide numbering/arrows: none added — Instagram's native carousel dots communicate position; no custom "1/8" labels per §0.5.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Carousel headline/lead family:** bold grotesk/sans (the family already established across JustPreneur posts — a Neue-Haas-Grotesk-Condensed-style weight), sentence case except where the approved copy itself uses caps (Slide 1's all-caps headline is preserved exactly as approved), warm white.
- **Slide 1 cover headline:** heaviest weight available, all-caps exactly as approved ("THE BEST TIME TO CUT IS WHEN YOU DON'T HAVE TO"), centered, warm white, with the second line "Netflix just proved it." set smaller directly beneath in sentence case.
- **Slide headers (2–6, 8):** small, bold, title-case chyron label ("The Memo," "The Number," "Where It Landed," "The Context," "What Stays," "Your Move"), warm white at ~85% opacity, positioned top-left beneath the JustPreneur wordmark.
- **Lead lines / big stats:** bold grotesk, noticeably larger size than supporting body — the "first thing your eye reads" after the header. On Slide 3, the numeral "~1,000" leads at the largest optical size on that slide.
- **Quote styling (Slide 2):** the memo quote set in a slightly heavier weight with subtle quotation marks as a typographic flourish (not script/serif), still within the single grotesk system; attribution line set smaller, regular weight, below.
- **Body/supporting detail (2, 3, 5):** same grotesk family, regular/medium weight, smaller size, generous line-height (≥1.3x) for comfortable phone scanning.
- **Gold pull-lines** ("Not retreat. Reallocation." / "Trim by choice. Not by crisis." / the closing echo sentence on Slide 7): set in energy gold, same grotesk family, bold, slightly larger than surrounding body text so each lands as a standalone emphasis moment — never gold for full paragraphs.
- **Slide 7 thesis lines:** heaviest weight, largest size in the entire carousel (this is the pivot/thesis beat), warm white, with the final sentence (matching the cover) in a subtle gold tint as the one color break.
- **Slide 4's four-term list:** set as four short, evenly weighted tags/lines of equal size — no single department emphasized over another, since the copy itself gives them equal billing.
- No script fonts, no decorative/legal-document lettering — keep the single editorial grotesk system consistent across all 8 slides.

## 5. Color palette

**Brand base (unchanged):** deep navy, steel gray, warm white, energy gold — used for all text, wordmark, headers, and chyron-style labels throughout.

**Story-specific coding layer (recurring motifs only):**
- **Tree/plant motif (slides 1, 6, 8):** deep navy base/background, the tree itself rendered in a calm, healthy steel-gray-to-warm-white gradient foliage (never a literal green — keep it inside brand palette by treating foliage as a cool steel-white tone), with the pruning cut/shears and any new-growth tip rendered in energy gold. Gold = "the deliberate, confident action" throughout.
- **Memo/document motif (slide 2):** warm white document shape on navy, subtle gold quote-mark glyph only — no other color.
- **Dot-grid motif (slide 3):** steel-gray dots as the base grid, roughly 6% of dots in energy gold to echo the stat, on a deep navy field.
- **Department icons (slide 4):** warm white line-art icons on navy, equal weight, no color hierarchy between the four (since the copy treats them as a flat list).
- **Comparison bars (slide 5):** the "then" bar in steel gray, the "now" bar in warm white with a thin gold top edge — avoid red/alarm coloring entirely, since this is a scale comparison, not a warning.
- **Slide 7 (thesis):** flat deep navy only, no gold/steel accents except the single closing-sentence gold tint, to maximize contrast at the pivot.
- JustPreneur wordmark: energy gold on navy, identical across all 8 slides.

## 6. Image treatment

- Clean, flat-lit, graphic-design-forward treatment throughout (not photographic/cinematic) — premium motion-graphics-plate quality, consistent with a business/culture trade-press explainer, not lifestyle photography.
- Tree/plant, document, dot-grid, department-icon, comparison-bar, and shears motifs are all rendered as simple, bold, dimensional graphic illustrations with soft directional lighting — never cluttered, never more than 2–3 distinct graphic elements visible per slide's motif zone.
- The tree/plant form stays visually consistent (same silhouette family, same proportions) across slides 1, 6, and 8 so the carousel reads as one continuous visual story, not three unrelated graphics generated independently.
- No text, numerals, logos, or real identifiable people/faces generated inside any AI image — all headers, headlines, body copy, stat numerals, and pull-lines are added in the design tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated art supplies motif, mood, and negative space only.
- Every motif zone is confined to the top 30–38% of each non-punch slide (§2) specifically to preserve the large, clean lower field needed for text — a readability requirement, not just a style choice.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

Keep all text within x: 70px–1010px and y: 90px–1260px on the 1080x1350 canvas (Instagram's standard safe-margin guidance for feed/carousel posts, avoiding UI crop and corner overlap). Specific notes:
- JustPreneur wordmark: top-left, anchored around x: 70–260px, y: 60–110px, identical across all slides.
- Slide headers (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8): directly beneath the wordmark, starting no higher than y: 140px.
- Motif zone: roughly y: 160px–560px (top ~30–38% of frame) on slides 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8.
- Body-copy block on text-heavier slides (2, 3, 5): starts no higher than y: 600px, ends no lower than y: 1240px, with left/right margins held at x: 70px/1010px throughout — never edge-to-edge.
- Centered punch-slide text (1, 7): vertically centered around y: 500–900, horizontally centered with the same x: 70–1010px side margins.
- Slide 4's four-term list: evenly distributed between y: 650px–1150px, each term comfortably within the x: 70–1010px margins, generous vertical spacing between terms so none feels cramped.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Every slide uses the identical grotesk type family and identical navy base — no font or base-color switching across the carousel.
- The gold = "deliberate confident action/emphasis" coding must stay consistent every time it recurs (pruning cut and new-growth tip in slides 1/6/8, 6% dot highlight in slide 3, gold top-edge on the "now" bar in slide 5, pull-lines throughout).
- The tree/plant motif's form must stay visually consistent across slides 1, 6, 8 (same silhouette, same proportions, same "healthy, never wilting" quality) so these three slides read as one continuous visual story.
- JustPreneur wordmark position, size, and opacity stay pixel-identical across all 8 slides.
- Slide headers use identical type treatment (size, weight, top-left position) every time they appear (2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8).
- Slide 7's cleared-board treatment is the only slide that drops the top-left motif zone entirely — this deliberate break signals "this is the thesis, pay attention here."
- No slide ever introduces red, warning-orange, cracked/broken imagery, or any other "crisis" visual cue — this is the one rule that must never be violated anywhere in the set, since it directly contradicts the story's thesis (cutting while strong, not cutting because something broke).

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm-white text (#F5F3EE-equivalent) on deep-navy base (#0B1E32-equivalent) comfortably clears WCAG AA contrast for both large headline text and the smaller body/detail text used on slides 2, 3, 5, provided body text stays at or above roughly 32px cap-height equivalent at 1080px width (text-heavier slides must not shrink text below this floor — if a slide's full approved copy cannot fit above this floor within the body-copy zone in §7, flag for QC rather than shrinking further).
- Energy-gold text/elements are reserved for short pull-lines, single-phrase emphasis ("Not retreat. Reallocation.", "Trim by choice. Not by crisis.", the closing echo sentence) only — never full paragraphs — since gold-on-navy body copy at small sizes loses comfortable contrast on compressed mobile screens.
- Steel-gray is used for icon linework, the dot-grid base, and the "then" comparison bar only, always against the deep-navy base, never as small body text, since steel-on-navy contrast is weaker than warm-white-on-navy at small sizes.
- No text is ever placed directly over the motif's high-detail area (foliage texture, grid dots, icon linework) — a soft navy scrim sits beneath any text that overlaps the motif zone's lower edge.
- Minimum single-line text size: headline/lead lines no smaller than ~56px cap-height equivalent; body/detail text on text-heavier slides (2, 3, 5) no smaller than ~32px cap-height equivalent; slide 7's thesis lines are the largest text in the carousel.
- Slide 4's four-term list uses no smaller than ~40px cap-height equivalent per term, since each term stands alone with generous surrounding space rather than competing with dense paragraph copy.
