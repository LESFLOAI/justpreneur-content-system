## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, cleared with qualifications)

Wake The Tiger, an immersive-art attraction company founded by members of the team behind the Boomtown Fair festival (co-founders Graham MacVoy, CEO, and Luke Mitchell, Chief Creative Officer), is scheduled to open "Absurd City" on October 15, 2026 at Westfield London — Unit 5001, Ariel Way, London, W12 7GF — in the ~80,000 sq ft unit formerly occupied by KidZania. As of this post, the opening has NOT yet occurred; treat as scheduled, not completed.

KidZania's UK operator, Edutainment Operations Limited, announced closure on January 2, 2024 and entered creditors' voluntary liquidation on January 11, 2024 (per The Gazette, the official UK insolvency record), leaving the unit vacant. Tickets for Absurd City have been on sale since July 21, 2026; no delay or postponement has been reported as of October 9, 2026.

Wake The Tiger's original Bristol "Amazement Park" (opened July 2022) has reportedly drawn 500,000+ visitors from 70+ countries — a company-reported figure, attributed to Wake The Tiger, not independently audited. The company is a certified B Corp (per the official B Corp directory) and describes itself as the UK's first visitor attraction to achieve that — a self-description, not an independently verified superlative.
