## Story / Facts Summary

On or around September 28, 2026, Amazon Music and Shakira announced a partnership tied to a free, no-subscription-required worldwide livestream of her October 3, 2026 concert in Madrid — one date within her 11-show "Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran" World Tour residency at the purpose-built, ~50,000-seat Shakira Stadium (September 18–October 11, 2026; Oct. 3 was not the tour's final date). The livestream was available via Prime Video, the Amazon Music app (including Fire TV), and Twitch. Surprise guest Dua Lipa joined Shakira for a duet of "Antología." Amazon has stated — self-reported, not independently verified — that the broadcast became the most-viewed livestream performance by a female artist on Amazon Music to date; no specific viewer count has been independently confirmed and none is used in this package.

As part of the same announcement, AWS, Amazon Music, the women-in-tech nonprofit Laboratoria, and Shakira's foundation Fundación Pies Descalzos launched **"AWS Entrena Latinas,"** a free, Spanish-language training program in cloud computing and AI job skills, starting in Colombia, with a **stated goal (not yet achieved) of reaching 1 million women by 2028**. This is job-skills/certification training for "career opportunities in technology" — explicitly **not** entrepreneurship training.

Separately, the announcement also introduced **"Las Mujeres Facturan con Amazon Music,"** a storefront on Amazon.com spotlighting products from existing women-owned and Latina-owned businesses. Treated as announced/launched; not independently confirmed live-browsable today.

**No claim in this package states a livestream viewer count, calls Oct 3 the tour's final show, calls the concert flatly "sold out," or calls the AWS training program's participants "entrepreneurs."**

## Angle

**"Two Clocks, One Moment"** — Amazon used one global culture moment (the concert livestream) to run two separate business mechanics on two different clocks: a storefront monetizing existing women-owned brands *today*, and a skills-training pipeline aimed at a *2028 goal*. Primary takeaway: the sharpest launches aren't single-purpose — they're time-stacked, pairing an immediate-revenue layer with a long-horizon capacity-building layer inside the same spotlight.
