## 0. Hard constraints carried into every visual decision

1. **No celebrity or named-individual likeness anywhere in generated art.** Shakira and Dua Lipa are real, currently active public figures. No generated image depicts any human face, performer silhouette on a stage, instrument, microphone, or outfit that could be read as either of them (or any specific real person). Their names live in the type layer only, added after image generation, as plain styled text — never as an AI-drawn portrait, stylized caricature, or "inspired-by" likeness. Any crowd/arena imagery in generated backgrounds must be a distant, anonymous, rear-view mass with no identifiable individual and no visible stage/performer at all.
2. **No fabricated or real logos anywhere in generated art.** Amazon, Amazon Music, Prime Video, Twitch, Fire TV, AWS, Laboratoria, and Fundación Pies Descalzos are real, currently operating entities/products. No generated image attempts their real logos, trade dress, wordmarks, app icons, or any invented substitute logo. All brand and program names ("Las Mujeres Facturan con Amazon Music," "AWS Entrena Latinas") live in the type layer only, rendered as plain styled text.
3. **No readable digits, text, or clock-face numerals baked into any generated image.** Every date, figure, program name, and qualifier is added afterward as a precise type layer (see §11). Backgrounds use only abstract arc/light motifs — never a literal clock face with hands and numbers (a literal clock-face icon reads as kitsch, not premium).
4. **Two qualifiers are mandatory, fact-verification-required attributions — not optional fine print, and must never be dropped or shrunk below body-copy size:**
   - Slide 2: `(self-reported)` attached to the "most-viewed livestream performance by a female artist to date" claim.
   - Slide 4: both "This is job-skills training, not entrepreneurship training" (the track-distinction clarifier) and "a goal, not a result yet" (attached to the 1-million-women-by-2028 figure).
   Treat all three as visible clarifying/disclosure lines directly beside their claim, never as a corner credit, never below the smallest body-copy size used elsewhere in the carousel.
5. **No invented data.** Only the approved facts appear: Sept 28, 2026 announcement date; Oct 3, 2026 stream date inside an 11-show Madrid residency (Sept 18–Oct 11) at Shakira Stadium; free via Prime Video, Amazon Music app (incl. Fire TV), and Twitch; Dua Lipa's "Antología" cameo; the self-reported most-viewed-livestream claim; the storefront name and purpose; the AWS Entrena Latinas partners (AWS, Amazon Music, Laboratoria, Fundación Pies Descalzos), language, subject matter (cloud computing and AI job skills), starting country (Colombia), and the 1M-by-2028 stated goal. No new stats, no rounding changes, no restated scope.
6. **The job-skills-training-vs-entrepreneurship-training distinction (Slide 4) and the storefront-vs-training-pipeline distinction (Slide 5) must stay visually unambiguous.** These are the story's central nuances — the visual must support, not blur, either distinction.

## 1. Overall creative concept

This is a **two-track business-mechanics explainer wrapped around one cultural moment** — not a concert recap and not generic founder-hustle content. The core visual idea: **one global spotlight funded two initiatives running on two different clocks, and the sharpest read of the story is in the gap between those clocks.**

Rather than leaning on concert photography or celebrity imagery (which the brand cannot responsibly or reliably render at the fidelity this story deserves), the carousel leans on **abstract arena-light atmosphere matched to a "Two Clocks" arc motif**: one short, bright, fast gold arc of light (Storefront / NOW) and one long, slow, cool steel-blue arc of light (Training / 2028), both visually sourced from the same shared point of light established on the cover — echoing "one moment, two clocks" without ever needing a literal clock face, a performer, or a logo.

A persistent **dual-clock rail** runs across the top safe zone of every interior slide (2–6): two small labeled arc-icon glyphs side by side — **STOREFRONT / NOW** (gold, short arc) and **TRAINING / 2028** (steel-blue, long arc) — each shown in a state consistent with that slide's content. Slide 3 lights up the gold arc alone; Slide 4 lights up the steel-blue arc alone; Slide 5 shows both resolved side by side; Slide 6 shows them interlocked into one calm resolved form.

The cover (Slide 1) sets this up atmospherically — a wide, anonymous arena-at-night light atmosphere with two faint arc-shapes (one gold, one steel-blue) resolving quietly out of the spotlight beams — without yet labeling either track. Slide 7 closes on a minimal, restrained version of the same two arc-glows beside the CTA.

## 4. Typography direction

- **Headline/cover typeface:** bold, semicondensed grotesk (consistent with the brand system used across prior JustPreneur carousels). Slide 1 headline set in the approved all-caps per the copy as written ("ONE CONCERT. TWO BUSINESSES. TWO DIFFERENT CLOCKS.") across three punchy stacked lines; subhead in sentence case directly beneath it, smaller, warm white.
- **Kicker (section labels, interior slides):** all-caps, bold, gold, large enough to anchor a fast scroll.
- **Body copy:** warm white, regular-to-medium weight, generous line height, word-for-word from the approved copy — no paraphrasing, no shortened names, no dropped parenthetical qualifiers. Slide 2 and Slide 4 carry the densest bullet lists in the set; keep them at a slightly smaller but still comfortably legible size on those two slides only, never shrink type globally.
- **Bullet treatment:** a simple dash or thin gold/steel-gray rule per line (matching the brand's existing bullet convention), generous vertical spacing between bullets so a five-bullet slide (Slide 2) never feels cramped — prioritize spacing over fitting everything above the fold if a trade-off arises.
- **Slide 2's `(self-reported)` qualifier:** set in the same line as its claim, in a distinct but fully legible weight (e.g., medium-weight steel-gray immediately following the warm-white claim sentence) — never smaller than body-copy minimum, never in parentheses-as-afterthought gray-on-gray low contrast.
- **Slide 4's two clarifier lines:** each set as its own short line directly beneath its related bullet, in a light steel-gray bordered caption treatment (thin rule above, generous padding), same minimum body-copy size as every other slide — these are disclosures, not footnotes, and must survive a fast scroll-past read.
- **Slide 5 two-lane labels:** "STOREFRONT = REVENUE NOW" and "TRAINING PIPELINE = CAPACITY BY A 2028 GOAL" set in bold caps as lane headers, gold/steel-blue respectively, with "Same moment, funding both." centered beneath both lanes in warm white, slightly larger than standard body copy to read as the slide's resolving line.
- **Slide 6 pull-quote:** the largest single body-copy treatment in the whole carousel (short enough to support it — two sentences). Warm white with "time-stacked" set in gold as the single emphasized word/phrase.
- **Slide 7 CTA headline:** "AUDIT YOUR NEXT LAUNCH" set large, bold, all-caps, gold or warm white (per brand's standard CTA treatment); the two question lines beneath in warm white, generous spacing, each on its own line.
- No script fonts, no faux "concert poster" novelty font despite the subject matter — stays in JustPreneur's own premium-business-editorial voice throughout.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 for all seven slides.
- Keep all text (including the dual-clock rail, kickers, bulleted body copy, clarifier lines, and wordmark) within a safe margin of at least 80px from every edge; keep the very top 100px and bottom 100px clear of critical text since Instagram's UI (profile chrome, like/comment bar) can crop or obscure those zones on some surfaces.
- Dual-clock rail sits at approximately y:110–190 — comfortably inside the top safe zone.
- JustPreneur wordmark sits centered at approximately y:1250–1300 (bottom safe margin), small and consistent across all seven slides.
- Slides 2 and 4 carry the densest copy in the set — if a five-bullet or four-bullet block risks running past the safe bottom margin at comfortable mobile type size, prioritize full legibility and complete, unshortened bullets over fitting a decorative background flourish; never shrink type below the carousel's established minimum to force a fit.
- No critical word, bullet, or qualifier line may be cropped by Instagram's rounded-corner or UI-overlay zones; verify against a standard IG safe-area template before export.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white body copy on deep-navy background exceeds WCAG AA contrast for large text at the sizes specified (headline, kicker, bulleted copy, pull-quote, CTA all qualify as "large text" per WCAG at the point sizes used in this brand system).
- Gold-on-navy (kickers, Storefront/Now track) and steel-blue-on-navy (Training/2028 track, Slide 5 lane label) must each be checked against the brand's standard gold/navy and steel-blue/navy contrast ratios used in prior carousels; if the signal steel-blue trends too light/desaturated against navy in final render, darken/saturate it slightly rather than lighten the navy field.
- Slide 2's `(self-reported)` qualifier and Slide 4's two clarifier lines are the single highest accessibility priority in this carousel: full body-copy size, steel-gray-on-navy or warm-white-on-navy (not gold-on-navy, to visually distinguish disclosures from headline claims), minimum AA contrast, no reduced opacity, never confined to a corner.
- No text sits directly on a high-detail/high-contrast part of any background image; all type zones fall on the specified low-detail negative-space bands (§3, §10 prompts) with a subtle navy scrim applied if a rendered background runs lighter than expected.
- No flashing, strobing, or rapid-motion-implying visual effects (this is a static carousel); the "two clocks" motif is conveyed through composition and arc stillness/resolution, not literal animation.
