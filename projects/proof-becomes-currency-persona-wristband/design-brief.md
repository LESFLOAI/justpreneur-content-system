## 1. Overall creative concept

This is a correction-and-lesson story for a business-press audience, not a founder-hype reel. The visual job is to (a) visibly correct a viral timeline error, and (b) dramatize the real lesson — that verifiable proof, not speed, is what moves investors — through an editorial "proof desk" aesthetic: think Bloomberg Businessweek or The Information doing a myth-correction explainer, not a motivational-quote template.

No real person is depicted anywhere in the set. Zach Yadegari, Cory Levy, and any other named individual appear only in the approved copy text added during layout — never as a rendered likeness. No company or brand logos (MyFitnessPal, Cal AI, Vine Ventures, Collective Global, WHOOP, Eight Sleep, iMessage, Persona) are rendered in any image; all of that attribution lives in the copy text added afterward.

A single recurring graphic device ties all seven slides together: **the Proof Seal** — a circular stamp/seal motif in warm gold, sitting low-right in the frame near a thin gold baseline rule (echoing JustPreneur's existing bottom-line convention). The seal's state advances with the argument:

- **Slide 1:** the seal appears only as a faint, pale, unfilled outline — an unverified claim still circulating.
- **Slide 2:** the seal motif is replaced by a timeline-track device (see Section 3) — the correction itself.
- **Slide 3:** the seal is half-filled solid gold — the first verifiable asset (a completed deal, real revenue) exists.
- **Slide 4:** the seal is fully outlined and bright, with thin radiating threads connecting outward to a few soft points — the "warm network" that proof opens.
- **Slide 5:** the seal is fully solid, polished gold — proof is complete; this is the thesis slide.
- **Slide 6:** the seal appears mid-impression, pressed into a paper/ledger surface — "build a receipt."
- **Slide 7:** the seal sits steady and fully resolved beside (not touching) the product render — calm, closing state.

Three visual movements:
- **Slides 1–2 (The Correction):** desk-level, editorial, data-forward — fixing the record.
- **Slides 3–5 (The Argument):** desk and boardroom environments building the proof-over-speed case.
- **Slides 6–7 (The Application + CTA):** a tactile "build a receipt" image, then a controlled, pre-order-only product render with the CTA.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

Every slide follows the same top-to-bottom hierarchy:
1. Headline, title, or body-copy block — upper portion of frame.
2. Photographic/graphic focal environment — lower half to two-thirds of frame.
3. Thin gold baseline rule + Proof Seal motif — fixed bottom-edge band, state advancing per slide as described above (Slide 2 swaps the seal for the timeline-track device only).
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, fixed bottom-center position, identical placement across all seven slides, sitting just above the gold rule/seal band.

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline/title typeface (Slide 1 headline; Slides 2–7 titles): a bold, high-contrast editorial serif with a business-press feel (Tiempos Headline / Canela / Freight Display style) — premium financial journalism, not a motivational template.
- Subheadline (Slide 1): a lighter-weight companion sans or a smaller cut of the same serif, clearly secondary to the headline.
- Body copy typeface (Slides 2–7): a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne / Inter / Neue Haas style) for maximum mobile legibility, especially given the longer factual sentences on Slides 3, 4, and 7.
- No kicker labels, no slide numbers, no production marks added as separate small text anywhere — all facts (dates, dollar figures, names) appear only within the approved copy sentences themselves, added in layout.
- Headline sizing: no line should exceed ~36–40 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective headline size on the 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, line-height ~130–140%, left-aligned (never justified). Slides 3, 4, and 7 carry the longest sentences — give these slightly more vertical copy allowance and a heavier contrast overlay rather than shrinking type below the minimum.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A range) — dominant background tone, identical across all seven slides.
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — secondary surfaces (desks, boardroom table, device bodies, stamp tool).
- Warm white (#F5F1E8 / #FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds.
- Energy gold (#C9A24B–#D4AF37) — desk-lamp glow, the Proof Seal motif in all its states, the timeline-track rule and markers on Slide 2, the wax-seal stamp itself, "NOT 4" accent on Slide 1, wordmark.
- No red, green, or additional accent colors anywhere in the set (the correction mark on Slide 1 is drawn in gold, not red, to stay inside the brand system) — keep the palette disciplined so the carousel reads as one continuous brand set.

## 6. Image treatment

- All imagery graded toward navy/steel/gold low-key desk-and-boardroom lighting so every slide feels like one continuous visual world.
- Apply a subtle dark navy gradient overlay (~40–55% opacity) behind every text zone on every slide to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying photo, with a heavier overlay (~55–60%) behind the longer paragraphs on Slides 3, 4, and 7.
- No text, numerals, dollar figures, dates, logos, or real-person likenesses generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy, all names, and all specific figures are added in a design tool afterward per this brief.
- Slide 2's timeline-track device must be built as a plain gold rule with two lit marker-nodes and a clearly long, empty gap between them (left marker near the left edge, right marker near the right edge) — this is the only slide where the generated image itself must encode a factual relationship (a long gap, not a short one); the exact date labels are added afterward in layout, never baked into the image.
- Slide 7's wristband must always be composed as a studio product render on a pedestal — never photographed in a hand, never shown unboxed, never staged to imply current/active shipping. No screen, no brand mark, no logo rendered on the band itself.
- No depiction anywhere in the set of Zach Yadegari, Cory Levy, or any other named person; no depiction of any real company logo (MyFitnessPal, Cal AI, Vine Ventures, Collective Global, WHOOP, Eight Sleep, iMessage, Persona); no rendered sale price, acquisition price, or valuation figure of any kind on any slide.
- Slide 6's hand/stamp interaction is built without any visible hand or arm, specifically to avoid AI-rendered malformed-hand artifacts — show only the stamp tool and the fresh impression.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of at least 96px from every edge. Instagram's feed UI can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px of the preview — keep headline/title/body anchor points starting no higher than ~150–180px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge (the gold rule/seal band itself can sit lower, in the ~1270–1320px band, since it is a thin graphic device rather than text).
- Keep body copy line length under ~880px wide (leaving ≥100px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading, with extra care on Slides 3, 4, and 7 where body copy runs longest.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080 x 1350) on all seven slides.
- Identical thin gold baseline rule position along the bottom edge on every slide; the Proof Seal motif advances in this exact order: faint unfilled outline (1) → replaced by the two-marker timeline-track device, long gap, left-to-right (2) → half-filled solid (3) → fully outlined with radiating threads (4) → fully solid/polished, doubled by the macro wax-seal environment shot (5) → mid-impression on paper (6) → fully resolved and steady, positioned apart from the product render (7).
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center, just above the gold rule/seal band) on every slide, slightly more prominent only on the final Slide 7.
- Identical navy/steel/gold low-key desk-and-boardroom color grade across all seven backgrounds.
- Identical title/body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention on Slides 2–7; Slide 1 carries the unique headline/subheadline treatment.
- No slide may include a rendered likeness of Zach Yadegari, Cory Levy, or any other named person; no slide may include a real company logo, a sale price, an acquisition price, or a valuation figure; no slide may depict the wristband as already shipping or in a customer's hand.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) text over the navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, especially over the newsprint texture on Slide 1 and the boardroom device-screen glow on Slide 4.
- Gold (#C9A24B) is reserved for large display text ("NOT 4" on Slide 1, the Proof Seal motif, the wordmark) on dark backgrounds only — do not set gold body copy at small sizes, where contrast drops.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas.
- Slides 3, 4, and 7 carry the longest paragraphs in the set — prioritize a slightly heavier gradient overlay behind text on these slides.
