## Facts Summary

On October 9–10, 2026, Live Nation announced it has agreed to acquire a majority stake (percentage undisclosed) in Chilean concert promoter Bizarro, a deal that remains subject to regulatory approval and has not yet closed. No financial terms were disclosed. Bizarro founder Alfredo Alonso will continue leading the company's creative and strategic direction. Live Nation CEO Michael Rapino said the agreement "complements" the company's existing partnership with DG Medios, calling Chile "one of the most dynamic music markets in Latin America, and a priority for Live Nation." Live Nation has held a majority stake in Chilean promoter DG Medios since December 2019 and announced a majority-stake partnership in Santiago's Movistar Arena (with BeLive Entertainment Group) on December 11, 2025. Separately, Live Nation closed a 51% acquisition of Peruvian promoter Bizarro Peru on January 22, 2026, for an undisclosed amount, via its local subsidiary Live Nation Perú. Chilean competition regulators (the Fiscalía Nacional Económica) have previously scrutinized market concentration involving Bizarro and Movistar Arena's controlling group (2018–2019 merger review, with behavioral conditions); the current status of any review of Live Nation's 2025–2026 Chilean acquisitions is unconfirmed.
