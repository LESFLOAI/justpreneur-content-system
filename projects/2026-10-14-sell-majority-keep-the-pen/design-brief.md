## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a business-editorial case study — closer to a Bloomberg/Axios "deal autopsy" than a motivational quote card. The entire carousel is built around one recurring visual device: **a fountain pen resting on a contract/term-sheet page**. The pen is the thesis made visual — it opens the carousel as the dominant image (slide 1), reappears as the "kept" side of the reframe (slide 4), anchors the lesson (slide 5), and closes the carousel resolved and quiet (slide 7). Everything else — equity/stake imagery, the concert-industry backdrop, the regulatory note — is treated as secondary texture around that spine.

A secondary, lighter motif — abstracted concert-stage lighting (a single spotlight beam, a lighting-control console, a stage silhouette) — is used only on slides 3 and 6 to ground the story in the live-music industry without depicting any specific person, venue, or company mark. No Live Nation or Bizarro logos, wordmarks, or brand colors are depicted anywhere. Alfredo Alonso is a real named individual — do not generate a fabricated likeness of him; where a human presence is implied (slide 3), use an anonymous silhouette shown from behind or in deep shadow, never a face, and never labeled as him.

Color-coding reinforces the "gave up / kept" thesis across the whole set: muted steel-gray tones signal what was given up (equity, majority control), while energy gold signals what was kept (the pen, creative/strategic control). This single consistent code is what makes the carousel feel designed rather than decorative.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: one dominant graphic/photographic device placed to leave a large, clean negative-space zone for headline + body copy — copy is never baked into the AI-generated image itself. Hierarchy, top to bottom, on every slide:
1. Headline or body-copy block — upper portion of the frame (or centered for the cover and slide 4).
2. Graphic/photographic focal device — lower half, or a restrained background field behind the copy block.
3. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, identical position on every slide (bottom-center).

No kickers, corner labels, slide numbers, dates, or citation marks are added anywhere in the carousel — only the approved headline/body copy plus the wordmark appear as text.

- Subhead "Live Nation's new Chile deal has a plot twist worth studying." — directly beneath, y≈560–660px, smaller weight, centered.

**Slide 2 — The Confirmed Facts**

**Slide 3 — The Detail Everyone's Skipping**
- Body copy block: y≈220–560px, left-aligned, max width ~840px: "Founder Alfredo Alonso isn't exiting." / "He's staying on to lead Bizarro's creative and strategic direction."

Lower half of frame, warm gold spotlight beam as the single accent of light in an otherwise navy/steel scene.
- Wordmark bottom-center, consistent position.

**Slide 4 — Gave Up / Kept (keep this slide simple — two short labels only, no extra copy)**
- Vertical layout, split by a thin gold hairline down the center or a clean horizontal split (top/bottom) — left/top half and right/bottom half are visually distinct zones.
- Left/top zone: label "GAVE UP" in steel-gray/muted type, y≈420–520px (if vertical split) or upper half (if horizontal split), with a small muted graphic element behind it (a fading stack of equity/coin shapes, desaturated, partially dissolving into the navy background).
- Right/bottom zone: label "KEPT" in energy-gold type, with the same fountain-pen motif from slide 1 rendered small and sharp, fully lit in gold, resting on a dark surface beneath the label.
- No other copy on this slide — this is the visual "reframe" beat and must stay uncluttered.
- Wordmark bottom-center, consistent position, sized small so it doesn't compete with the two-word reframe.

**Slide 5 — The Lesson (Final Takeaway)**
- Body copy block: y≈180–760px, left-aligned, max width ~860px, full approved paragraph set in a slightly smaller body size than slides 2–3 to fit the longer copy at a legible line length (break into natural paragraph groups matching the source: the capital/control sentence, the "ownership and control" sentence, the Alonso-leverage sentence).
- Background/graphic device: a simple balanced-scale motif — one side holding the muted stacked-equity shapes from slide 4, the other side holding the gold pen — rendered small, low in the frame, so it reads as a quiet balance rather than a bold illustration. This visually reinforces "control vs. ownership are separable, not automatic."
- Wordmark bottom-center, consistent position.

**Slide 6 — Texture Note (Regulatory Context)**
- Body copy block: y≈220–620px, left-aligned, max width ~840px: "Context: Chilean regulators have scrutinized market concentration involving Bizarro and Movistar Arena's controlling group before (2018–2019 merger review, with behavioral conditions)." / "Whether that scrutiny applies to this new deal is unconfirmed."
- Background/graphic device: a generic institutional building silhouette (a neutral courthouse/government-building facade, unbranded, no flag, no seal, no agency name) at dusk, rendered in cooler, more desaturated navy/steel tones than any other slide in the set — signaling "institutional, unresolved" without citing or implying a specific real agency's identity or logo.
- Wordmark bottom-center, consistent position.

**Slide 7 — Closing CTA**
- Body copy block: y≈260–620px, centered or left-aligned, max width ~860px: "Save this before your next term sheet." / "Then ask yourself: if you sold tomorrow, what would you negotiate to keep?"
- Background/graphic device: the slide 1 contract-and-pen still life, now shown resolved and calm — pen resting directly on the signature line rather than beside it, warm gold light steady rather than dramatic — the visual bookend to the opening slide. A single small, simple save/bookmark mark may sit near the bottom of the copy block only because the approved copy itself instructs "Save this" — kept small and functional, not decorative.
- JustPreneur wordmark, bottom-center, may be slightly more prominent as the sign-off slide.

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface (slide 1 only): a bold, high-contrast editorial serif with a "financial press" feel (e.g., a Tiempos Headline / Publico-style display serif) — signals premium business media, not a generic template.
- Body copy typeface (slides 2, 3, 5, 6, 7): a clean grotesk/sans (e.g., a Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style sans) for maximum mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Slide 4 labels ("GAVE UP" / "KEPT"): same editorial serif family as the slide-1 headline, set in bold caps, letter-spaced — large enough to read instantly at thumbnail size since they carry the entire slide.
- Headline sizing (slide 1): no line should exceed ~30 characters at the chosen weight; target minimum ~64px effective size on a 1080-wide canvas.
- Body copy sizing: minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (not justified/centered body paragraphs, except slide 1's subhead and slide 7's CTA which may be centered to match their cover/closer role).
- Slide 5's longer paragraph: slightly reduced size vs. slides 2/3/6 (still above the ~40px floor) with tighter paragraph spacing so it fits without crowding the balanced-scale graphic.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A) — dominant background across all seven slides for cohesion.
- Steel gray (#4B5563) — "gave up" side of the thesis: the equity/stake imagery, the pending-stamp motif on slide 2, the regulatory building on slide 6.
- Warm white (#F5F1E8–#FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds.
- Energy gold (#C9A24B–#D4AF37) — "kept" side of the thesis: the pen on slides 1, 4, 5, 7; the spotlight beam on slide 3.
- Slide 6 shifts cooler and more desaturated than the rest of the set (less gold, more steel-gray) to visually mark "unresolved/unconfirmed" — the only intentional tonal deviation in the carousel, used the same way slide 1 and slide 7 intentionally echo each other as bookends.

## 6. Image treatment

- All background graphics/photos: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm gold highlight pops, consistent color grade across all seven slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying graphic — this is a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, or real identifiable people/faces should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated backgrounds supply mood, motif, and negative space only.
- No depiction of any Live Nation or Bizarro logo, wordmark, trademark, or brand color system, and no fabricated likeness of Alfredo Alonso or any other named real person — where a human presence is implied (slide 3), use an anonymous silhouette only, shown from behind or in deep shadow.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge at minimum; Instagram's feed UI can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px — keep headline/body anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~860px wide (leaving ≥110px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.
- Slide 4's two labels must each sit fully within the safe zone of their respective half of the frame — do not let either label crowd the center divider or the top/bottom safe margins.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080x1350) on all seven slides.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center) on every slide for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/steel/gold) except slide 6's intentionally cooler, more desaturated cast to signal "unconfirmed," and slide 4's deliberate muted-gray vs. gold split to carry the reframe.
- The fountain-pen-on-contract motif must look like the same object across slides 1, 4, 5, and 7 (same pen style, same paper tone) so the visual thread reads as one continuous idea, not four unrelated images.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across slides 2, 3, 5, 6; slide 1's headline/subhead and slide 7's CTA may be centered as the cover/closer.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text, not just the general background.
- Gold (#C9A24B) is reserved for the slide-4 "KEPT" label and small accent elements on dark navy/steel backgrounds only, where contrast is strong — never place gold text on a light/warm-white area.
- The steel-gray "GAVE UP" label on slide 4 must sit on a dark enough navy background (or have its own gradient patch) to clear 4.5:1 contrast — gray-on-navy is the lowest-contrast pairing in this palette and needs explicit checking at layout time.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas, so it remains legible at feed-thumbnail scale.
