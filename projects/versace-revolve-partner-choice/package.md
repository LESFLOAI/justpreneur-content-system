## Facts Summary (Approved Facts Block, cleared with qualifications by Fact Verifier)

On September 21, 2026, Donatella Versace and Revolve Group, Inc. (NYSE: RVLV) jointly announced a new joint venture to create an independent, as-yet-unnamed fashion and beauty brand, with Donatella Versace serving as Chief Creative Officer. The companies stated the venture is expected to launch its first products in 2027, beginning with beauty, initially across the United States, Latin America, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East; the brand's name has not yet been disclosed. This follows Donatella Versace's March 2025 departure as Chief Creative Officer of the Versace fashion house (a role she held for nearly 30 years), after which she moved into a chief brand ambassador role at Versace and was succeeded as Versace's Chief Creative Officer by Dario Vitale, effective April 1, 2025. Versace, the fashion house, is now owned by Prada Group following a 2025 acquisition; the new Versace–Revolve venture is described as structurally independent of that fashion house. Whether Donatella Versace holds an equity or ownership stake in the new venture, or is engaged solely in a paid creative-director/CCO capacity, has not been disclosed by either company as of this writing and must not be asserted as fact.
