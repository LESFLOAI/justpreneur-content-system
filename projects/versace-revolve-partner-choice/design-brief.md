# Visual Design Brief — "Donatella Versace Didn't Rebuild With Luxury. She Rebuilt With Reach." (7-slide carousel)

Story: Donatella Versace and Revolve Group, Inc. (NYSE: RVLV) announced a joint venture on September 21, 2026 — a new, independent fashion and beauty brand with Donatella as Chief Creative Officer, launching beauty first in 2027 across the U.S., Latin America, Asia-Pacific, and the Middle East. Strategic angle from Strategist: "The Partner Choice Is the Real Headline" — the story is not that Donatella has a new job, it's *who* she chose to build with (a digital-native, data/DTC-driven retailer) instead of another legacy luxury house, after nearly 30 years at Versace (now Prada Group-owned, with Dario Vitale as CCO since April 2025) and her move into a chief brand ambassador role in March 2025.

Tone requirement: neutral, strategic, business-analysis register throughout. No disparagement of Prada, Versace, Dario Vitale, or "legacy luxury" as a category — the split-screen motif contrasts two *types of infrastructure* (heritage atelier vs. digital-native retail), not a "winner vs. loser" framing. This is an observation about partner selection, not gossip or a takedown.

## 1. Overall creative concept

Treat this as a business-strategy editorial in the register of Business of Fashion or The Business of Fashion's analysis desk — polished, restrained, boardroom-premium, never a fan-account or tabloid-fashion post. The visual spine is a recurring **split-screen motif**: one half evokes the legacy fashion-house world (marble, gold hardware, ornate ateliers, spotlighted mannequin/dress form, runway staging), the other half evokes the digital-native retail world (server racks, glowing data grids, fulfillment/logistics infrastructure, e-commerce interface light). The split runs through the carousel like a visual argument: cover (both worlds meeting), Slide 3 (legacy world, dimming but dignified), Slide 4 (digital-native world, cool and expansive), Slide 5 (a new, independent third space forming between them), Slide 6 (the infrastructure/audience network made visible as the actual "asset" worth evaluating), and Slide 7 (a resolved, unified close).

No real logos, wordmarks, or trademarked signage for Versace, Prada, or Revolve appear in any generated image — the Medusa head, any brand crest, or any retailer wordmark is off-limits. No photorealistic depiction of Donatella Versace, Dario Vitale, or any other named real individual — all human presence in generated images is anonymized, silhouetted, or represented symbolically (an empty spotlight, a dress form, a chair) rather than as a specific likeness, since we have no verified likeness rights and do not want to fabricate faces attributed to real people.

## 2. Composition and focal hierarchy

All slides: one dominant graphic/photographic device placed to leave a large clean negative-space zone for headline + body copy — text is added in layout, never baked into the AI-generated image. Hierarchy per slide, top to bottom:
1. Small kicker/section label (e.g., "THE MOVE," "OLD WORLD") — top margin, gold, all-caps, letter-spaced (Slide 1 cover and Slide 7 CTA use no kicker, matching the cover/sign-off bookend convention)
2. Headline/body copy block — upper-to-mid frame
3. Graphic/photographic focal device — lower half, or as a full-bleed background field behind the copy block
4. JustPreneur wordmark — small, bottom-safe-margin, identical position across all 7 slides (bottom-center)

## 4. Typography direction

- Headline typeface: a bold, high-contrast editorial serif (Tiempos Headline / Publico-style) for Slide 1's headline — signals "premium business media," not a fashion-influencer template.
- Body copy typeface: a clean grotesk/sans (Söhne/Inter/Neue Haas-style) for all body paragraphs on Slides 2–7 — maximizes mobile legibility at small sizes.
- Kickers ("THE MOVE," "OLD WORLD," "NEW PARTNER," "WHY IT MATTERS," "THE LESSON"): sans, all-caps, letter-spaced (~150–200 tracking), small size (~28–32px equivalent), gold or warm-white. Slides 1 and 7 use no kicker (cover/sign-off bookends).
- Headline sizing on Slide 1: large enough to be legible at thumbnail size on a phone — target minimum ~60px effective headline size on a 1080-wide canvas, two-line stack breaking after "Luxury."
- Body copy sizing (Slides 2–7): minimum ~40–44px effective size at 1080 width, generous line-height (~130–140%), left-aligned (Slides 2–6) or centered (Slides 1, 7) for readability.
- Slide 3's three facts (tenure, ambassador/Vitale/Prada, parallel-roles) and Slide 2's two facts (JV announcement, CCO/launch/regions) should each start on their own paragraph break so the timeline reads instantly on mobile — do not run separate facts together in one dense block.
- Slide 4 and Slide 5's two-line structures each get their own paragraph break for the same reason.
- Slide 6's two-line lesson should be set with clear visual separation (line break or extra leading) between "don't evaluate them on the name in the room" and "evaluate them on the infrastructure and audience they actually control" so the contrast reads as the punchline.

## 5. Color palette

- Deep navy (#0B1220–#10172A) — dominant background/shadow tone across all seven slides for consistency
- Steel gray (#4B5563 range) — server-rack surfaces, structural/architectural elements on both sides of the split
- Warm white (#F5F1E8/#FAF7F0) — primary text color on dark backgrounds, marble highlight tone, data-grid light highlights
- Energy gold (#C9A24B/#D4AF37) — kickers, accents, the atelier's spotlight and brass hardware, the network nodes on Slide 6, the wordmark
- A single cool accent — data-grid blue (#4A6FA5 range, kept desaturated/muted, not saturated tech-blue) — reserved for the digital-native/Revolve-side visuals on Slides 1, 4, 5, and 7 only, balanced against the gold so neither "side" reads as visually dominant or favored
- No red, no bright saturated color anywhere — this is a calm, strategic-observation story, not a rivalry or scandal story, so the palette stays even-handed and premium rather than dramatic

## 6. Image treatment

- All background graphics/photos: desaturated toward navy/gray with warm gold and muted cool-blue highlight pops, consistent color grade across all seven slides so the carousel reads as one cohesive set.
- Apply a subtle dark gradient overlay (navy, ~40–60% opacity) across whichever zone holds text, on every slide, to guarantee contrast regardless of the underlying graphic — a layout-stage step, not part of the AI image prompt.
- No text, logos, or real identifiable faces should be generated inside the AI images themselves — all copy is added in a design tool afterward per this brief. AI-generated backgrounds supply mood, motif, and negative space only.
- No depiction of the Versace Medusa-head logo, any Versace/Prada/Revolve wordmark, crest, or trademarked signage in any image — use only the generic/abstract devices described above (dress form, spotlight, server racks, light-form convergence, network graphic).
- No photorealistic rendering of Donatella Versace, Dario Vitale, or any other named real individual — any human presence is anonymized (silhouette, empty dress form, unoccupied spotlight) rather than a specific likeness.
- Keep the split-screen "sides" visually balanced in size, lighting quality, and dignity throughout — neither the legacy-atelier side nor the digital-native side should be rendered as more attractive, higher-quality, or more "correct" than the other; the visual argument is about infrastructure type, not which side is better.

## 7. Instagram safe-margin instructions

- Canvas: 1080 x 1350 (portrait) for all seven slides, consistent throughout the carousel.
- Keep all text and the wordmark within a safe zone of 96px from every edge (left/right/top/bottom) at minimum; Instagram's UI (profile row, caption, like/comment icons) can crop into the very top ~90px and very bottom ~250px on the feed preview — keep headline/kicker anchor points starting no higher than ~150px from the top, and keep the wordmark comfortably above ~120px from the absolute bottom edge.
- Keep body copy line length under ~860px wide (leaving ≥110px margins each side) for comfortable mobile reading.
- On Slide 1 and Slide 7, keep the split-screen seam itself away from the extreme left/right thirds of the frame so it doesn't read as an accidental crop line at thumbnail size — center it, softened, behind the text zone.

## 8. Slide-to-slide consistency rules

- Identical canvas size (1080x1350) across all seven slides.
- Identical kicker style/position (top-left, gold, small-caps, ~150–200 tracking) on Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 use the centered cover/sign-off treatment (no kicker) as bookends.
- Identical JustPreneur wordmark size/position/color (bottom-center) on every slide for carousel cohesion and brand recognition mid-swipe; Slide 7's wordmark may step up slightly in size as the sign-off.
- Identical color grade/mood across backgrounds (navy/steel/gold/warm-white/muted-blue consistent) — no slide should read as favoring one "side" of the split over the other in warmth or clarity.
- Identical body-copy typeface, size range, and left-alignment convention across Slides 2–6; Slides 1 and 7 use centered alignment as cover/sign-off bookends.
- The split-screen motif recurs and evolves across Slides 1, 3, 4, 5, and 7 (both worlds meeting → legacy world alone, dignified → digital-native world alone, expansive → a new third space forming between them → both worlds resolved into one unified close) so the carousel visually argues its own thesis even at a glance-through.
- Same gradient-overlay treatment behind text on every slide for contrast consistency.

## 9. Accessibility and contrast check

- Warm white (#F5F1E8) body/headline text over deep-navy-gradient-overlaid backgrounds targets a contrast ratio well above WCAG AA (4.5:1) for normal text — confirm during layout that the gradient overlay is opaque enough behind every line of text on every slide, especially Slide 6 where the network-graphic has more visual texture.
- Avoid placing gold (#C9A24B) text directly on light/warm-white areas (e.g., the marble motif on Slide 3, the data-grid highlight areas on Slide 4) — gold is reserved for kickers/accents/network-nodes on dark navy/steel backgrounds only, where contrast is strong.
- Minimum text size on mobile: no body text should render below ~40px effective size on a 1080px-wide canvas (roughly 14–16pt equivalent at full-size phone viewing) to remain legible when scaled down to feed thumbnail size.
- Slide 3's three-part timeline and Slide 2's two-part fact block must remain the same size and contrast as the rest of the body paragraph — do not shrink any fact to the point of illegibility on mobile.
