- Venus Williams and other headliners also featured on opening day — Mann was not the only major name on Oct 5, which is useful context but does not undermine the core claims.

> Advertising Week New York named content creator Dhar Mann as the event's first "Chief Creator Officer" for its 2026 edition, which ran October 5–8, 2026, at The Penn District in Manhattan.
