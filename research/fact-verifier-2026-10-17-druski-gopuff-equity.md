## Single-Source Recirculation Finding

Story Scout's flag is **confirmed and strengthened** by this check. The near-identical quote from Yakir Gola ("a true creative and commercial partnership") and the identical "undisclosed equity stake" framing appear verbatim or near-verbatim across TheGrio, AfroTech, Black Enterprise, Complex, Chain Store Age, Retail Tech Innovation Hub, NetInfluencer, and DesignRush. This is the signature of a single Gopuff press release/announcement being recirculated by multiple outlets rather than independently reported or verified. No outlet presents an original interview, a leaked term sheet, a regulatory filing, or any fact not traceable to that one announcement. Gopuff is privately held, so no SEC disclosure exists to cross-check equity terms.

## Required Attribution Language

- Attribute the deal and its terms to "Gopuff's announcement" / "according to Gopuff" / "the company said" — not to independent reporting.
- Never state or imply a percentage, dollar value, or valuation for the equity stake. State plainly: "financial terms, including the size of the stake, were not disclosed."
- Frame quotes as coming from "a statement" / "the announcement," not from an interview conducted by the outlet or by JustPreneur.
- Use "reportedly" or "according to the company" when restating the HARD PASS/Tom Brady campaign connection and the Coulda Been Records/4Lifers integration details.

## Claims That Must Be Removed or Softened

- Any phrasing suggesting the equity stake size, valuation, or Druski's resulting net worth impact is known — must be removed. It is undisclosed everywhere.
- Any phrasing implying multiple independent outlets "confirmed" or "verified" the deal terms — must be softened to reflect that all reporting traces to one company announcement.
- Avoid stating Druski's birth year/place if not needed for the story (sourcing is inconsistent and not material to this piece).

## Missing Context

- No independent, non-Gopuff-sourced confirmation of the deal's existence or terms was found (expected, since Gopuff is private and this is a marketing-style announcement).
- Readers should understand this is a company-driven announcement/press release story, consistent with typical brand-partnership news, not an investigative or filed-disclosure story.

## Approved Facts Block

> Gopuff announced on October 9, 2026 that comedian and entrepreneur Druski (Drew Desbordes) has entered a multi-year partnership with the company that, according to Gopuff, includes an equity stake for Druski. The size of the equity stake and other financial terms were not disclosed and are not publicly known. Under the announced partnership, Druski's entertainment ventures — Coulda Been Records and 4Lifers Entertainment — will be folded into the collaboration to co-create products and consumer experiences with Gopuff. Gopuff co-founder and co-CEO Yakir Gola is quoted in the announcement calling it "a true creative and commercial partnership." The deal reportedly builds on a prior relationship, as Druski previously appeared alongside Tom Brady in Gopuff's "HARD PASS" ad campaign. All available reporting (TheGrio, AfroTech, Black Enterprise, Complex, Chain Store Age, Retail Tech Innovation Hub, and others, Oct 9-12, 2026) traces back to Gopuff's own announcement; no independent confirmation of deal terms exists, and none should be implied.

## Final Status

**Cleared with qualifications** — the existence and general nature of the announced partnership (parties, date, brands involved, undisclosed-equity framing) are adequately corroborated across multiple reputable outlets and may be reported. However, all deal-specific details must be attributed to Gopuff's announcement, equity size/value must never be stated or estimated, and copy must not imply independent verification beyond the single underlying company statement.
