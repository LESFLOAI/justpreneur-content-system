# Approved Facts Block — "The Wolseley Goes Global" (Minor Hotels / Wolseley Hotels NYC)
Verified by: Fact Verifier | Date checked: 2026-10-18
Status: Cleared with qualifications

## Headline facts (verified)
- Minor Hotels (Thai hospitality group Minor International, Group CEO **Dillip Rajakarier** — note the double "l") announced the global launch of a new luxury hotel brand, **The Wolseley Hotels**, on **17 March 2026** via an official press release (media.minorhotels.com), with its first confirmed property to be **The Wolseley Hotel New York**.
- The brand launch itself was first previewed in a **11 July 2025** Minor Hotels announcement of four new brands (The Wolseley Hotels, Colbert Collection, Minor Reserve Collection, iStay Hotels); the March 2026 release was the first time a specific property/site was confirmed.
- On **7 October 2026**, Minor/Wolseley publicized further detail: **The Wolseley Members Club**, a private members' club component of the same New York hotel, with a sequence of lounges, dining rooms, private salons and terraces, centered on a restored "Stanford White Room." This is a follow-on amenity reveal for the already-announced hotel — NOT the original brand-launch announcement. Any copy should frame 10/7/2026 as "new details unveiled" or "the members' club reveal," not as "when Minor launched Wolseley Hotels."
- Location: **130 West 44th Street, Manhattan (Midtown/Theatre District, near Bryant Park/Times Square)** — the existing Chatwal Hotel, which exits the Hyatt Unbound Collection portfolio as of **29 September 2026** ahead of conversion.
- Building history: originally built in **1905** as the clubhouse for **The Lambs** (America's first professional theatrical club), designed by **Stanford White** of the firm **McKim, Mead & White**. The building is on the National Register of Historic Places.
- Room count: **76 guestrooms and suites** (the dominant, repeated figure across the official release and nearly all trade coverage). One blog-level source said "77-key"; treat 76 as correct and the outlier as an error.
- Amenities confirmed: The Wolseley New York restaurant (the restaurant brand's first U.S. location, described as Minor's "culinary flagship for North America"), a cellar-level speakeasy bar, a wellness/wellbeing centre, and the separately-unveiled Wolseley Members Club (lounges, salons, terraces, Stanford White Room).
- Ownership/operation: **Ben-Josef Group Holdings** (New York-based developer) acquired the ground lease for the property for **$53.2 million in October 2025** and is the owner/developer. **Minor operates the hotel under a management agreement with no equity stake** — there is no separate "local NYC operating partner" distinct from Minor; Ben-Josef is the landlord/owner, not an operating partner.
- Targeted opening: hotel overall — **"early 2027"** (per official release and most trade press). The Wolseley Members Club specifically — **"Q2 2027"** (per the 7 October 2026 announcement). Treat these as two slightly different, both-hedged target windows for two different components (hotel vs. club), not a contradiction to resolve into one date.
- Quote: Rajakarier has been quoted (in coverage of the 7 October 2026 Members Club reveal) describing the goal as creating "an all-day social living room for people whose lives move effortlessly between Uptown and Downtown, New York and London," and wanting to bring together "founders and artists, producers and investors, hoteliers and philanthropists, established voices and emerging talent." Attribute this quote specifically to the October 2026 members'-club coverage, not generically to "the brand launch."
- Background/origin: Minor International became the controlling/majority shareholder of **Corbin & King** (owner of The Wolseley, Brasserie Zédel, and other London restaurants) after placing the company into **administration in January 2022** (Minor was the lead secured creditor, citing defaulted loans since May 2020) and then winning an **auction on 1 April 2022** (reported price above £60 million), which ended co-founder **Jeremy King's** equity stake and control. Corbin & King was subsequently renamed **The Wolseley Hospitality Group**. Minor and King dispute the characterization of this episode: Minor maintains the business was insolvent and needed support; King called it a "power play"/hostile takeover and later criticized Minor's "scattergun" international-expansion approach as "completely inappropriate" (that criticism dates to 2021–2022, around the takeover dispute — it is NOT a comment on the 2026 Wolseley Hotels announcement).

## Attributed / weaker claims — use with hedging and sourcing
- **CEO vacancy / stalled expansion narrative:** Wolseley Hospitality Group CEO Baton Berisha stepped down in September 2024 (after Minor brought in AlixPartners restructuring advisers in June 2024 to address cashflow issues). Trade analysis (MCA Insight, "Minor International 'don't get The Wolseley and Jeremy King's vision'") asserts Minor "failed to deliver" on international rollout plans and had gone "more than 18 months" without appointing a permanent CEO. This is directionally consistent with known facts and timing (Berisha's Sept 2024 exit to the March 2026 brand announcement is about 18 months) but the exact figure and its publication date could not be independently corroborated beyond this one trade outlet. **Treat as an attributed claim** ("according to UK trade press...") — do not state as flat fact that there was "no CEO" or that the October reveal is "the first concrete proof" the expansion is real; that framing is reasonable-inference-level, not verified fact.
- **Membership fees/structure for The Wolseley Members Club:** Not published anywhere found, including a Minor Hotels recruitment listing for a "Director of Membership and Culture" role. State plainly that pricing/structure has not been disclosed; do not estimate or imply a figure.
- **Jeremy King's reaction to this 2026 announcement:** None found. King has given unrelated 2026 interviews (e.g., a 2 March 2026 Gentleman's Journal podcast) reflecting generally on the 2022 split, and he separately runs Jeremy King Restaurants' portfolio (The Park, Arlington, and the reopened Simpson's-in-the-Strand) — a distinct, unrelated 2026 storyline. **Do not attribute any quote or reaction about Wolseley Hotels/NYC/the Members Club to Jeremy King.** Do not conflate his Simpson's-in-the-Strand news with this story.
