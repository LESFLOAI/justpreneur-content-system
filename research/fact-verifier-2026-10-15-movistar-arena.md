## Approved Facts Block (verbatim)

Telefónica completed its exit from the Colombian telecommunications market in two stages: it sold its 67.5% stake in Colombia Telecomunicaciones (Coltel, Movistar's Colombian operator) to Millicom — Tigo's parent company — in a deal that closed February 6, 2026 for approximately US$214 million cash; separately, the Colombian government completed the sale of its remaining 32.5% stake in Coltel to Millicom on April 24, 2026, for approximately COP $856,002 million. Telefónica had operated in Colombia since 2005 (roughly two decades); Millicom has stated it is integrating the Movistar operation into Tigo, with the Movistar brand expected to be phased out of the Colombian market by the end of 2026, though this phase-out is not yet reported as complete.

Separately, Bogotá's Movistar Arena is ending its eight-year naming-rights sponsorship with Movistar Colombia, which expires October 31, 2026. This naming-rights contract is independent of — though its non-renewal is made more certain by — Telefónica/Movistar's corporate exit from Colombia; BeatHub Entertainment (the venue's operator) had stated as recently as July 2026 that the venue would keep the Movistar name for as long as the sponsorship remained active.

The arena is operated by Colombiana de Escenarios (CDE) under a concession/public-private partnership with Bogotá's IDRD (Instituto Distrital de Recreación y Deporte), originally structured in 2016. Telefónica/Movistar was never an owner or operator of the venue — only its naming sponsor. On September 17, 2026, BeatHub Entertainment (founded by the team behind ticketing platform Tuboleta) became the sole shareholder of Colombiana de Escenarios, after buying out the 50% stake previously held by Chile's BeLive Entertainment Group (via Venues Colombia/Fidelitas Entertainment), following a pre-clearance review by Colombia's competition regulator, the SIC.

On October 5, 2026, Gabriel Sánchez Olea became General Manager of Colombiana de Escenarios, replacing Luis Guillermo "Luigi" Quintero, who had led the venue for eight years and departed to head Procafecol (Juan Valdez). Sánchez Olea is an economist (Universidad de los Andes) with an MBA from Georgetown University; he joined the arena's project team in 2018 and most recently led BeatHub's corporate strategy.

As of the venue's October 6, 2026 announcement, BeatHub Entertainment has not assigned a new naming-rights sponsor. Instead, it opened a call for proposals ("convocatoria") from interested companies, and expects to name a new naming partner in early 2027. More than 50 shows remain scheduled at the venue for the rest of 2026, and more than 80 artists are already booked for 2027; BeatHub says venue operations and programming are unaffected by the ownership and sponsorship changes.
