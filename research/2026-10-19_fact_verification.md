Billion Dollar Boy (BDB) Group, a social and influencer marketing agency, announced
on October 14, 2026 that it has acquired Creator Authority, a B2B creator-marketing
agency and LinkedIn Marketing Partner founded in 2023 by Brendan Gahan (previously
founder of Epic Signal, which he sold to Mekanism). Creator Authority's reported
clients include Amazon, Adobe, IBM, and HubSpot (a longer client list circulating in
coverage, including AT&T, Dropbox, Mastercard, and Notion, is unverified beyond the
original reporting chain and should not be presented as independently confirmed).

The deal is reported as BDB's first-ever acquisition: an all-cash transaction,
described only as "multimillion-dollar," with an earnout; the exact price has not
been disclosed. Per Business Insider's reporting (relayed via multiple trade
outlets), the deal grew out of a 2023 LinkedIn outreach from BDB co-founder Ed East
to Gahan, which evolved into acquisition talks. Gahan becomes SVP of B2B at the
combined BDB Group. Creator Authority's team (about 15 people) is expected to grow
to 30–40 under BDB.

BDB Group says — as its own, company-reported and unaudited figures, not
independently audited — that its global revenue grew 24% over the past 12 months,
and it projects 2027 revenue will exceed $100 million.

Do not state a specific acquisition price. Do not name a co-founder alongside
