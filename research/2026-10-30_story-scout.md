# Story Scout Research — 2026-10-30 10:00 AM slot

## Top Candidate: DoorDash to Acquire Instacart for ~$17B

### 1. Working headline
"DoorDash Just Bought Its Biggest Rival — Here's the $17B Lesson in Owning the Category"

### 2. What happened (two sentences)
On October 28, 2026, DoorDash (Nasdaq: DASH) signed a definitive merger agreement to acquire Instacart (Nasdaq: CART, corporate name Maplebear Inc.) for approximately $17 billion in a cash-and-stock deal, at $65.00 per share — a 26% premium to Instacart's October 22 closing price. DoorDash disclosed the deal via an 8-K filed October 29, 2026, framing the combination as uniting DoorDash's delivery network/logistics with Instacart's grocery/fulfillment technology to better compete against Walmart and Amazon.

### 3. Date of development
Merger agreement dated October 28, 2026; publicly disclosed/filed October 29, 2026. Deal talks had been reported by WSJ starting ~October 26, 2026 (stock moved on the rumor: Instacart +7.9%, DoorDash -6.1% per Wedbush commentary cited in Taiwanese coverage).

### 4. Why it's gaining attention now
- It is a same-week, still-breaking story (filed yesterday relative to posting date).
- It's one of the largest consolidation deals in the on-demand delivery/gig economy space since DoorDash's own IPO era, reuniting two companies that reportedly explored a deal back in 2021-2022 and didn't close it then.
- Removes Uber as a rumored suitor — a competitive subplot.
- Clear "two memberships become one" consumer story (DashPass + Instacart+ combination, claimed ~$100M/year savings for members) — a tangible, relatable hook.
- Market reaction (stock moves) gives it real-time financial-news momentum.

### 5. Potential JustPreneur lesson
Several usable angles:
- **"Buy the competitor, not just compete with them"** — when two platforms are stuck fighting over the same delivery fee and membership dollar, sometimes the smartest strategic move isn't out-innovating the rival, it's acquiring them and consolidating the customer base/membership economics.
- **Ownership/revenue angle**: combining DashPass + Instacart+ into one membership tier is a direct lesson on subscription-stacking vs. subscription consolidation — relevant to any creator/business running multiple paid tiers.
- **"The deal they turned down years ago came back bigger"** — DoorDash reportedly explored buying Instacart years earlier (~2021) at a lower valuation and didn't. Now it's paying more. Lesson: walking away from a deal doesn't mean the opportunity disappears — it can resurface at a different price and different leverage.

### 6. Best format
Carousel (5-7 slides): slide 1 hook ("DoorDash just bought Instacart for $17B"), slide 2 deal terms, slide 3 the membership-stacking lesson, slide 4 the "they almost did this years ago" angle, slide 5 the takeaway for founders/operators. Reel is a secondary option with voiceover on the "two memberships become one" hook.

### 7. Expected audience response
High engagement expected — recognizable consumer brands (everyone has used DoorDash or Instacart), direct relevance to anyone paying for multiple subscriptions, and timely financial-news-adjacent content tends to get shares/saves from a business-minded audience. Some comment risk around "prices will go up now" consumer skepticism — manageable, even useful for engagement.
