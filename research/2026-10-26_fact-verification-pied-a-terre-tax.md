# Fact Verification — NYC Pied-à-Terre Tax (2026-10-26 cycle)

**Overall status:** Cleared with qualifications (no claim is fundamentally false in a way that breaks the story's premise; several numeric/attribution errors from the Story Scout's draft claims were corrected below before being passed to the Strategist).

## Claim-by-claim

1. **Signed by Hochul, thresholds $5M/$1M, effective 7/1/26–6/30/31** — VERIFIED. Signing date May 28, 2026. Phase One (7/1/26–6/30/28) thresholds: Class One (1-3 family) ≥$5M assessed value; Class Two (condo/co-op) ≥$1M assessed value. Phase Two (from 7/1/28) raises the Class Two threshold to $5M as well. Bill number "S.9009C" could not be independently confirmed against a primary legislative source — dropped from downstream copy.

2. **Rates** — CORRECTED. Houses (Class One): 0.8% / 1.05% / 1.3% tiered, capping at 1.3% — NOT 4% as originally drafted by Story Scout. Condo/co-op (Class Two): 4%–6.5%, confirmed as drafted.

3. **Revenue/property-count estimates** — must be attributed per source, not merged: Gov. Hochul's office originally estimated ~13,000 properties / ~$500M per year, later revised down to ~10,000 properties. NYC Comptroller's independent estimate: ~11,200 properties, ~$500M gross, net likely $340M–$380M after adjustments.

4. **Exemption filing deadline** — VERIFIED. Extended three times: Aug 21 → Sept 18 → Oct 13, 2026 (CityRealty's own Oct 6–12 report calls Oct 13 the "third and final" extension; corroborated by Inman, Brownstoner, Bloomberg Law, CRE Daily). ~9,300 exemption applications filed by mid-October; ~5,200 (~55%) approved as primary-residence exemptions (per NYC DOF data cited by Planetizen).

5. **Sept 29, 2026 ruling** — CLEARED WITH NUANCE. Justice Wayne M. Ozzi, NY Supreme Court, Richmond County, ruled that DOF notices to ~17,000 owners were "arbitrary and capricious"/unlawful (burden-shifting); ordered notices redone and the ~900,000-property online roll taken down. He explicitly left the underlying STATE tax intact — only the city's implementation was struck down. City appealed. Sources differ on whether the stay that night was purely automatic vs. an affirmative Second Department order around Oct 6, 2026 — safer language: "the city appealed and the rollout continued under a stay," not "an automatic stay kicked in immediately."

6. **Earlier procedural sequence** — VERIFIED. Ozzi TRO issued Aug 10, 2026 (hearing set Aug 31); Second Department Associate Justice Phillip Hom stayed the TRO Aug 13, 2026, letting rollout resume; Ozzi's merits ruling Sept 29; Second Department (Brooklyn-based) again stayed that ruling around Oct 6, promising a decision by Nov 10.

7. **Suffolk County suit (Ross v. State of New York)** — CLEARED WITH QUALIFICATION. Confirmed: Suffolk County Supreme Court, Index No. 628629/2026, filed Sept 28, 2026. Plaintiffs confirmed: Wilbur Ross and Steve Wynn. Could NOT confirm "Hilary Ross" as co-plaintiff or any named co-op entity — dropped from downstream copy unless independently re-verified against the actual complaint. Claims include Privileges and Immunities, Dormant Commerce Clause, Due Process/Equal Protection, and a state constitutional tax-cap theory (NY Const. Art. VIII §10). Pending, no ruling.

8. **CityRealty price-cut report** — ATTRIBUTED CLAIM, single-sourced. Confirmed CityRealty's Oct 6–12, 2026 article reports 250+ NYC condos/co-ops/townhouses cutting prices that week, explicitly linked to the looming tax/exemption-deadline pressure. No independent second-source corroboration of the exact count found — must be attributed to CityRealty by name in copy, not stated as a flat market-wide fact.

9. **Currency check (status as of 2026-10-26)** — MATERIAL FINDING. No ruling on the city's appeal has been issued as of today. The Second Department said (per an Oct 6, 2026 Bloomberg Law/Bloomberg Tax wire story, syndicated widely) it would rule by Nov. 10, 2026 — a date that has not yet arrived. The Ross/Wynn constitutional case also remains pending with no ruling. The city intends to put the surcharge on tax bills due Jan. 1, 2027 regardless of pending appeals.

## Required corrections carried into downstream copy

1. House tax rate corrected to 0.8%–1.3% (not 0.8%–4%).
2. Revenue/count figures attributed by source and date, not merged into one number.
3. Dropped "Hilary Ross" and any named co-op entity from the Suffolk suit.
4. Dropped the unconfirmed bill number "S.9009C."
5. Attributed the 250+ price-cut figure explicitly to CityRealty.
6. Framing must never say the Sept 29 ruling "struck down the tax" — only the city's rollout/notice process was invalidated; the state surcharge itself remains in force under stays.
7. The Nov. 10, 2026 appellate decision deadline foregrounded as the key "where things stand today" fact.

## Missing context flagged

- Two separate lawsuits exist and must not be conflated: the Richmond County/Ozzi suit (process-only challenge) vs. Ross/Wynn (Suffolk County, constitutionality challenge) — the latter is the bigger existential threat to the tax.
- City plans to bill the surcharge Jan. 1, 2027 regardless of litigation outcome.
