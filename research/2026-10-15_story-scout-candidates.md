## CANDIDATE 1 (RECOMMENDED): BTS's two Bogotá stadium shows generated an estimated $99,000 million COP (~$23-25M USD) in local economic activity

**Headline:** Two Nights, $99 Billion Pesos: What BTS's Bogotá Shows Reveal About the Hospitality Math Behind a Touring Act

**What happened:** BTS played two sold-out nights (Oct 2-3, 2026) at Estadio Nemesio Camacho El Campín in Bogotá, drawing more than 100,000 total attendees, about 43% of whom traveled from outside Bogotá (other Colombian cities and abroad). Bogotá's Secretaría de Gobierno and the city's Tourism Observatory (Observatorio de Turismo del IDT) reported the shows generated an estimated $99,000 million COP in economic activity, with the largest shares going to food & beverage (~$39,108M COP) and lodging (~$29,384M COP), and city-wide hotel bookings for that week up 46.6% year-over-year.

**Date of development:** Concerts occurred Oct 2-3, 2026; the Secretaría de Gobierno's post-event economic balance was published Oct 6, 2026; follow-up press coverage ran through Oct 8, 2026.

**Why it's gaining attention now:** It's a live, officially-sourced number (from the Bogotá city government, not a PR firm) published days ago, and it lands right as Bogotá's broader October concert calendar (Los Fabulosos Cadillacs, David Bisbal, Aitana, Iron Maiden, Fonseca, etc.) is separately reported to be driving a 110% jump in hotel bookings and 90% jump in tour packages city-wide (Viajes Falabella data), so there's a live "concert economy" conversation happening in Colombian media right now.

**Potential JustPreneur lesson:** A touring act is infrastructure, not just entertainment — the artist captures the ticket revenue, but hospitality, food service, and transport businesses around the venue captured the majority of the $99B in spending ($68B+ of it went to lodging + F&B combined, more than ticket value itself for most attendees). The takeaway for hospitality/experience entrepreneurs: position your business around anchor events before they happen, because the "halo economy" around a show is often bigger than the show.

**Best format:** Carousel — breaking down the $99,000M COP into its component buckets (F&B, lodging, transport, retail) with a simple visual bar chart, ending on the "lesson" slide about hospitality capturing the majority of event-driven spend.

**Expected audience response:** Strong — recognizable global act (BTS), concrete large number, clear "wait, where did the money actually go" hook, resonates with both NYC/hospitality audience and LATAM audience simultaneously.
