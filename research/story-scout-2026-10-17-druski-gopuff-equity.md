Druski Didn't Just Promote Gopuff. He Became an Owner.

The shift from "paid spokesperson" to "equity partner" — creators with real cultural leverage are increasingly negotiating ownership instead of flat fees. Lesson: once you have proven audience pull, the real upside is in equity/ownership structures, not appearance fees. Useful framing for creators and brands alike on how modern influencer deals are structured.
