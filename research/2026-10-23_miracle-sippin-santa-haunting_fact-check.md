## 6. Missing Context That Could Materially Change Interpretation

- The 2026 "150+ locations" figure is **not a record or a dramatic leap** — 2025 reporting already placed Miracle at "close to 150." Presenting 2026 as unprecedented growth would be misleading.
- The commonly-cited "200+" and other combined figures in year-to-year press coverage often **combine Miracle and Sippin' Santa**; treating them as Miracle-only inflates the apparent scale.
- The "distribution channel" thesis, while plausible and reported, rests on circumstantial/trade evidence (custom glassware reserved for licensees, required ingredient/supply purchases, breakage/replacement costs funding repeat purchases) rather than any disclosed financials or a company admission about revenue mix between licensing fees and glassware sales. No source found discloses what share of Cocktail Kingdom's or Miracle's revenue comes from glassware sold through the pop-up network — this is a real gap and should be acknowledged if the story makes any revenue-mix claim.
- Cocktail Kingdom is described in the Oct. 5, 2026 release itself as a "sister company," not simply "Boehm's other company" — this is a useful, citable primary-source phrase that supports the corporate-relationship claim without overreaching into the unconfirmed "distribution channel" framing.
