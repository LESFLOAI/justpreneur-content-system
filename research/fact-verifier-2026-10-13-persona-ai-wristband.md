## 5. Claims That Must Be Removed or Softened

1. **"Four months later" — must be corrected.** The real interval between the March 2, 2026 acquisition announcement and the October 8, 2026 Persona funding announcement is approximately **seven months**, not four. This is the headline's central numeric claim and it is currently wrong under every reading of the available dates. **This must be fixed before copy is written** — e.g., "Seven months later he raised $10M for what's next," or reframe without a specific month count if the Strategist prefers.
2. **WHOOP/Eight Sleep as "founders" participating personally** — not supported by any source found. If used, name only the companies and do not specify the mechanism of their involvement (personal vs. corporate), or drop this detail.
3. **Any single precise Cal AI revenue figure** (e.g., "$30M" or "$40M" alone) — must be presented as a range with attribution, not a single invented-precision number.
4. **Any claim that Yadegari "dropped out" of Miami or has definitively left** — not confirmed; most recent definitive detail is a reported "gap semester" (TMZ, March 2026).
5. **Any claim of a dollar sale price for Cal AI** — universally undisclosed; do not let revenue figures stand in for a sale price anywhere in copy.
