## 1. Critical factual error (root cause of the Fail)

**Location:** `copy.md` Slide 4 and Final Caption; also repeated verbatim in `design-brief.md` §3 (table) and §11 ("Exact on-screen copy" item 4).

**Copy as written:**
> "CBS reportedly lost around $40M on the run — a figure Jimmy Kimmel has publicly disputed. Allen reportedly still walked away with $15–20M."

> (Caption) "CBS reportedly lost roughly $40M on the run... while Allen reportedly still collected $15–20M."

**What the Approved Facts Block actually says** (fact-verification file, item 4, and "Required Attribution Language" §4):
> "Byron Allen has declined to disclose exactly what he pays CBS for the slot; reported estimates range from $15 million to $20 million annually."

The $15–20M figure in the Approved Facts Block is money **Allen pays CBS** for the airtime (per theGrio's $20M figure and the $15M figure describing CBS's *resulting profit*, per TheWrap/Hollywood Reporter/Variety's "$40M loss → $15M profit" framing). Nowhere in the Approved Facts Block is this a payment **to** Allen. The copy's "walked away with" / "collected" language reverses the direction of the money entirely — it tells readers Allen personally received $15–20M from the arrangement, when the sourced reporting says the opposite: Allen is the one paying CBS that amount for the slot.

**Why this is a Fail, not a minor correction:**
- It is an invented/distorted fact relative to the Approved Facts Block (QC check #1), not merely an unhedged one — the hedge word "reportedly" is present, but it hedges a claim that was never reported in the first place.
- It recreates, inside the *chosen* Angle 1 copy, precisely the risk the Strategy memo identified as Angle 2's specific weakness: "Could read as criticizing CBS or implying Allen 'won' financially at the network's expense... risks a partisan-sounding take on a disputed figure." The Strategist picked Angle 1 partly to *avoid* this trap; the Copywriter's execution reintroduced it anyway.
- It is baked into `design-brief.md`'s verbatim on-screen-copy table, meaning if this package proceeded to `/justpreneur-finish` untouched, the error would be typeset directly into the final slide-4 artwork as exact text, not caught until after real pixels exist.
- Named real individuals + specific disputed dollar figures + reversed payment direction = an avoidable, checkable factual misstatement in a business-news-style post. This is exactly the category of error this review exists to catch.

**Required fix:** Rewrite slide 4 and the caption's second paragraph so the $15–20M figure is correctly attributed as what **Allen pays CBS** for the airtime (optionally alongside the separate, also-hedged $15M CBS-profit figure, if the Copywriter wants to keep both numbers — but they must be kept distinct and correctly directional, not merged into one implied "Allen received $15–20M" claim). Suggested correction direction (for the Copywriter to execute, not for me to finalize):
> "CBS reportedly lost around $40M a year on the old slot — a figure Jimmy Kimmel has publicly disputed. Allen reportedly pays $15–20M annually for the airtime, which CBS says turns that loss into about $15M in profit."
