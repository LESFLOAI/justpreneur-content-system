# Strategy Brief — Movistar Arena Naming Sponsor Exit

**Slot:** 2026-10-15, 10:00 AM
**Recommended angle:** Angle 1 — Counterparty Risk

## Angle 1 — Counterparty Risk (The Sponsor Didn't Fail You, They Left the Country) — RECOMMENDED

1. Thesis: An eight-year naming-rights revenue line can vanish overnight not because of anything the venue did, but because its sponsor's parent company exited an entire national market for reasons completely unrelated to the partnership.
2. Entrepreneurial lesson: Revenue concentration risk isn't just "what if this client stops paying" — it's "what if this partner's business ceases to exist in my market entirely." Map your top revenue relationships and ask what happens if the counterparty disappears for reasons that have nothing to do with you.
3. Opening hook: "Movistar didn't cancel its sponsorship of this arena. Movistar left the country."
4. Format: Carousel, 7 slides (hook, exit timeline, separate sponsorship fact, "never owned the venue" clarifier, operational-continuity proof point, the lesson, CTA).
5. Visual concept: Split-timeline graphic — sponsor's exit track vs. venue's response track, converging rather than one causing the other outright.
6. Risk of misunderstanding: Audience could assume the venue is struggling or closing — must state clearly that 50+ 2026 shows and 80+ 2027 artists are already booked and operations are unaffected.

## Final strategic thesis

An eight-year revenue relationship can evaporate for reasons that have nothing to do with your execution — the venue's resilience came from treating the sponsor's name as a replaceable revenue line sitting on top of infrastructure and relationships it actually controlled.

## Primary takeaway

Audit the partners your revenue depends on — not just whether they might stop paying, but whether their business could stop existing in your market entirely, for reasons fully outside your control.

## Desired emotional response

Calm, clear-eyed respect — "that's smart risk management," not alarm or pity for the venue.

## Desired audience action

Prompt them to identify their own top 1-3 revenue dependencies and privately ask "what happens to me if this partner disappears from my market, not just my business, entirely?" — drive saves/comments naming their own exposure.
